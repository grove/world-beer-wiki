# Diacetyl: formation and removal during fermentation

Diacetyl is a vicinal diketone often associated with buttery aromas. In brewing it can arise when yeast-produced alpha-acetolactate is converted outside the cell and then later reduced by yeast as fermentation matures.[^v14-diacetyl]

## The important pathway

The review connects precursor production with valine metabolism, spontaneous precursor breakdown, and enzymatic reduction. Its central lesson is that a diacetyl measurement at one moment does not describe the later beer without knowing yeast activity and process state.[^v14-diacetyl]
