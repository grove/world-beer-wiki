# Microorganisms and fermentation ecology

Read **species**, **strain**, **maintained culture**, and **fermentation process** as different levels. A detected organism is not automatically a mandatory ingredient, the main source of an aroma, or evidence of safety.

| Reference | Main distinction |
|---|---|
| [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md) | One species contains different brewing lineages and phenotypes. |
| [Lager yeast and hybrid ancestry](lager-yeast-hybrids.md) | Biological ancestry is not a complete account of the invention of lager. |
| [Brettanomyces](brettanomyces.md) | A yeast association does not establish sourness or spontaneous inoculation. |
| [Lactiplantibacillus plantarum](lactiplantibacillus-plantarum.md) | A selected souring product is not a universal species specification. |
| [Pediococcus damnosus](pediococcus-damnosus.md) | Observed acidification in a defined lambic ecosystem. |
| [Acetic acid bacteria](acetic-acid-bacteria.md) | Organism, oxygen context and measured metabolites belong together. |
| [Lachancea and acid-producing yeast](lachancea.md) | Yeast can produce lactic acid as well as alcohol. |
| [Aspergillus oryzae and koji](aspergillus-oryzae-and-koji.md) | The mold, its cultivated substrate and alcoholic yeast are distinct. |
| [Microbial succession](microbial-succession.md) | Fermentation is a changing ecosystem, not simply a species list. |
