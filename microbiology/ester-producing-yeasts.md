# Yeast acetate esters: biochemical flavor production

## Fruity does not mean fruit was used

Acetate esters—including isoamyl acetate—are aroma-active products of yeast metabolism. A 2023 review explains their synthesis via alcohol acetyltransferase and the roles of alcohol precursors and intracellular conditions. This means some recognizable fruity impressions can be fermentation products rather than added bananas, pears or other fruit.[^v13-esters]

## Why recipes do not map directly onto aromas

A broader molecular review describes different classes of fruity and floral compounds from yeast, malt and hops: esters, thiols, terpenoids and more. Ingredient selection, available precursor chemistry and fermentation conditions can all affect the result. One sensory adjective is seldom a unique chemical fingerprint.[^v13-yeast-aromas]
