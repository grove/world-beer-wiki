# Yeast activity during bottle refermentation

Bottle-conditioned beer can continue biological activity after the main fermentation. A technical review discusses additional yeast, fermentation of remaining substrate, changes in aroma, and interaction with oxygen already present in the package.[^v14-bottle]

## Live does not mean one fixed outcome

The paper describes various ways yeast can enter the package and shows why selected culture, viable cells, fermentable substrate and packaging all matter. It also notes pressure risks associated with refermentation: this reference is not a priming or pressure-calculation guide.[^v14-bottle]

## Protection is not immortality

Some yeast activity can reduce oxygen-related damage, but bottled beer still ages through multiple chemical mechanisms. Read [oxidative staling](../sensory/oxidative-aging.md), [packaging](../processes/packaging-and-aging.md), and [beer yeast](../ingredients/brewing-yeast.md).
