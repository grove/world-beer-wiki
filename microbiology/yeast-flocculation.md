# Yeast flocculation and sedimentation

Flocculation is the formation of aggregates of yeast cells, affecting their sedimentation and the recovery or clarity of beer. A brewing review relates the trait to functional FLO genes while also emphasizing the role of environment and cell handling.[^v14-floc]

## Not a species-wide personality

Calcium availability, pH, ethanol, temperature, media viscosity and strain-specific genetic differences influence whether and when cells aggregate. A label such as “English yeast” does not tell the reader a measured flocculation rate for the strain in a particular wort.[^v14-floc]

## Technology and flavor meet

Sedimentation may change how yeast is collected and how much remains available during maturation. Compare [bottle conditioning](../processes/blending-and-bottle-conditioning.md), [cask conditioning](../processes/cask-conditioning.md) and [pure-culture history](../history/hansen-and-industrial-yeast-cultures.md).
