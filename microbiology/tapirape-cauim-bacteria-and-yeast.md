# Tapirapé cauim: bacterial and yeast samples

Three microbiological studies of Tapirapé cauim do not supply one universal species list. The 2007 bacterial study sampled a primarily cassava-based preparation and observed acidification, with lactic acid bacteria becoming prominent. A different 2007 yeast study sampled rice–cassava fermentation in time intervals and identified several yeast taxa, including *Candida tropicalis* and *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*.[^v111-cauim-bacteria][^v111-cauim-yeast]

## A third substrate changes the question

A 2010 study examined peanut- and rice-based cauim with both culture-dependent and PCR-DGGE approaches, identifying bacteria and yeasts and changes across the experiment. 'Detected', 'isolated', and 'predominant in a sample' are not synonyms for an organism carrying out a proven biochemical role. Sampling methods and substrate differences must accompany any comparison.[^v111-cauim-dynamics]

## Do not publish a starter recipe from a taxon table

Several taxa described in sampled preparations have clinical or food-safety implications. Presence in research does not confer safety, probiotic function or a suitable household starter. Nor does the laboratory observation define a centuries-long continuity with Guarani/Tupinambá historical cauim. This entry is a methods comparison that directs readers to the attributed Tapirapé cultural context, not operational advice for culturing unknown microbes.[^v111-cauim-bacteria][^v111-cauim-yeast]
