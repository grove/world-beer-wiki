# Afterword: An Empty Glass {#afterword .unnumbered}

When the drink is gone, something remains. A vessel needs washing. A producer has accounts to settle. A field will have to be planted again. In time, a container may reach a museum while the work that filled it becomes difficult to recover. What survives is seldom a fair sample of what mattered.

One pleasure of these investigations has been to follow the less conspicuous evidence. A qualification in a methods section can change the meaning of a result. A later source can explain how a story became familiar while failing to establish that it happened. A rule can reveal what an authority wanted without telling us how people behaved. Read closely, such details stop looking like obstacles between the reader and the story. They become events within it.

The work also changes the scale of attention. At one moment, the important distinction lies between two forms of a molecule. At another, it lies between the person named in an account and the people who did the work. Neither scale can do the other's job. A chemical explanation of an aroma cannot establish why a household keeps brewing. An account of a household cannot identify an unknown compound. The better questions let different kinds of evidence meet without making them interchangeable.

This is why a tidy verdict is sometimes less useful than a clearer description of the remaining problem. We can know which experiment would discriminate between two explanations. We can identify the document whose date matters, or the comparison that a small study did not make. We can learn that a proposed adaptation worked in one set of soils and still ask what happens elsewhere. An open case need not be an empty one.

Readers can continue the investigation without accepting an invitation to become suspicious of everything. A useful question is more specific than a general doubt. Which batch was measured? When was the account written? Does the label describe a procedure, an ingredient or a promise? Who supplied the name? Sometimes the answer will confirm the ordinary story. That is a result too. The purpose of checking is to find out, not to guarantee a reversal.

And there is still room simply to enjoy a drink, or to decline one. Knowing how an aroma can arise does not require turning every gathering into a demonstration. The understanding remains available when a question becomes interesting. It may make a familiar smell newly noticeable, or lend weight to a name on a museum label that would otherwise have passed unread.

The empty glass is an inadequate archive. It says little about the field, the organism, the carrier or the brewer. Yet it has brought those histories close enough to ask about them. Somewhere beyond it, someone is keeping a culture alive, comparing a result, repairing equipment or deciding what to plant. The next clue may begin as a routine detail in that work.

# Research Note: Following the Sources {#research-note .unnumbered}

The research began with World Beer Wiki at commit `3b985c19a9dcccef8d334be54b5405e42093e812`, edition 1.19. The repository's narratives, evidence metadata, cross-links and Atlas tooling helped identify connected cases. Its articles were treated as a map of claims to investigate, not as independent confirmation of those claims. The book is a separately maintained contribution under `books/the-beer-detective/`; it does not alter the underlying subject articles.

For each chapter, the research followed relevant links to original papers, historical texts, museum records, legal documents or situated accounts. The source register records what was actually accessible on 28 October 2026. “Full text” means the complete work was available and relevant passages were consulted; it does not mean every reference cited by that work was independently read. Abstracts, indexed excerpts, public previews and secondary descriptions are labeled separately. A publisher's citation of a source was never treated as proof that the source itself had been examined.

Evidence was checked at the scale at which it was obtained. A community sample was not expanded into a continent-wide finding. A laboratory mechanism was distinguished from its importance in every commercial beer. An artifact catalogue's identification was separated from a modern inference about the people who used the artifact. A model's conditional result was kept apart from a forecast of what must occur. Dates attached to fieldwork, photography, publication and later commemoration were compared where their differences could mislead a reader.

Conflicts remained visible. The proposed brewing interpretation at Raqefet is presented with objections as reported in the researchers' published response; the original commentary was not fully available. The two Jiahu investigations employed different evidence and do not establish that the same vessels were retested by identical means. Later accounts of Pilsen's condemned beer assign agency differently. The climate chapter does not substitute figures in a repository manuscript for the published article's final abstract. These distinctions change the stories that the evidence can support.

Chapter notes identify sources for the corresponding passages, including originating wiki routes. The separate *Sources and Chapters* document gives a more extensive map, and the machine-readable claim register retains access descriptions, evidence locators and unresolved questions. These records accompany the project package. Selected further reading offers a shorter route through the literature.

The original prose was drafted and revised with AI assistance. Internal passes addressed structure, factual accuracy, sentence-level style, cultural representation and skeptical-reader objections. Additional AI reviews by sessions that had not drafted the relevant chapters produced documented corrections and cuts. These reviews are internal quality work; they are not independent human review, peer review or community consultation.

The most consequential remaining needs are human developmental editing; specialist checks of the archaeological, historical and chemical interpretations; and community-informed readings of the chapters on living traditions. Published fieldwork can convey particular observations without supplying an entire community's judgment about this account. Source-language verification would improve passages reached through translations and English-language summaries. Detailed priorities and questions are listed in the editorial report.

All scenes are either descriptions supported by cited evidence or plainly signaled explanatory examples. No historical dialogue, private thought, interview or field visit has been invented. Short quotations are attributed in the relevant note. The cover's tablet is an original schematic illustration, not a reproduction of a specific object or a transcription of an inscription.

This edition closes its source record on 28 October 2026. It is complete as a book and open as a research project: corrections should identify the chapter, the disputed statement and the evidence that changes it.
