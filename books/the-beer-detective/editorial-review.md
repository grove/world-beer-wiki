## Pass 1: developmental editing

### Findings and decisions

The governing subject is how an explanation earns its authority. The investigations approach it through different objects and activities: a mortar, a hymn, an account, absorbed residues, a closing brewery, an origin story, a volatile molecule, a laboratory reading, a shared building, a trading household, a starter, an ingredient rule, a field study and a crop. The structure supports movement between material evidence and the people who produce, use or interpret it.

The opening invites the reader through a recognizable aroma and leads toward the stone in chapter 1. The concluding movement goes from cooling within a brewery to the agricultural conditions outside it. The last chapter's paired fields resist a simple old-versus-modern crop verdict and end with a real possibility that still requires cultivation. This offers a resolution appropriate to an investigative book without making uncertainty the only available ending.

Several chapters were strengthened materially rather than merely polished:

- Ninkasi became an inquiry into what a poem can preserve and what a reconstruction must supply, instead of a categorical rejection of the word *recipe*.
- The barley-gushing chapter begins with the negative field result, so later purified-protein experiments cannot become an automatic diagnosis of every overflowing bottle.
- The mixed-starter chapter makes the separately added commercial yeast a central discovery. It no longer manufactures an apparent succession puzzle by omitting the intervention.
- Pilsen gained a documented stakeholder, Václav Mirwald, whose tavern and brewing rights make the institutional history concrete.
- The masato chapter replaced generalized caution with actual preparation and sampling limitations, including unconfirmed fermentation duration and the mixing of fermented mash with water.
- The final crop chapter gained Barony Mill, the Trust's restoration purpose and a documented transfer of milling skill. Conservation now includes a particular working institution and named people.

### Repetition addressed

The largest recurring risk was a study-summary → caution → analogy pattern. Some passages explained why an example deserved inclusion after the example had already made its case. The fresh science pass removed or replaced several such pauses: an itinerary recap in the banana chapter, a body-level access disclaimer already carried by the notes, repeated model-versus-bottle explanations, an additional cupboard analogy, and a paragraph defending the inclusion of foam-protein research.

The framing review gave each section a clearer job. The introduction now spends more time on the reader's coming encounters; the research note holds detailed disclosure; the afterword no longer interrupts its conclusion with a second limitations statement. These changes reduce defensiveness without hiding the edition's actual status.

A human developmental editor should still assess the cumulative rhythm in a continuous reading. Evidence distinctions are central to the premise, but repetition can make a sound argument feel slower than its material deserves. The distributed internal passes do not establish how an unbriefed general reader will experience the whole book.

## Pass 2: investigative fact-checking

### Method actually used

The wiki served as a map of questions and references, not as verification. Chapter writers opened original papers, historical texts, institutional collection records, official sources and practitioner accounts where accessible. The claim registers connect consequential statements with wiki routes, exact source URLs, access dates, locators and uncertainty. Notes distinguish full text, selected full-text sections, indexed excerpts, abstracts and researcher descriptions of archives that were not independently inspected.

Part-level fact-checking tested chronology, named places and people, numerical claims, causal language and experimental design. Fresh reviewers reopened selected consequential sources. Neither a link labeled open access nor an indexed result was silently relabeled as a complete article reading. No raw scientific dataset, original tablet, unvisited archive or living community was claimed as personally investigated for the book.

### Important corrections and boundaries retained

| Case | Correction or qualified conclusion |

| Raqefet | The brewing interpretation remains disputed. Eitam's objections were reached through the researchers' published response; the original commentary was not fully read. Early evidence does not establish that brewing caused agriculture. |
| Ninkasi | Civil's full commentary reveals variants and unresolved operations. Recipe-like knowledge and literary form can coexist; an unambiguous modern formula does not follow. |
| Ur III | Individual museum tablets and periods are kept separate. Irregular messenger provisions do not establish universal daily wages; barley equivalents need not be ingredients. |
| Jiahu | The 2026 work is complementary, not a chemical retest of the original 2004 samples. Later liquids in bronze vessels are separate from Neolithic pottery residues. |
| Cerro Baúl | Capacity is reconstructed; ceremonial closure and elite women's involvement are contextual interpretations. The dregs study's Moquegua scope is not made pan-Andean. |
| Porter | The late Harwood narrative is examined through historical source transmission. Reading a modern researcher is not presented as inspecting the original 1802 page. |
| Aroma and bitterness | Ingredient provenance, molecular detection, sensory influence and preference are different results. Equal IBU does not guarantee equal experience; the analytical method still has a valid purpose. |
| Light and aging | Direct UV photolysis differs from flavin-mediated visible-light chemistry. The particular cysteinylated-aldehyde candidates did not explain the rising staling aldehydes in the cited experiment. |
| Gushing | A non-significant field association does not negate a possible purified-protein mechanism. DLS particle signals are distinct from the proposed nanobubble identity; foam does not measure toxin concentration. |
| Lager ancestry | Modern Irish isolates establish observed European occurrence, not the timing of arrival or an Irish medieval origin. The proposed Munich encounter remains a historical hypothesis. |
| Zoigl and livelihoods | Communal facilities do not imply unrestricted membership or equality. Wort production and later fermentation are located correctly. Wa studies are not silently treated as independent representative samples. |
| Chicherías and starters | Sign colors belong to particular places. Cultured isolates are not complete microbiome inventories. Added commercial yeast remains part of the makgeolli experiment. |
| Purity law and Pilsen | Prescribed rules, practical yeast use, later legal stories, institutional decisions and anniversary dates are distinguished. A source's erroneous claim that yeast was unused before 1906 is excluded. |
| Masato | Exact water/masato sample groups, indicator limitations and unknown timings prevent a safety guarantee or a causal treatment estimate. Source claims of instant pathogen killing are not adopted. |
| Color perception | Both beers were color-adjusted from a common base. Failure to demonstrate discrimination is not proof of identity, and nonsignificant post-tasting differences are not proof of exactly zero effect. |
| Energy and crops | Modeled heat recovery is not measured plant performance. Observations differ from conditional projections. Bere's manganese advantage is not universal superiority or general immunity to climate stress. |

Version differences were treated as evidence problems. The climate chapter's linked repository manuscript differs from the final published abstract; the more dramatic numerical ranges were not substituted into the narrative. Online publication dates, journal issue dates and later publisher-platform migration dates were separated where they could change chronology.

## Pass 3: prose and literary editing

The writers and fresh readers checked complete chapter prose for unexplained terms, invented scene details, dialogue, private motives, excessive abstraction and repeated endings. No first-person fieldwork, laboratory participation or interview was fabricated. Humor and analogy remain original explanatory devices rather than substitute evidence.

Direct line-level changes include correcting the claim that *fruity* is a one-syllable word; removing a glib sentence about the goddess signing a document; replacing the Jiahu mold joke with a materials-versus-transformation contrast; giving hydrophobins an intelligible description of their water-attracting and water-repelling regions; replacing unexplained heat-integration terminology with the operation it describes; and changing an “impossible” lifecycle verdict to an unsupported comparison that would require data.

Exact details were added when they improved narrative: the common-beer-batch extract assay in the field experiment, Mirwald's tavern, the masato preparation step, the actual attributes expected in the dark-beer experiment, and the named milling relationship in Orkney. Generic reminders were cut where the same distinction was already clear or properly documented in an endnote.

The prose is deliberately accessible without removing technical distinctions. First-use explanations cover such terms as ester, radical, nucleation, bound aldehyde, hybrid, wort and saccharification. Original source language is not reproduced in long passages. The few necessary technical and historical terms do not stand in for local knowledge or a complete process description.

## Pass 4: cultural and ethical review

The internal review checked whose work is visible and whether a study's location has been inflated into a continent-wide tradition. Wari and Inca are distinguished. Local names and practices remain attached to their reported settings. Tonto is not made interchangeable with wheat beer because both can smell fruity. Molle is not reduced to an exotic flavoring for presumed barley beer. Present-day Andean brewers are skilled collaborators, not unchanged exhibits of an ancient past.

The livelihood chapters keep water, time, supply and capital visible. They do not present women's persistence as a remedy for inadequate infrastructure or make every woman a universal keeper of tradition. Communal brewing remains a specific institutional arrangement whose rules and exclusions need investigation. Heritage recognition is not evidence of unanimous agreement or universal access; national listings are distinguished from international UNESCO inscription.

The Shawi study's participatory methods remain part of the evidence. Its original community validation is explicitly different from review of this book. Safety questions do not erase the drink's cultural significance, and cultural significance does not establish a hazard-free product. No medicinal plant dose, ingestion recommendation, brewing safety threshold or current legal prescription is supplied.

Bere is treated as varied living material. Orkney, Shetland and the Outer Hebrides retain different cultivation situations. The chapter credits both research and the continued growing and processing that left material available for investigation. Reading an institution's public account does not become consultation or endorsement.

These are internal representation checks. Community-informed and source-language review is still required for a publication claiming that stronger level of assurance.

## Pass 5: hostile-reader review

The reviewers tested attractive but unsupported conclusions: beer caused farming; a hymn is either a complete formula or contains no technical knowledge; a ration table is a brew sheet; equal measurement means equal sensation; a positive laboratory mechanism diagnoses a field problem; a modern isolate proves an ancient route; a shared brewery proves equality; an absence in a culture-dependent survey proves biological absence; a legal ingredient list defines every beer; and a small indicator study certifies safety.

The most productive objections changed the text. Jiahu lost the identical-sample implication. The starter investigation gained its omitted yeast addition. The masato chapter gained actual missing timing information. The color chapter now states statistical findings without converting a null result into proof of no effect. The lager chapter no longer uses modern Irish occurrence to settle historical arrival. The final chapter keeps a failed Bere accession and the more favorable Dundee result alongside the striking Orkney success.

The strongest remaining literary objection is cumulative repetition of evidence lessons. Targeted cuts and concrete additions improve this, but an independent general reader has not yet tested enjoyment, momentum or the desire to continue. The report does not invent that reader's approval.

## Unresolved access and specialist questions

Source limits remain where notes say they remain. Particularly consequential gaps include complete access to the Raqefet debate, some Jiahu methods and supplementary material, the Wari dregs paper, selected historical originals, several sensory and industrial abstracts, original interview transcripts, and the full contemporary heat-integration paper. These gaps constrain claims; internal review has not removed them by relabeling access.

Concrete questions for future human review are:

1. **Developmental editor and general reader:** Where does the investigation lose momentum? Which evidence distinctions are necessary on the page, and which have already been learned elsewhere? Does the ending earn its restrained hope?
2. **Archaeobotanist:** Does the current status of the Raqefet debate warrant the chapter's degree of confidence? Do the Jiahu microfossils, identifications and sample relationships remain accurately described after full methods and supplementary material are considered?
3. **Assyriologist:** Are bappir, product distinctions, barley equivalents and the limits of the selected translations represented fairly? Have any modern brewing assumptions entered an apparently neutral explanation?
4. **Andean archaeologist and appropriate community-informed readers:** Are the Wari closure, women's work, hospitality and contemporary analogies properly scoped? Are local terms and the relations between makers, researchers and institutions handled respectfully?
5. **Historians of brewing and regulation:** Does the porter source genealogy, medieval women's documentary visibility, Bavarian rule and Pilsen foundation fairly balance what is known with what remains indirect or disputed?
6. **Brewing chemist, sensory scientist and microbial geneticist:** Do the model-to-product inferences, negative results, null comparisons and ancestry hypotheses carry the right weight? Has newer literature materially altered any mechanism or historical reconstruction?
7. **Food microbiologist and Shawi-informed reviewer:** Are the field study's indicator results, preparation details and community priorities presented together without implying a treatment guarantee or dismissing local knowledge?
8. **Practitioners and source-language readers for the living traditions:** Are terms, locality, economic constraints and divisions of labor accurate in the places described? Does the text grant one small study more cultural reach than it has?
9. **Thermal engineer, agronomist and crop maintainers:** Are modeled heat recovery, historical cooling, cultivar-specific responses, conditional projections and seed-maintenance work connected without promising a universal engineering or agricultural solution?

These are review questions, not a claim that anyone has been contacted or has agreed to review. No review request, testimonial or endorsement has been fabricated.

## Copyediting and production boundary

Part-level checks matched chapter-specific note calls and definitions, parsed claim-register JSON and verified cited wiki paths in the checkout. Cross-edits preserved those connections and added source records where new factual material entered. These mechanical checks verify structure, not historical or scientific truth. The integrating editor owns final assembled-manuscript checks, counts, navigation, rendering, EPUB/PDF validation and the production manifest.

## Final editorial assessment

The manuscript contains complete investigations, original prose, distinct material openings and a traceable research apparatus. The most consequential editorial gains came from recovering an omitted intervention, specifying a limited measurement, returning to a fuller source and giving abstract arguments named people or concrete operations. Those gains support a coherent book rather than a conversion of wiki entries into paragraphs.

The edition should be described as internally edited and awaiting independent review. Its remaining limitations are substantive and visible. Human judgment about specialist accuracy, cultural representation and sustained reading pleasure remains an unfinished stage, not an honor already earned through the number of internal passes.
