# Cover direction

Three original compositions were constructed as editable SVG, with no generated
imagery, stock illustrations, photographic reconstruction or third-party art.

**A — The evidence (selected):** Vermilion-orange field, cream and dark blue
typography, and a small schematic clay tablet. The title takes most of the
space; the object gives the reader a question. Its marks are a graphic
abstraction, not a transcription or drawing of a specific artifact.

**B — Look closer:** Cobalt, pale yellow and coral, with a magnifying glass
containing a budding-cell motif. It makes the investigative premise immediate,
but gives disproportionate emphasis to microbiology.

**C — The curious vessel:** Forest green, soft pink and cream. A deliberately
simple vessel holds the curve of a question mark. This has a warmer food-book
character, but the title/object relationship is less distinctive at small size.

Concept A connects ancient material evidence with a contemporary typographic
voice. Bricolage Grotesque supplies weight and irregularity without faux aging.
EB Garamond is used inside the book. Both fonts are distributed under the SIL
Open Font License; their original license texts and sources accompany the design.

The final front cover is provided as editable SVG, a 1800 × 2700 pixel PNG
(300 pixels per inch at 6 × 9 inches), and vector PDF. A full paperback wrap
would require a printer's paper stock, spine width and bleed template; this
package supplies a front cover, not a fabricated production specification.

To edit text, install the fonts in `fonts/`. To rebuild exports, run
`python tools/design_cover.py` from the book directory. Inkscape, Pillow and
fontTools are required. Private build fonts and caches remain in `.build/`.

Font sources: [EB Garamond](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ebgaramond)
and [Bricolage Grotesque](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bricolagegrotesque).
