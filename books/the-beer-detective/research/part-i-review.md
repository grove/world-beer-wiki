# Part I internal review record

Reviewed and revised 28 October 2026. This is the drafting agent's internal AI self-review. It is not independent human peer review, a new laboratory analysis, an independent translation, a site visit, or consultation with the communities discussed.

This record describes the drafting handoff. The subsequent [fresh cross-part review](cross-review-part-i.md) records direct prose edits, an additional source for the explanation of *qu*, and updated counts; it supersedes the corresponding handoff descriptions and numbers below.

## Developmental pass

Read all six completed narratives and checked that each has its own investigative question, material entrance, and conclusion. The sequence moves through stone, a hymn, accounts, pottery residues, a brewery's destruction, and a changing industrial product. Chapter 6's engine is a documented counterpoint to the sole-inventor story, not a proposed origin of porter. The monthly-stock calculation is explicitly hypothetical and supports the storage argument without pretending to be an eighteenth-century balance sheet.

Kept method explanations connected to the object being investigated. The main editorial risk remains the cumulative amount of interpretive discussion across six evidence-focused chapters. Some explanatory analogies were retained because they distinguish specific errors: barley equivalents from ingredients; a possible reconstruction from a recording; copied reports from independent testimony. A fresh cross-part reader may still decide to cut repeated caution at the whole-book stage.

## Factual and source-access pass

Checked consequential dates, named people, numbers, objects, and causal statements against the retrieved sources rather than relying exclusively on wiki summaries. Endnotes and the 39-claim register describe the actual access level. Concrete corrections and decisions were:

- **Raqefet:** Added the published 2019 response to criticism; marked that Eitam's complete original commentary was not read. The brewing interpretation is conditional. Cultivation, domestication, chronology, and causation are kept separate. The floral-grave evidence supplies burial context and is not counted as independent brewing proof.
- **Ninkasi:** Downloaded Civil's complete chapter and read the relevant textual commentary. Replaced a categorical poem-versus-recipe framing with the narrower issue of an unambiguous reproducible formula. Identified the English translation as the access layer and preserved uncertainty around bappir, sequence, and surviving copies. Removed a glib sentence about a goddess signing a document.
- **Ur III:** Distinguished Met 1985.180.2, Maxwell MMA 67.134.3, Valdosta/CDLI P273413, and Met MMA 57.16.6. Dates and accession numbers belong to particular objects. Kept irregular messenger provision separate from universal wages; kept accounting equivalents separate from physical ingredient quantities. Did not import the CDLI note's speculative pasteurization gloss or the British Museum page's generalized beer-safer-than-water assertion.
- **Jiahu:** Explicitly corrected the temptation to describe the studies as a chemical retest of the same sherds. The 2026 paper identifies its contribution as complementary. Preserved uncertainty around the 2004 fruit/wax interpretations and absence of surviving ethanol. Kept the later bronze liquids separate. Used Mijiaya and Qiaotou for methodological comparison without inventing ancestry or transmission.
- **Cerro Baúl:** Kept Wari distinct from Inca and described planned closure as an archaeological interpretation. Capacity is an estimate, and the approximate water mass is arithmetic rather than an excavation measurement. Women and rank are inferred through contextual evidence, not individually identified. The 2024 abstract supports a limited Moquegua claim, not a pan-Andean absence of household brewing. Obtained and inspected a complete open copy of the 2019 archaeometric article after the publisher page was rate-limited; its exact compound-assignment limitations reinforce the chapter's qualified language. Updated the endnote and register to reflect that access upgrade.
- **Porter:** Read Sumner's complete article and Ellis's primary manual rather than citing only an abstract. The 1802 magazine/guidebook texts themselves were not inspected, and the note says so. The familiar Harwood narrative is presented as a late claim, not performed as a factual scene. The engine's original installation is kept separate from later alterations; the correction of horsepower claims is attributed to curatorial research, not a measurement made for this book.

## Prose and cultural pass

Read the complete prose for invented scene-setting, unearned certainty, condescension, repetitive openings, and a European definition of beer silently controlling the account. No dialogue, witnessed feast, personal memories, journeys, interviews, or sensory encounters were invented.

Changed the Jiahu opening to contrast materials with transformation, removing a joke that implied mold naturally makes a beverage objectionable. Kept molle central to its own process instead of describing it as a foreign flavoring for presumed barley beer. Distinguished contemporary Andean brewing expertise from a supposedly unchanged archive of the ancient past. Preserved the ability of people without domesticated cereals or modern microbiology to possess complex practical knowledge. Removed unneeded exact update dates from two CDLI collection-note citations; those dates were immaterial to the historical claims and risked distracting from the objects' dates.

The remaining prose uses explanatory analogies and some humor, but those devices do not replace evidence. The Shubayqa comparison does not declare a winner in a beer-versus-bread contest. The modern inspired beers are identified as modern projects. Research participants are not described as timeless representatives of an ancient culture.

## Hostile-reader pass

Tried each chapter against the stronger claim an enthusiastic reader might mistakenly extract:

| Tempting inference | What the revised chapter actually warrants |
| --- | --- |
| Beer caused agriculture. | Raqefet offers a disputed early-brewing interpretation; causation would require a much longer chain of evidence. |
| The hymn either is a complete recipe or tells us nothing technical. | Literary and practical knowledge can overlap while important operating choices remain unresolved. |
| Every worker received a daily beer salary, and the numbers give a recipe. | Particular accounts concern particular allocations; valuation and production are different relationships. |
| New scientists disproved the old Jiahu ingredient list by testing the same pot. | Different methods and samples address complementary aspects of a shared site-level problem. |
| Beer held Wari together, and all Wari households were forbidden to brew. | Particular institutions and sampled settings support limited interpretations about hospitality and specialized production. |
| A late inventor story proves that no individual ever contributed anything. | The specific heroic narrative lacks the necessary contemporary support; individual agency remains part of distributed technical change. |
