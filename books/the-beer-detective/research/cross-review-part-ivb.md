# Fresh internal review of chapters 23–24

Completed 28 October 2026 by the Part I drafting agent after the chapters' drafter confirmed that the prose was stable and relinquished editing ownership. Both chapters and their endnotes were read in full. This is a fresh internal AI reading, not human engineering, agronomic, historical, community or editorial review.

## Closing-arc assessment

The chapters provide a coherent final movement. “The Borrowed Winter” begins with harvested ice and follows the acquisition, transfer and reuse of heat. Its final paragraph moves from control inside a building to the conditions in which ingredients grow. “The Field Ahead” begins with paired crop trials and returns to seed, variation and the people who maintain it. These are distinct investigations that connect through a concrete dependency. The final seed-and-field image was retained.

The climate chapter's strongest decision is to place the Burray result beside Dundee rather than using either as a universal verdict on old or modern barley. Its treatment of projections, irrigation, crop economics and conservation preserves the difference between a risk, a candidate response and a guaranteed outcome. The refrigeration chapter likewise does not turn an engineering model into measured factory performance.

No historical ice source or contemporary technology receives an unsupported environmental certificate.

The fresh review supports the closing arc and corrects specific language; it does not substitute for a human reader's assessment of pacing or specialist and community review.
