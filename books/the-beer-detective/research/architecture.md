The narrative travels from surviving traces, through physical mechanisms, to
living institutions and the reader's own assumptions. Each chapter is selected
for a distinct intellectual payoff; no chapter is a style catalogue.

## Selection decisions

Bottle color as an age test is handled briefly in the light chapter rather than
repeated as a separate investigation. Clove aroma is a short contrast in the
banana chapter. Alcohol-label definitions were set aside: a regulatory survey
would interrupt the narrative and rapidly become dated. Packaging comparisons
and numerical sustainability rankings are excluded unless functional units
and system boundaries can be matched. No unseen interviews or field visits
will be staged in the prose.

The living-traditions section gives Africa and Asia substantial chapters;
the Americas appear in archaeology and present-day community research.
Country counts are not a substitute for cultural depth. Specific community
review needs remain open and are identified honestly.

## Evidence and review workflow

Read the wiki metadata and its originating evidence, then independently open
the original source or a legitimate open version. Record full text, abstract,
excerpt and blocked access separately. Inspect consequential counterevidence.
Use chapter-numbered endnotes and a separate claim register. Do not turn a
wiki source-access claim into a claim that this project read that source.

Each part receives developmental, factual, prose, cultural and skeptical-reader
passes. The complete manuscript receives an additional cross-part edit.
AI reviews are internal reviews, even when conducted in separate agent sessions.
No independent human review is implied.
