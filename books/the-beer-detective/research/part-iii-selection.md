## Narrative variation

The six investigations use different forms of movement. Chapter 13 follows a representation across archives and centuries. Chapter 14 follows a production route through property and institutions. Chapter 15 follows a missing input through the economics of a working week. Chapter 16 follows a sign from household exchange to archaeological and heritage interpretation. Chapter 17 resolves a scientific contradiction through experimental methods. Chapter 18 follows operations and their timing, opening with an ordinary thermal decision.

Signs recur deliberately in 13, 14 and 16, but do different work: refusal of sale, rotation of service, and geographically specific invitation. The repeated object is a connective device, not a claim that these practices descend from one another. Likewise, household production crosses several chapters without making the households economically identical.

## Source hierarchy and actual access

The wiki is a map and a source of questions. Each chapter cites its wiki route and original sources. Full original text was sought and obtained when available, but access labels never promote excerpts to a complete reading. Relevant gains beyond the wiki include the complete original *Goesaert* opinion, detailed Zoigl practitioner accounts, the 2023 Wa methods, the Piura resolution, the makgeolli preparation methods, Choo's original 2016 yeast paper, and the complete ten-page mbege article through an open-access PDF copy.

Bennett's full book, Hayashida's full paper, the water-scarcity paper's direct full text, Lobnibe's full paper, the 2024 Wa paper, Gimbi–Kitabatake's full article, Hemp's full paper and Myhre's full paper were not accessed. Their use is correspondingly narrow. The dolo article is a review. The wiki-linked 2023 FEMS genomic article is also a review; the manuscript uses the original 2016 experimental paper instead for the particular yeast example.

The machine-readable companion provides forty claim records with source URL, access description, consultation date, relevant locator, wiki path and uncertainty. A reference to a source does not imply agreement with every sentence it contains: speculative health claims, inflated cultural generalizations and unreliable numerical inferences have been excluded.

## Boundaries and further reporting

The work contains no interviews conducted for this book, no reconstructed dialogue, no original visits and no human cultural consultation. Directly reported speech is paraphrased and attributed to the publishing researcher. Hypotheticals are marked as such. The brief quotation from the historical court opinion concerns its language, not a living participant's unrecorded voice.

Further reporting could usefully ask brewers about labor allocation, recognition of readiness, meaningful product variation, access rights and preferred improvements. Such work would need actual participant agreement and appropriate language expertise. These questions are recorded as future work; no invitation, approval, consultation or endorsement has been invented.
