# The Beer Detective

*Uncovering the Myths, Mysteries and Untold Stories of Beer*

A free narrative nonfiction book developed from World Beer Wiki's research
routes, with independent source checking and original prose. The project lives
apart from the wiki's reference articles and has its own production checks.

## Edition

Research baseline: [`3b985c19a9dcccef8d334be54b5405e42093e812`](https://github.com/grove/world-beer-wiki/commit/3b985c19a9dcccef8d334be54b5405e42093e812), edition 1.19.

Research and production began 28 October 2026. This is an AI-assisted review
edition. Independent human editorial, specialist and community review has not
occurred. Specific limitations will be recorded in the manuscript's notes and
editorial review, rather than presented as endorsements.

## Planned publication

Twenty-four substantial investigations in four parts; approximately 35,000–50,000
narrative words. Deliverables include a single Markdown master, reflowable EPUB,
6 × 9 inch PDF, three vector cover concepts, a final editable cover and raster
export, source register, editorial report, publishing copy and reproducible
build tools.

See [the editorial architecture](research/architecture.md) for selection and
scope. Chapters are being researched and drafted in parallel, then assembled
and reviewed across the whole book. Git checkpoints preserve the working stages.
