# Belgian beer heritage inscription: a dated institutional milestone

## What happened in 2016

UNESCO's Representative List added **Beer culture in Belgium** in 2016. This is an administrative decision about recognized practices and their transmission. It is not the invention date of Belgian beer, and not evidence that any beer style was standardized that year.[^v13-belgium-unesco]

## A different sort of calendar entry

For a date-aware encyclopedia the event belongs to a historical-institutional timeline, not the annually recurring festivals list. We record the **year** from the official decision, but avoid inventing an exact inscription day: the public listing specifies the session and year, while the present source record is not used to assert a particular day's vote.[^v13-belgium-unesco]

## What the event recognizes

Read [Beer culture as heritage](../culture/belgian-beer-as-living-heritage.md), [Trappist institutions](../culture/trappist-work-and-brewing.md) and [Belgian ales](../categories/belgian-and-farmhouse-ales.md). An institution's award is a documented event; the cultural practices it describes have their own histories.

## Evidence boundaries

Because an exact start day was not established for the particular decision here, the article records a historical year without misclassifying the event as a season or fabricating a day-specific edition.
