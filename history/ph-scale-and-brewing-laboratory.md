# How the pH concept emerged from brewing science

The pH concept is now basic scientific vocabulary, but the Carlsberg Foundation connects its introduction to S. P. L. Sørensen’s work at the laboratory. Brewery questions about chemistry helped stimulate methods with applications far beyond beer.[^v14-lab]

## Measuring rather than guessing

According to the foundation, Sørensen developed the pH scale while studying acidity and biochemical reactions. In brewing, acidity interacts with fermentation and mashing, yet a measured pH is not itself a recipe or an indicator of a beer’s cultural origin.[^v14-lab]
