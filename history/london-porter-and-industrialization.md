# London porter and the business of defining a beer

## An origin tale can serve an industry

James Sumner's 2008 historical paper challenges the familiar telling of London porter as a single discrete invention that immediately created giant breweries. His argument instead emphasizes how product definitions and large-scale industrial brewing emerged together across an extended period. The abstract explicitly calls the invention narrative a retrospective construction.[^v13-porter-sumner]

## The identity of porter also changed

Martyn Cornell's historical account discusses porter’s rise, decline and contemporary revival. Modern porter categories should not be mapped mechanically onto an eighteenth-century product: malt technologies, industrial conditions, naming and consumer expectations changed. Porter is both a sensory family and a case study in how a market creates historical identity.[^v13-porter-camra]
