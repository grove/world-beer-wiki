# The Hymn to Ninkasi as literary beer evidence

The Sumerian Hymn to Ninkasi evokes brewing materials, work and a divine figure associated with beer. The Oxford Electronic Text Corpus preserves a scholarly translation with gaps; reading the poem as a precise present-day recipe would erase its literary character.[^v14-ninkasi]

## What is in the surviving words

The translated lines mention beerbread, grains, water and processes described through poetic images. Repetition and unresolved passages matter: translators are not looking at an industrial recipe card, and no particular modern attenuation, yeast strain or alcohol concentration follows from the text.[^v14-ninkasi]

## Multiple sources make history

Tate Paulette’s book description emphasizes archaeological remains, iconography and written documents as complementary evidence for ancient Mesopotamian brewing. A poem establishes a cultural representation; a vessel may document a material practice; neither alone proves the flavor of an ancient serving.[^v14-ninkasi-book]
