# Emil Christian Hansen and pure-culture brewing in 1883

In 1883 Emil Christian Hansen at the Carlsberg laboratory established a method for cultivating brewing yeast as a pure culture. The foundation’s own historical timeline says the method was shared rather than patented.[^v14-hansen]

## What changed

A Carlsberg biography describes first production-scale use in November 1883. Selecting and maintaining a chosen yeast lineage made repeatability easier at industrial scale, but it did not make older mixed cultures inherently illegitimate or prove that beer before 1883 lacked skill.[^v14-hansen2]
