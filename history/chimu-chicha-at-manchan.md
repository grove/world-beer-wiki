# Chimu-era chicha production at Manchan

The archaeological interpretation of chicha at Manchan places brewing in a non-elite residential area of a Chimu regional center (approximately 900–1470 CE). This is more specific than saying maize beer was simply “ancient Andean.”[^v14-chimu]

## Production and politics

Jerry Moore’s abstract connects the production setting with social obligations in which leaders provided chicha and food in exchange for labor. Archaeological production contexts can make political economy visible without providing exact brewing instructions.[^v14-chimu]

## Do not confuse continuity and identity

The [modern Chicha de Jora](../styles/chicha-de-jora.md) profile offers a comparative path, as does [reciprocal labor](../culture/chicha-and-labor-reciprocity.md). That comparison does not prove every modern chichería follows a Chimu recipe or inheritance line.
