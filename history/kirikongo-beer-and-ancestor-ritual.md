# Kirikongo: archaeological interpretation of beer and ancestors

## Why a pot can be social evidence

An archaeological study of Kirikongo in Burkina Faso examines ceramic assemblages and depositional contexts to investigate possible beer use in ancestral and household rituals. The authors interpret material traces in relation to social practices; their paper does not report an excavated step-by-step recipe or direct testimony from historical participants.[^v13-kirikongo]

## Context and chronology

Archaeological material describes a long occupation record and interactions between domestic space and ritual placement. The paper's public abstract supports a specific interpretive case, not a universal rule about beer and African ancestor veneration, nor continuity of a particular present-day custom.[^v13-kirikongo]
