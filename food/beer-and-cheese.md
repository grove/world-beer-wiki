# Beer and cheese: variable sensory matches

Beer with cheese can be enjoyable without being predictable. A 2015 consumer study examined liking for combinations of bottom-fermented red beers and different cheeses and found striking variation among individuals and combinations.[^v14-cheese]

## Food changes the experience

The authors’ abstract reports that cheese altered perceived beer characteristics during consumption; a person’s ratings of individual items did not completely determine their favorite pairing. This makes pairing an interaction, not a lookup table from style name to cheese type.[^v14-cheese]

## How to use the result

Compare the [expert interview framework](beer-food-pairing-as-a-hypothesis.md) with [alcohol-free pairing evidence](alcohol-free-beer-with-food.md). The sampled beers and cheeses cannot justify rules for all sour, farmhouse or traditional beverages.[^v14-food]
