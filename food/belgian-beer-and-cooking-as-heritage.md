# Beer in Belgian cuisine: heritage evidence versus tasting rules

Food can enter beer culture in more ways than a list of “correct pairings.” UNESCO's 2016 description of Belgian beer heritage includes cooking with beer, beer-washed cheese and the place of serving and appreciating beer in social life. These are statements about practices within a living cultural setting, not experimental rankings of what every diner will prefer.[^v114-unesco]

In this setting, expertise may be transmitted by families, professional brewers, teachers, restaurants or institutions. The association of food and beer can carry regional meaning, celebrate an occasion or reflect an individual taste. Its significance is therefore cultural even when the exact sensory mechanisms are not measured.[^v114-unesco]

A washed-rind cheese can raise several different research questions: Was beer used as part of production? Is the food served with beer? Are the two products made in the same area? Did a controlled sensory study actually assess that combination? UNESCO's summary documents cultural examples, but it cannot answer all four questions for an arbitrary cheese and beer.[^v114-unesco]
