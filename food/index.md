# Beer with food and at the table

Beer preferences can change with meal context. These pages compare study designs and observed preferences rather than prescribe universal pairings. Alcohol-free beer is included explicitly.

- [Can alcohol-free beer work with food?](alcohol-free-beer-with-food.md) — 2026 comparative tasting of three beer styles and a single Swedish shrimp dish.
- [Beer and cheese: variable sensory matches](beer-and-cheese.md) — A 2015 study of consumers tasting bottom-fermented red beers and cheeses.
