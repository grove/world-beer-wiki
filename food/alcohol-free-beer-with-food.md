# Can alcohol-free beer work with food?

A 2026 consumer study compared alcoholic and non-alcoholic versions of a pilsner, wheat beer and IPA, both alone and with *skagenröra*, a Swedish shrimp dish. In this experiment the non-alcoholic versions were less liked alone but did not perform significantly differently in the tested pairing.[^v14-food-na]

## The contextual finding

The result reminds researchers that beverage preference can change when food is present. It does not imply that every alcohol-free beer works equally well with every dish, nor that a food pairing eliminates the health risks of alcohol in the alcoholic comparison products.[^v14-food-na]
