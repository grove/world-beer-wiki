# Research edition 1.10 — culture, process and evidence

**Edition 1.10 · 2026-10-28 · 27 new subject articles and 15 source-backed revisions.** This release begins from merged PR #10 (1.9); previous edition validation and research reports remain unchanged as historical records.

## What is new

| Research axis | Added evidence and important boundary |
|---|---|
| Amazon and Andes | Yaminahua masateadas as situated gatherings, Cusco chicherías and maize work, north-coast production-scale research, and the Wari Moquegua state-specialization hypothesis. |
| Indonesian and Thai starch ferments | Tapai ketan, ragi tapai, Amylomyces sampling, an early tapé laboratory model, and khao mak as a related ferment; no forced 'beer' label. |
| Non-barley brewing | Buckwheat, quinoa and amaranth processing limits, 40% adjunct experiment correctly labeled as still containing barley, and starch-conversion approaches. |
| Non-alcoholic beer | Biological and physical routes, wort aroma, selected yeast screening, experimentally inoculated challenge samples, resource-boundary questions. |
| Packaging and sensory science | Riboflavin, iso-alpha acids and light-struck photochemistry integrated with material culture and an evidence-based narrative. |

## Revisions rather than duplicates

Fifteen original articles received new source-cited sections, including the existing lightstruck chemistry, maltose-negative yeast and limited fermentation pages. Rather than create near-duplicate articles, these canonical pages retain their URLs and now link to deeper materials. The additions are indexed across the same thirteen subject layers.

## Research and editorial limits

The source register contains 19 unique papers and reviews, many re-used across new and revised articles. Consultation was often confined to a publisher abstract or indexed excerpt. Laboratory samples, community practices, archaeological theories and sensory interpretations are separate evidence forms; none are used as universal recipes, dates, product-label guarantees or commercial safety standards. Independent community and subject-specialist review has not occurred. No media is reproduced from external publishers.

## Maintenance

The finite specification adds ATLAS-067 through ATLAS-072, and the source-quality checker now supports edition 1.10 while retaining its earlier-edition checks. A new source register and seven question-led trails help both human and LLM readers trace the evidence.
