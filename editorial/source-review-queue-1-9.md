# Edition 1.9: independent-review and access queue

**Open work, not completed review.** New cited material may need independent full-text, translation, legal-history and community verification. Source hygiene is not historical correctness.

| Priority | Evidence boundary | Specific next step |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [S’Tieng rượu cần](../culture/stieng-ruou-can-and-transmission.md) | Invite willing S’Tieng practitioners or a regional scholar to check names, community distinctions and consent for any future ritual description; never publish restricted ingredient knowledge. |
| P0 | [Ma rượu cần](../culture/ma-ruou-can-in-lam-dong.md) | Seek Ma-authored or locally reviewed testimony beyond a tourism promotional description; validate event associations without assigning fixed dates. |
| P0 | [Bhutanese ara](../culture/bhutan-ara-and-social-ritual.md) | Obtain full 2024 Royal Thimphu College article and Bhutanese practitioner review; the indexed abstract is insufficient to describe detailed rites or participation. |
| P0 | [Sumerian terms](../history/sumerian-beer-terms-and-uncertainty.md) | Compare Damerow’s linguistic arguments with later specialist scholarship, exact tablet transliterations and critical translations; do not publish modern “authentic recipes.” |
| P1 | [1830 Beerhouse Act](../history/beerhouse-act-1830-debates.md) | Inspect the enacted original PDF and subsequent law texts before asserting exact operative sections or later social consequences. Hansard speakers’ economic or health claims are not outcome data. |
| P1 | [Munich 1812 decree](../history/munich-beer-garden-1812.md) | Obtain a digitized Bavarian primary decree and compare municipal narratives against period wording; keep current food custom distinct from historic law. |
| P1 | [Nuruk microbiology](../microbiology/nuruk-42-sample-survey.md) | Consult the full 42-sample paper and a Korean researcher; track isolate methods, regional sampling, potential safety findings and test conditions. |
| P1 | [Huangjiu comparisons](../microbiology/huangjiu-manual-and-mechanized-microbes.md) | Obtain full 2021 and 2026 study methods, compare actual batch designs, and have a Chinese fermentation specialist check translation and causal claims. |
| P1 | [Qasr Ibrim barley](../history/qasr-ibrim-barley-ancient-dna.md) | Revisit original sampling contexts with an archaeobotanist; ancient crop evolution is not direct evidence of beer production. |
| P2 | [Vietnam cooperatives](../culture/vietnam-rice-distilling-cooperatives.md) | Verify fieldwork-era output and updated local regulations through modern Vietnamese-language sources; do not call old interview estimates “current.” |
