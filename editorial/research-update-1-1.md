# Research expansion: edition 1.1

This edition extends the original 153-profile wiki into a connected reference with **166 beer and tradition profiles, 17 ingredient references and 13 category guides**. The focus is explanatory depth, traceable evidence and useful comparison. The [source register](../references/source-register.md) now contains 260 external subject-source records, compared with 161 in the initial edition.

## Thirteen new profiles

[Apong — Mising rice-beer traditions](../styles/apong.md) · [Bili bili — Chadian sorghum beer](../styles/bili-bili.md) · [Chhang — Himalayan grain-fermentation traditions](../styles/chhang.md) · [Faro](../styles/faro.md) · [Italian Pilsner](../styles/italian-pilsner.md) · [Merissa — Sudanese sorghum brewing](../styles/merissa.md) · [Munkoyo — root-assisted cereal fermentation](../styles/munkoyo.md) · [New Zealand IPA](../styles/new-zealand-ipa.md) · [Rice Lager](../styles/rice-lager.md) · [Session IPA](../styles/session-ipa.md) · [Shangshan fermented-grain beverage](../styles/shangshan-beer.md) · [West Coast IPA](../styles/west-coast-ipa.md) · [West Coast Pilsner](../styles/west-coast-pilsner.md)

The kinds are deliberately different: newer market and judging labels, living traditions, historical documentation, a related grain ferment and archaeological evidence. Munkoyo is not silently reclassified as a standard alcoholic beer, and Shangshan is not presented as a recovered ancient style name.

Changes include more specific process explanations, additional firsthand or research evidence, clearer source access and stronger comparisons. The revised articles distinguish guideline definitions from producer practice, experimental formulations from household traditions, and archaeological interpretation from a living recipe. Sahti's article type now emphasizes its living tradition while retaining its explicit BJCP mapping.

## New reference layers

The [ingredient directory](../ingredients/index.md) has separate references for water; barley and malt; wheat; oats; rye; rice; maize; sorghum and millet; cassava; hops; brewing yeast; kveik; bacteria; mould and mixed starters; sugars; fruit; and herbs, spices and juniper.

The [category directory](../categories/index.md) adds practical comparisons for lagers, IPA, porter and stout, wheat and rye, British and Irish ales, Belgian and farmhouse families, sour fermentation, smoke and wood, traditional brewing, starch-ferment boundaries, historical evidence and low/no alcohol. The introductory classification guide explains why these axes overlap.

## Quality and maintenance

Independent AI passes checked selected high-risk claims and source mappings. Review corrections included narrowing a raw-material claim to the cited rice bran, clarifying caramel-malt processing, removing an unsupported IPA alias and separating sequencing detection from culturable organisms in the lambic account. These are editorial checks, not human expert approval.

Navigation, aliases, source backlinks and coverage counts were regenerated from the article metadata. An optional [offline validator](../tools/validate_wiki.py) checks links, anchors, citations, metadata, relation targets, directory membership and reachability. The [validation report](validation-report.md) records its actual final results.

## Deliberate limits

No world-completeness claim, mandatory recipe ranges or universal modern-to-historical genealogy is added. Abstract-only and indexed-source limitations remain explicit. The prose is original synthesis; copies of external publications are not bundled. Articles retain `status: draft` and do not acquire a human-reviewed trust claim.
