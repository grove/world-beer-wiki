# Brewing words in plain language

These short explanations are reading aids. Follow the linked profile for context and its external sources; they are not complete technical definitions.

| Term | Plain-language explanation | Example and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Body** | How light, full, or substantial the beer feels in the mouth. | [American Light Lager](../styles/american-light-lager.md) |
| **Carbonation** | Dissolved carbon dioxide, experienced as bubbles and sparkle. | [Weissbier](../styles/weissbier.md) |
| **Malt** | Grain prepared through germination and drying for brewing. | [Chicha de jora](../styles/chicha-de-jora.md) |
| **Grist** | The mixture of grains used for a brew. | [Historical London porter](../styles/historical-london-porter.md) |
| **Wort** | The sugar-containing liquid collected from the brewing grains, before fermentation. | [Kornøl](../styles/kornol.md) |
| **Mashing** | Working with grain and water so useful material, including fermentable sugars, enters the brewing liquid. | [Keptinis](../styles/keptinis.md) |
| **Adjunct** | An additional brewing material beyond the main malt base; the exact usage depends on context. | [American Lager](../styles/american-lager.md) |
| **Dry hopping** | Adding hops later in production to emphasize aroma rather than relying only on the kettle addition. | [American IPA](../styles/american-ipa.md) |
| **Attenuation** | The extent to which fermentation removes fermentable material; higher attenuation often means a drier finish. | [Brett beer](../styles/brett-beer.md) |
| **Lactic acidity** | Sourness associated with lactic acid, distinct from hop bitterness. | [Straight sour beer](../styles/straight-sour-beer.md) |
| **Mixed fermentation** | Fermentation involving more than one kind of yeast or bacteria. | [Mixed-fermentation sour beer](../styles/mixed-fermentation-sour-beer.md) |
| **Spontaneous fermentation** | Fermentation established from environmental microorganisms rather than simply one deliberately pitched brewing-yeast culture. | [Lambic](../styles/lambic.md) |
| **Raw ale** | A process description indicating that the wort is not boiled; it does not mean the grain is never heated. | [Kornøl](../styles/kornol.md) |
| **Koji** | A mold-grown substrate used to supply starch-breaking enzymes in Japanese brewing. | [Sake](../styles/sake.md) |
| **Nuruk** | A Korean grain-based fermentation starter carrying a community of microorganisms. | [Makgeolli](../styles/makgeolli.md) |
| **Tannin** | A contributor to a drying or puckering sensation, distinct from sweetness and acidity. | [Wild specialty beer](../styles/wild-specialty-beer.md) |
| **Base beer** | The underlying beer before a specialty ingredient or treatment is considered. | [Fruit beer](../styles/fruit-beer.md) |
| **Style category** | A classification boundary used by a named reference system, not automatically a historical lineage. | [International Pale Lager](../styles/international-pale-lager.md) |

## Additional process and classification terms

| Term | Reading definition | Context and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Saccharification** | Breaking starch into smaller sugars through enzymes; distinct from alcoholic fermentation. | [Saccharification](../ingredients/mould-and-mixed-starters.md) |
| **Gelatinization** | A heat-and-water change that makes starch more accessible; it is not itself fermentation. | [Gelatinization](../ingredients/rice.md) |
| **Kilning and roasting** | Heat treatments that shape malt properties; their role differs from grain germination. | [Kilning and roasting](../ingredients/barley-and-malt.md) |
| **Yeast strain** | A particular lineage or isolate within a species, with properties that need strain-specific evidence. | [Yeast strain](../ingredients/brewing-yeast.md) |
| **Kveik** | Traditional Norwegian brewing cultures and related isolates; not one complete beer style. | [Kveik](../ingredients/kveik.md) |
| **Starter culture** | Material carrying organisms used to initiate fermentation or starch conversion; it can contain more than yeast. | [Starter culture](../ingredients/mould-and-mixed-starters.md) |
| **Hop creep** | Renewed fermentation associated with enzyme activity introduced by dry hopping under suitable conditions. | [Hop creep](../ingredients/hops.md) |
| **Cask-conditioned beer** | In CAMRA’s account, beer continuing to condition in its cask and served without injected carbon dioxide. | [Cask-conditioned beer](../categories/british-and-irish-ales.md) |
| **Lagering** | Cold conditioning, which should be described separately from the choice of yeast. | [Lagering](../categories/lagers-and-cold-conditioning.md) |
| **Category axis** | The question used to group entries: ingredients, fermentation, sensory family, place, period or another stated dimension. | [Category axis](../categories/beer-classification.md) |
