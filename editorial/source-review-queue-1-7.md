# Edition 1.7 — research and independent review queue

These are **open review actions**, not reviewed claims. Every proposed step requires actual access to the named record or voluntary review by a relevant practitioner; do not upgrade an article merely because an LLM repeated the same statement.

| Priority | Subject | Next defensible research step |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [Abydos brewery](../history/abydos-early-royal-brewery.md) | Obtain published season-specific excavation reports and residue analyses; distinguish the estimated single-batch output from archaeological measurements and the proposed royal cult link. |
| P0 | [Piura chicherías](../culture/piura-chicheras-and-household-sales.md) | Read Hayashida’s full 2008 article and consult contemporary Piura-based brewing voices; do not recycle historic weekly sales schedules as current opening times. |
| P0 | [Arequipa archives](../history/arequipa-chicherias-colonial-republican-records.md) | Revisit Belan’s Spanish full text and named archival references with a historian; distinguish criminal-file selection bias from typical daily sales. |
| P0 | [Ghana pito businesses](../culture/ghana-pito-women-enterprise-survey.md) | Examine purposive survey questionnaire, statistical model and Ghana-based contextual scholarship; do not project the 362-person sample nationally. |
| P0 | [Japan sake inscription](../culture/japanese-koji-sake-heritage-and-apprenticeship.md) | Read full UNESCO nomination documents and Japanese-language community accounts with a practitioner; do not equate designation with scientific proof of all history. |
| P0 | [Philippine bubod and tapuy](../microbiology/bubod-tapuy-isolate-study.md) | Translate/check the 1990 Japanese full paper and interview Filipino makers; distinguish isolate count, culture prevalence and current process knowledge. |
| P1 | [Brewsters in England](../history/ale-tasters-and-brewster-protests.md) | Read Bennett’s complete chapters and underlying court rolls where feasible before quoting medieval speech verbatim. |
| P1 | [HorA/HorC bacteria](../microbiology/hora-horc-and-hop-resistance-markers.md) | Obtain experiment full texts and review with a brewing microbiologist; no standalone food safety or contamination diagnosis from gene detection. |
| P1 | [Packaging LCA](../environment/beer-packaging-transport-and-return-loops.md) | Compare functional units, return loops, model uncertainties and distance assumptions across the 2022 and 2024 assessments before offering numbers. |
| P1 | [Spent grain reuse](../environment/spent-grain-food-safety-and-circularity.md) | Check food processing and safety regulations in specific markets before implying a reuse product is ready for sale. |
| P2 | [Cheese pairing](../food/cheese-beer-preference-is-not-a-rule.md) | Obtain full panel methodology, food selections and participant context before offering a replicable sensory conclusion. |
