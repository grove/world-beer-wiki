# Research edition 1.11 — evidence before equivalence

**28 October 2026 · 34 new articles · 18 original articles substantively revised.** Starts from merged 1.10; earlier edition reports remain historical snapshots.

## Research contributions

| Area | Contribution and limit |
|---|---|
| Mexican agave fermentation | Pulque, aguamiel, five-stage metagenomics, first-pulque colonial ritual interpretation, and 1778–1821 Chalco taxes. Church transcriptions and fiscal documents are different forms of evidence. |
| Brazilian cassava food-drinks | Historical cauim, Tupinambá Olivença giroba, Tapirapé household food and three substrate-dependent microbiological studies. Shared terms or starch are not proof of an unchanged Indigenous recipe. |

## Limitations and method

Most primary fieldwork and microbiology papers were accessed as indexed abstracts, while several open technical reviews and experimental reports were consulted in more detail. A title and citation do not imply direct access to the full paper. A community name is not permission to reproduce restricted ritual knowledge, and a biochemical observation is not a medical or microbial safety certification. Independent community, historical and scientific review is **not complete**.
