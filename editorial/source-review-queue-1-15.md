# Edition 1.15: unperformed independent checks

These are **open tasks, not completed approvals**. No living community has been asked to endorse a legal description or a new global beer category. Do not use this collection to justify unsafe handling or unlicensed reproduction of source materials.

| Priority | Source and scope | Next genuinely independent check |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [England-applicable 2023 consultation](../categories/no-low-alcohol-descriptors-across-borders.md) | Check subsequent governmental response, any enforceable English regulations, current guidance and devolved-nation distinctions. Do not present a proposal as 2026 law. |
| P0 | [US TTB labeling and legal definitions](../history/us-beer-vs-malt-beverage-definitions.md) | Verify current CFR text, agency updates, state-law variation and individual product coverage with a qualified US regulatory specialist. |
| P1 | [1991 ATF circular](../history/from-1991-atf-circular-to-current-guidance.md) | Review intervening rulemaking and contemporaneous marketing or trade documents; do not infer a continuous policy history from two snapshots. |
| P1 | [2021 CO2 nucleation study](../sensory/beer-bubbles-and-model-boundaries.md) | Replicate or compare different beer matrices, temperatures and intentionally nucleated versus ordinary glassware. No universal sensory ranking inferred. |
| P1 | [Glass service guidance](../objects/beer-clean-glassware.md) | Compare independent hygiene standards and venue-specific professional inspection; visual lacing is not microbiological confirmation. |
| P1 | [Low-/no-alcohol challenge study](../microbiology/nolo-hurdles-are-not-label-rules.md) | Read full methods, matrices and test-organism limits with qualified food-safety microbiologists; never turn the study into a home preservation protocol. |
| P1 | [Water accounting](../environment/brewery-water-ratio-versus-water-footprint.md) | Test functional units, location, flow and agricultural boundaries using a real documented brewery inventory before reporting numbers. |
| P1 | [Water manual](../environment/direct-and-indirect-water-in-brewing.md) | Seek lawful access to member-only full guidance and compare with local water regulations; do not invent target ratios. |
| P2 | [Alcohol-free pairing trial](../food/alcohol-descriptors-on-a-menu.md) | Review full design, participants, recipes and independent repetitions; one dish and three styles are not a universal pairing law. |
| P2 | [Community terminology](../culture/institutional-vocabularies-of-beer.md) | Obtain voluntary maker or community language review before promoting a local term to a global synonym. |

The standing [ATLAS-012](atlas-specification.md) human/community review work item remains **planned**, and historical review queues are not silently closed. Licensed imagery/recordings and live events remain **deferred**.
