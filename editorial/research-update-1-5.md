# Research edition 1.5 · 27 October 2026

This installment starts from the **merged 1.4 Git tree** and adds **31 new source-bound subject articles**, **14 substantive research extensions** to existing pages and a complete machine-maintained subject index. The wiki now includes **372 subject articles** across **13 reference layers**, while preserving the original **166 beer/tradition profile paths**. These are differently scoped article counts, not a claim about the total number of beer styles worldwide.

## Significant new lines of inquiry

**Ancient brewing as social infrastructure:** Cerro Baúl in Wari Peru provides unusually detailed architectural and botanical evidence of large-scale fermentation, including molle drupes, purpose-built spaces and ceremonial closure. The earlier excavation text and later abstract-based reassessment have different access limitations and conclusions; neither proves an exact recipe, an empire-wide custom or uninterrupted descent.

**Who has appeared in brewing records?** Medieval brewsters, Oxford’s institutional economy, Smithsonian oral-history finding aids, a 1970 brewery acquisition and a contemporary Black brewing collaboration provide distinct lenses on labor, ownership and voice. These sources are not interchangeable: book abstracts, museum accounts and finding aids must retain their context.

**Microbial communities beyond conventional yeast:** Himalayan marcha, Korean nuruk-associated amylolytic yeast, Vietnamese bánh men, Ivorian sorghum-beer isolates and experimental Ethiopian tella botanicals illuminate distinct functional paths. Detection does not establish function; a sampled organism does not determine a universal regional recipe or prove product safety.

**Aroma, bitterness and evidence:** Controlled beer-model bitterness experiments and reviewed dry-hop chemistry explain why bitterness, IBU and astringency differ. A 2026 Tuscan sensory study is limited to its sampled beers and panel. A separate guide compares the current BJCP 2021 beer edition and the Brewers Association 2026 edition without conflating category systems or reproducing protected descriptions.

## Better everyday navigation and maintenance

The new [complete subject index](../navigation/all-atlas-subjects.md) covers all thirteen layers rather than the original style-only directories, and can be regenerated or checked deterministically with `python tools/build_subject_index.py --check`. Original paths and old research reports remain unchanged. The new [source supplement](../references/atlas-sources-1-5.md) preserves the consulted scope of each work and its backlinks. The [review queue](source-review-queue-1-5.md) specifies highest-value follow-up research and actual independent review steps. Additional scoped implementation items ATLAS-032–ATLAS-038 appear in the [specification](atlas-specification.md).

## Boundaries and missing work

No interview recordings or unavailable chapters are represented as read. Abstract-only results are narrow; search-indexed primary-paper passages do not replace full original figures and tables. No community representative, brewer, microbiologist or historian has independently signed off on this research. Structural checks do not establish historical truth or certify a safe production process. No new external images, recordings or restricted source text are bundled.
