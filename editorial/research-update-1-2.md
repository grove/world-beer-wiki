## What changes in understanding

Cultural accounts preserve named settings: Zoigl's shared infrastructure, a Norogachi narrative about reciprocal work, Mising starter exchange, monastic production criteria, and bounded introductions to Sesotho occasions and dolo livelihoods. They are not interchangeable national customs or claims that consuming alcohol defines community membership.

The microbial and process pages separate a species, a selected strain, a maintained culture, inoculation and subsequent transformations. The calendar separates when beer is produced, why a batch is prepared, when it is served and a dated event edition. Objects and sensory pages explain why a vessel or an aroma does not automatically identify a recipe. Four narrative routes connect these distinctions without invented dialogue or origin legends.

## Research provenance

The [source supplement](../references/atlas-sources.md) identifies external primary research, firsthand fieldwork, institutional and producer accounts, the explicitly labelled dolo review, and internal dependencies on earlier corpus synthesis. New prose does **not** imply that every original source was freshly re-read. Direct-access problems and the tesgüino screenshot limitation remain visible. No restricted ritual instructions, medical benefits or universal safety assurances were added.

## Existing pages and the searchable site

Eight existing beer/tradition profiles and four ingredient overviews gained labelled Atlas connection sections. These are navigation improvements, not a claim that their full histories were independently re-investigated. Original evidence notes and canonical filenames remain unchanged.

The concurrently added searchable-site implementation is preserved. New sections remain automatically discoverable, article chips cover the new subject layers, and linked maintenance Python sources are staged as readable files. The optional site does not replace Markdown as the source of truth.

## Scope tracking and maintenance

The [specification](atlas-specification.md) contains fourteen stable-ID work items and acceptance criteria. The [progress report](atlas-progress.md) exposes eleven implemented first-installment items, one planned independent-review item and two deferred features. These states describe scoped delivery, not cultural completeness or human verification.

The atlas utility checks evidence-bearing relationships, calendar metadata, new section membership and deliverable paths. It exports a deterministic concept catalog, qualified reverse links and the scope ledger. The read-only validation workflow provides test results and a portable snapshot; no database or network service is required for these checks.

## Remaining gaps

Coverage is still uneven, particularly beyond the selected European and African cases and the Norogachi/Mising accounts. Locally reviewed festival calendars, original-language terminology, detailed botanical identity, more primary microbial studies, and economic/historical fieldwork remain priorities. Human specialist and community review has not occurred. Licensed images, audio, a compound-level sensory atlas and interactive/date-triggered features are not delivered here.
