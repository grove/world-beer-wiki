## Evidence and interpretation

- **Local cases:** Shawi participatory photography and water/masato sampling, one field-observed mbege technique, a Rombo anthropological **abstract**, Ethiopian fermentation studies, and a Darjeeling/Sikkim microbiology **abstract** are different research designs. None is a region-wide universal recipe.
- **Safety boundary:** 26/26 tested water samples versus 2/11 masato samples positive for coliform are observations in one study, **not** a recommendation to substitute fermented cassava for safe water. No bark foraging/ingestion advice follows from the msesewe fermentation observation.
- **Scientific boundaries:** LTP1 and Protein Z laboratory evidence does not guarantee finished-beer foam performance; hop thiol precursors, terpenoid transformations, yeast transcripts and aroma perception are distinct endpoints.
- **Access transparency:** Record the indexed abstract/excerpts where full paper access was not obtained. A link to a PDF is not proof of review. None of the articles have been independently brewer-, historian-, microbiologist- or community-reviewed.
