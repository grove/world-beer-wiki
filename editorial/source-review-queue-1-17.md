# Edition 1.17 — work still needing independent verification

**An open queue, not completed approvals.** The appearance of a source in the register shows attribution and access notes, not professional sign-off or community consent.

| Priority | Topic and evidence boundary | Next independent action |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [Controlled Fusarium-to-beer study](../processes/fusarium-malt-to-beer-transformation.md) | Independently inspect full methods, replicates, mass-balance calculations and analytical uncertainty with specialists. It was a deliberately contaminated experiment, not a commercial production or decontamination study. |
| P0 | [EU food contaminant categories](../food/spent-grain-and-food-safety-boundaries.md) | Confirm applicability of the current consolidated regulation, later amendments, product definition, by-product destination and local rules with competent authorities; no universal beer limit inferred. |
| P0 | [Grain-DNA, metabolites and food-safety inference](../microbiology/fungal-dna-versus-mycotoxin-measurement.md) | Review validated assay performance, limits of quantification and alternative compound forms; DNA, toxin and viable fungi cannot be treated as interchangeable test results. |
| P1 | [2024 Manitoba crop trial](../microbiology/fusarium-strain-effects-in-barley.md) | Review study methods with plant pathologists; compare additional genotypes, seasons, environments and replication before forecasting field or beer risks. |
| P1 | [Barley sprouting and drought](../environment/barley-harvest-wet-weather-risks.md) | Inspect independent agronomic field datasets; confirm cultivar, crop stage and geographic transferability rather than issuing a harvest forecast. |
| P1 | [Bere Scottish-island heritage](../history/bere-introduction-date-uncertain.md) | Seek voluntary local farmer, miller, archival and archaeobotanical review. The original introduction date remains uncertain and a living landrace is not an exact Neolithic recipe. |
| P1 | [UHI Bere research page](../culture/orkney-bere-knowledge-and-revival.md) | Recheck direct page access after the source URL returned a server error; confirm institutional summaries and current participants against primary publications. |
| P1 | [Arsi versus Estie market studies](../culture/estie-malt-barley-cooperatives.md) | Seek Ethiopian farmer/cooperative voices and local social-science review; avoid combining different districts' estimates into a national income guarantee. |
| P1 | [Malt hot-steep sensory](../sensory/malt-hot-steep-experiments.md) | Read full experimental paper, check panel sizes/training and compare with actual finished beer testing before deriving consumer preference claims. |
| P1 | [Spent grain as food or feed](../food/spent-grain-and-food-safety-boundaries.md) | Verify actual product-specific contaminant, microbiological, nutritional and regulatory assessment. No handling or processing guidance offered by this wiki. |
| P2 | [Modified mycotoxin systematic review](../ingredients/don-glucoside-as-modified-mycotoxin.md) | Review full risk-assessment methods and matrix-specific analytical standards beyond the indexed sections; avoid equating molecular conversion with detoxification. |
| P2 | [Environmental resilience claims](../environment/bere-barley-crop-resilience.md) | Collect a reproducible crop and brewery life-cycle inventory using a consistent functional unit before publishing footprint comparisons. |
