# Edition 1.13 independent-review and full-source queue

**Open tasks, not completed endorsements.** Even when a source is indexed or open access, the current articles have not been independently reviewed by the named communities, brewers, historians, microbiologists or water engineers. No restricted ritual instructions or unsafe household protocols are requested.

| Priority | Evidence boundary | Independent next step |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [Jiahu 2026 reanalysis](../history/jiahu-2004-to-2026-reinterpretation.md) | Obtain and inspect the full 2026 article, figures and context of individual starch/mold identifications; seek external archaeobotanical review before saying any recipe is established. |
| P0 | [Tchoukoutou yeast conflict](../microbiology/tchoukoutou-yeast-surveys-in-disagreement.md) | Compare 2011, 2013 and 2025 full methods, sample identity, isolation versus sequencing denominator and dates with Beninese authors; no universal *S. pombe* or *S. cerevisiae* dominance claim. |
| P0 | [Muratina fibre](../microbiology/muratina-yeast-immobilization.md) | Inspect full 2003 Kenyan thesis and discuss botanical identity, starter handling and local terminology with qualified Kenyan makers; do not publish an unverified culture-reuse or ingestion method. |
| P0 | [Sagana marriage study](../culture/muratina-and-agikuyu-marriage-negotiation.md) | Consult full 2026 ethnography and willing community reviewers before expanding accounts of religious practice or assigning annual events. |
| P0 | [Bushera households](../culture/bushera-household-fermentation-in-southwestern-uganda.md) | Verify original surveys with Ugandan regional researchers and local producers, especially names, food-drink status and changes since 2003. |
| P1 | [Tchapalo fermentation and storage](../microbiology/tchapalo-fermentation-versus-spoilage.md) | Obtain full 2011, 2020 and 2022 paper methods and expert food microbiology review; selected laboratory inhibition tests and spoilage trials are not real-world safety certificates. |
| P1 | [Malwa classification](../styles/malwa-ajon.md) | Obtain Iteso-authored/locally reviewed sources and measured batch information; 2025 secondary-review use of “nonalcoholic” is not an ABV guarantee. |
| P1 | [Chibuku industrial history](../culture/chibuku-and-industrial-opaque-beer.md) | Separate product marketing and engineering histories from independent accounts of rural brewing and contemporary commercial practice. |
| P1 | [Water reuse](../environment/brewery-water-reuse-italy-portugal.md) | Inspect full LCA functional units and the Portugal pilot’s contaminant monitoring and regulatory criteria before drawing transfer or safety conclusions. |
| P1 | [Aldehydes and antioxidants](../sensory/beer-matrix-bound-aldehydes.md) | Independent brewing chemist to review spiked concentrated matrices and storage endpoints; do not infer clinical health effects or universal shelf lives. |
| P2 | [Czech packaging LCA](../environment/czech-brewery-scale-and-packaging-lca.md) | Review full datasets, grid assumptions, bottle return cycles and transportation before ranking packaging or brewery sizes. |
