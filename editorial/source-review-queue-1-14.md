# Edition 1.14 — research and independent-review queue

**Open tasks, not completed verification or endorsements.** This list gives specific source and expertise gaps. It does not solicit restricted cultural knowledge, unsafe microbiology instructions or proprietary process parameters.

| Priority | Subject and evidence boundary | Next independent action |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [Pilsen 1842 founding](../history/pilsen-1842-evidence-and-origin-myths.md) | Access Rail's entire 2012 analysis and the original 1892 jubilee document, and compare Czech-language brewing records with a qualified historian. The producer chronology is not an origin proof for all lager. |
| P0 | [Tella household vessel smoking](../culture/tella-household-vessels-and-hospitality.md) | Seek willing local Ethiopian maker and language review; inspect the original studies behind the 2024 synthesis before assigning botanical or antimicrobial details. No unverified sanitization advice. |
| P1 | [English hop growing and work](../culture/kent-hop-farms-and-work.md) | Consult hop-pickers' oral histories, farm ledgers and building surveys. Historic England's architectural synthesis is not a laborer-by-laborer history or a live harvest forecast. |
| P1 | [Belgian heritage learning](../culture/belgian-beer-learning-networks.md) | Seek current practitioner/education documentation and local perspectives; UNESCO inscription does not judge recipe authenticity for every producer. |
| P1 | [American brewer labor and migration](../history/american-beer-households-immigration-and-industry.md) | Inspect individual Smithsonian oral-history audio/transcripts before quoting named interviews or expanding details about workers and enslaved labor. |
| P1 | [Industrial lager cell viability](../microbiology/industrial-lager-yeast-viability.md) | Review full 2025 methodology, strain properties and replication design with industrial microbiologist. Avoid transferring the specified process window to other plants. |
| P1 | [Draught system quality](../microbiology/draught-lines-as-a-different-microbial-habitat.md) | Consult the current complete Brewers Association manual and applicable local rules; technical best practice is not surveillance of any individual venue. |
| P1 | [Humulinone and foam](../sensory/protein-z-humulinone-foam.md) | Read full 2023/2024 study and compare finished-beer validation with the 2025 LTP1 work. Do not extrapolate protein solution findings into beer shelf life. |
| P1 | [Yeast/trub resource reuse](../environment/hot-trub-and-spent-yeast-valorization.md) | Assess representative sampling, downstream contaminant testing, regulatory and LCA burdens before recommending reuse or food/feed uses. |
| P2 | [Closed-loop brewery model](../environment/brewery-closed-loop-case-vs-general-rule.md) | Recalculate functional units and independently check reported/estimated Tsingtao intensities and assumptions for bottle return, electricity and upstream barley. |
