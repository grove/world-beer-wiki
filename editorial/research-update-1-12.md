# Research edition 1.12 — grains, microbes, places and times

**30 newly researched subjects, 18 sourced revisions, 15 distinct external works, 73 article-to-source records.** The wiki now spans **579 subject articles across 13 sections**. This is expanded coverage, not 579 universally standardized beer styles. Earlier canonical URLs (including Boza, Kvass and Ikigage) remain intact.

## What was learned, and why it matters

### Zimbabwe: a cereal malt is not merely an enzyme supplier

The [Mahewu profile](../styles/mahewu.md) connects [community cereal choices](../culture/mahewu-cereal-choices.md), [porridge and malt preparation](../processes/mahewu-cooked-porridge-and-malt.md), [microbiota originating in millet malt](../microbiology/mahewu-malt-origin.md) and [2026 defined-culture trials](../microbiology/mahewu-starter-cultures-2026.md). A 2019 study and the 2026 open-text research answer different questions: the former traced sampled fermentation populations to a possible input, while the latter selected organisms and measured controlled fermentation behavior. The [2024 grain-substitution survey](../environment/mahewu-grain-availability-and-resilience.md) is about respondent preferences and access, not a national prediction or emissions assessment. None of the studies validates a recipe's universal shelf stability or drinking safety.

### Türkiye and Bulgaria: markets and fermentations are different evidence

[Boza](../styles/boza.md) was already a canonical profile and was **revised in place**. A [1997 Turkish experimental process](../microbiology/boza-fermentation-1997.md) measured ethanol in one preparation; a [2022 Bulgarian isolate study](../microbiology/bulgarian-boza-pediococcus.md) characterized a selected set of lactic-acid bacteria from a different place. [Cold-season Istanbul vending](../culture/istanbul-boza-sellers.md) is a documented service association, while an [Ottoman historian's abstract](../history/ottoman-drink-restrictions-and-negotiation.md) does not prove every bozahane was targeted by every alcohol or tavern ban. These sources should not be merged into one universal recipe, health claim or origin legend.

### Taiwan: a named people's ritual is not the community identity of an unnamed sample

Taiwan's Indigenous-affairs agency describes [Paiwan millet and community occasions](../culture/paiwan-millet-and-community-occasions.md), including a [Five-Year Ritual in specific groups](../calendar/paiwan-five-year-ritual-and-millet-wine.md). A separate [2013 university-indexed microbial study](../microbiology/taiwan-millet-starter-lab-study.md) examined a Taiwanese Indigenous millet beverage but its indexed abstract **does not name the sampled community**. No laboratory isolates are attributed to a Paiwan rite without that chain of provenance. No fixed Gregorian ceremony date or restricted ritual method is asserted; community review remains open.

### Georgian highlands: aludi, occasions and the boundaries of official heritage accounts

An official regional overview documents [Aludi](../styles/aludi-georgian-highland-beer.md), its [ceremonial gatherings](../culture/georgian-highland-beer-and-blessings.md) and [summer seasonal associations](../calendar/aludi-summer-gatherings.md). Government tourism writing is valuable as a public account, not equivalent to an independently reviewed ethnography, a detailed community recipe or a UNESCO inscription. The comparison with [Finnish Sahti](../styles/sahti.md) is editorial—not a proposed lineage.

### Japan, Eastern Europe and Rwanda: keep the transformation and sample in view

A sake-industry-authored review distinguishes [Koji Amazake](../processes/koji-amazake-saccharification.md) from [sake-lees variants](../food/amazake-sugar-and-sake-lees.md); the name does not certify that every product is ethanol-free. A 2022 barley-malt [Kvass experiment](../processes/kvass-bread-versus-malt.md) is not a household rye-bread history, so the existing [Kvass](../styles/kvass.md) profile is amended rather than replaced. A [2010 Ikigage study](../microbiology/ikigage-southern-rwanda-samples.md) sampled Southern Province Rwanda; it does not define all Rwandan practices or [Urugwa](../styles/urugwa.md). A secondary index's 2012 citation is noted separately from the 2010 issue record.

## Open limits and editorial ethics

[The 1.12 review queue](source-review-queue-1-12.md) lists source access, local-language, Paiwan/community, Georgian producer, microbiology and brewing-history questions. Abstracts are not misrepresented as full reading; producer and government accounts remain attributed. We have **not** obtained independent cultural, historian, fermentation microbiologist, food-safety or brewer approval. No sacred or restricted knowledge, inferred universal recipe, fixed calendar date, medicinal benefit or safety certification was added. All new research articles remain `draft`. As with prior releases, structural validation checks internal consistency, not historical fact.
