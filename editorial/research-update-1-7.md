# Edition 1.7 — archaeology, labor, rice starters and research-method clarity

**Baseline:** 402 subject articles across 13 sections. **Now:** 428. **Added:** 26 new subjects; **revised:** 16 existing articles with cited extensions and actual research limitations; all prior canonical URLs preserved. New related-ferment entry: Philippine tapuy.

## Research themes

**Egyptian excavation and inference.** The 2018–2022 North Abydos investigations provide direct material context for a high-capacity early royal brewing operation, while its estimated output and cultic associations remain interpretative.

**Women, business and historical evidence.** Piura household chicherías and signs, Arequipa court archives, Ghanaian pito bars and small enterprises, and Bennett’s medieval English household history are compared as **different source types**, not a universal history of gender.

**Fermentation beyond a single yeast.** Bubod/tapuy isolate studies distinguish sugar-making and alcohol-forming functions; selected hop-resistant LAB experiments demonstrate why one gene cannot certify a microbe’s behavior. Koji-associated Japanese sake heritage remains a related ferment, not a relabeled beer style.

**Systems and sensory evidence.** Packaging LCA depends on distribution and reuse; spent grain needs safe handling; diacetyl changes through maturation; food-pairing preference depends on participants and circumstances.

## Scope and ethics

A useful encyclopedia distinguishes archived testimony, a fieldwork sample, a heritage inscription, a microbial isolate and an industrial trial. These pages do not propose universal recipes, health claims, current sales schedules, or direct descent between unrelated communities. None has been independently community- or specialist-reviewed.
