## Constraints and provenance

The [source supplement](../references/atlas-sources-1-4.md) registers **35 source IDs** with actual access scopes. Many pages use peer-reviewed abstract data only; they do not invent experimental details from inaccessible full texts. Some pages report an institution’s account of its own history. Local case studies and products remain situated. No independent human expert, scientific, historical, or community review took place.
