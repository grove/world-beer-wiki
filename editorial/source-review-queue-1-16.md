# Edition 1.16 — what still needs independent checking

**Open work, not completed approvals.** Source links are not evidence of fieldwork consent or full-text access. This queue prioritizes claims that could mislead readers if transferred across methods, markets or brewing communities.

| Priority | Scope and source limit | Next independent action |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [ISO triangle testing](../sensory/triangle-tests-for-beer.md) | Obtain legal access to all of ISO 4120:2021, its annexes and exact statistical procedure; confirm the design with qualified sensory scientists before reproducing any operational standard. |
| P0 | [Brewing laboratory guide](../processes/gravity-extract-and-estimated-alcohol.md) | Independently examine full 2019 paper and supporting calibrations; direct PMC open was intermittently blocked although indexed full-text sections were accessible. Do not translate experimental settings into safety instructions. |
| P0 | [pH, TA and acidification](../categories/acidity-ph-ta-and-sourness.md) | Seek microbiology and process-safety review before making product-specific claims. Acid-base measurements are not pathogen challenge studies. |
| P1 | [IBU experiment](../stories/two-beers-one-ibu.md) | Read the full 2022 ASBC journal article, sample sizes and compound analyses rather than only its publisher abstract. Do not generalize two beer cases into universal bitterness thresholds. |
| P1 | [Colour and turbid samples](../processes/beer-colour-measurement.md) | Verify current full ASBC/EBC procedures, instrument calibration and sample preparation against actual beers; no laboratory experiment was performed for this edition. |
| P1 | [Hydrometer and estimated ABV](../objects/hydrometers-and-reading-conditions.md) | Review instrument calibration standards and appropriate lower-strength measurement methods with qualified laboratory analysts. |
| P1 | [Richardson's historical records](../history/richardson-saccharometry-and-extract.md) | Read Sumner's complete article and Richardson's original 1784 text and compare interpretations with a historian. The museum object date remains approximate; do not fabricate an event day. |
| P1 | [Community and naming authority](../culture/brewing-numbers-and-institutional-authority.md) | Obtain voluntary local-language and practitioner input where community traditions are discussed. No living tradition has endorsed an instrument-derived label. |
| P1 | [Consumer expectation study](../sensory/expectations-and-beer-colour.md) | Obtain full participant/sample methods and independent replications before attributing an effect size to all drinkers. |
| P1 | [Food pairings and discrimination](../food/difference-tests-do-not-rank-pairings.md) | Review a real food-and-beer experiment with participant context and blinded preference measures; triangle-test significance cannot substitute for liking. |
| P2 | [Green claims versus lab metrics](../environment/analytical-consistency-is-not-a-life-cycle-footprint.md) | Compare with representative brewery-level environmental inventories and consistent functional units rather than treating analytical accuracy as sustainability certification. |
| P2 | [Images and standards rights](../objects/saccharometer-in-the-museum.md) | Check museum media licenses and standards copyright before adding images, excerpts or reproducing detailed normative procedures. |
