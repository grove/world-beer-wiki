# Edition 1.6 research update — 28 October 2026

**Baseline:** 372 subject articles. **After this expansion:** 402 across the same thirteen reference layers: 30 new subjects and 22 existing researched articles extended with new, explicitly scoped source notes. The four new beverage/related-ferment profiles bring the named profile count from 166 to 170. All earlier canonical subject URLs remain unchanged.

## What is new and why

**Community and substrate identity:** Ugandan Tonto and Rwandan Urugwa show how a comparative label like “banana beer” cannot substitute for a named drink and its distinct evidence. Three newly documented Southern Assam topics—Hmar Zu, Karbi Hor Alank and a five-cohort source comparison—preserve studied community names and sample limits. Lammin Sahti's apprenticeship story is explicitly a maker's account; its wedding and funeral associations belong to an occasion, not a fixed international festival calendar. A historical Chilean literature review is treated as mediated colonial testimony, not a uniform Mapuche voice.

**Experimental inference:** A new methods layer distinguishes DNA-marker detections, predicted pathways, isolated strain sequences and measured fermentation outcomes. Two independent 2026 farmhouse genetics studies are compared without adding isolate totals as a census or deriving an invention date from phylogeny. Rwandan and Ugandan substrate differences, root amylases in a Zambian munkoyo study, hop-resistant LAB, and phenolic aromas are documented without extending lab results to a whole community.

**Materials and sensory explanations:** Rhynchosia root, banana juice and ferulic acid now have distinct references. The “clove without clove” and “banana aroma without banana fruit” comparisons help readers interpret sensory terms without inventing ingredient declarations.

**Environmental boundaries:** A 2024 Mexican microbrewery assessment and a 2025 Japanese bottled-beer LCA are compared as separate local models. The crop-production exclusions and upstream-water definitions prevent false per-litre rankings.

**Research transparency:** A new offline research-hygiene checker verifies the edition's citations, explicit source access levels, draft review status and minimum cross-navigation. Existing canonical articles gained source-specific additions, not merely a block of links. The source supplement distinguishes abstracts, excerpts, primary experiments, community-facing producer narratives and reviewed synthesis. The project cannot claim specialist or community approval.

## What was not achieved

No original interviews, onsite cultural observation, laboratory experiments or independent human review were performed. Some cited works were accessible only through indexed abstracts or retrieved excerpts. Local-language account verification, safer interpretation of microorganism data, and full paper/method checking remain explicitly open. No medication, nutrition/health or fermentation-safety guarantee is claimed. The calendar metadata does not invent scheduled events, and neither LCA is a universal product footprint.
