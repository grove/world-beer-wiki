# Open source and community review queue — 1.6

These are **open actions**, not certification. Prioritize research and permission before claiming broader cultural scope.

| Priority | Articles | Unresolved question and next acceptable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | [Ugandan Tonto](../styles/tonto.md) | Read full 2026 field methods and sample storage/quality limits; obtain Uganda-based producer/practitioner contextual review. Do not generalize methanol results from two districts or publish untested safety advice. |
| P0 | [Rwandan Urugwa](../styles/urugwa.md) | Locate original Rwandan field and botanical sources behind the 2016 review. Confirm vocabulary, regional variation and contemporary context in locally authored accounts. |
| P0 | [Assam five-community comparison](../culture/southern-assam-five-beverage-study.md) | Resolve the source's Zao/Zou variant with the authors or language-specific local sources; confirm local names, group labeling, and permissions for culturally situated starter details. |

| P1 | [South African umqombothi](../microbiology/umqombothi-amplicons-and-predicted-function.md) | Check full sample replication, bioinformatics, interpretation of fungal ITS and predicted functions; separate taxa detection from actual safety and growth. |
| P1 | [Munkoyo Rhynchosia](../ingredients/rhynchosia-root-for-munkoyo.md) | Full 1997 article access plus Zambian maker and plant taxonomist review; confirm plant part, naming, local use and enzyme conditions without foraging advice. |

| P2 | [Other regions](../categories/traditional-and-indigenous-beers.md) | Deepen community-authored, permission-aware accounts in the Pacific, Central Asia, West/Central Africa and Latin America. Avoid continent-level shortcuts. |
