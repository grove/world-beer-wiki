# Edition 1.18 — independent review and full-source research queue

These are **open actions, not completed approvals**. No source is elevated from a publisher abstract to a full paper by citation reuse. Nothing here constitutes a pressure, microbial, regulatory, migration-testing or industrial-operation protocol.

| Priority | Subject and evidence boundary | Next independent action |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | [2025/40 EU packaging regulation](../environment/eu-packaging-reuse-law-scope.md) | Verify consolidated act, current national implementation, operator role, exemptions, HORECA guidance and future target dates with qualified EU counsel; a 12 August 2026 application day is not automatic 2030 compliance. |
| P0 | [Food-contact bisphenols](../food/can-liners-and-food-contact-evidence.md) | Review 2024/3190 and 2026 correction against the actual packaging contact layer, transition rules and approved test evidence; never infer compliance or exposure from container appearance. |
| P0 | [Package refermentation risks](../microbiology/package-refermentation-pressure-vs-quality.md) | Confirm actual producer safety programs and relevant engineering guidance with qualified brewery safety experts. No priming, pressure or intervention guidance from this atlas. |
| P1 | [2022 and 2024 beer packaging LCAs](../environment/seven-breweries-versus-pet-keg-case.md) | Obtain both complete inventories and harmonize functional unit, route, distance, load, reuse, cleaning, loss and energy assumptions; published scenario rankings must remain scoped. |
| P1 | [Reuse-system methods review](../environment/returnable-bottles-and-system-losses.md) | Read full 2025 article, inspect proposed best practices and compare hypothetical values against real refill/return data. |
| P1 | [EBC total package oxygen method](../processes/total-package-oxygen-has-two-phases.md) | Obtain legitimate access to the normative method and validate headspace/liquid measurement definitions, calibration and uncertainty before citing quantitative thresholds. |
| P1 | [2025 craft-beer storage study](../sensory/unpasteurized-ale-storage-case-2025.md) | Independently review methods, strain, replicate design and panel statistics before comparing other beer matrices or deriving shelf-life claims. |

| P1 | [1892 crown patent](../history/crown-cork-patent-vs-invention-myth.md) | Cross-check original patent drawings, correspondence, contemporary trade sources and ASME dates with a historian; inventor chronology is not a complete history of bottled beer. |
| P1 | [Smithsonian handled beer can](../objects/baltimore-handled-beer-can.md) | Verify collection provenance, donor notes and manufacturer identification with the museum; secure image permissions before any licensed illustration. |
| P2 | [Beer freshness resource hub](../processes/beer-package-oxygen-pathways.md) | Seek authorized access to member-only BA materials rather than inventing filling settings or a universal TPO target. |
| P2 | [Packaged beer and home consumption](../culture/packaged-beer-and-home-markets.md) | Inspect consumer/worker oral histories and local retail records. Museum collections and industrial LCAs cannot speak for all customers or communities. |
