# Can a changing climate change your beer?

A beer begins in fields and orchards of ingredients—often across different climates. Researchers modeled how simultaneous drought and heat extremes could affect barley supply, while another team measured historical shifts in European hops and projected further risks.[^v14-barley-climate][^v14-hops-climate]

## Two kinds of evidence

The barley analysis combines crop and economic scenarios. The hop paper uses actual regional observations as well as models. Neither tells us what any specific future bottle will taste like; variety choices, sourcing, brewing adjustments and energy systems intervene.[^v14-hops-climate]
