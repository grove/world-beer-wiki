# Is the Hymn to Ninkasi really a beer recipe?

An ancient poem mentions grain, beerbread and brewing. Is that enough to brew its beverage? The Oxford translation of the Hymn to Ninkasi contains repeated poetic lines and unresolved passages—not quantified ingredients, fermentation measurements or a procedure that can be replicated unambiguously.[^v14-ninkasi]

## The appealing shortcut

Calling the hymn the “oldest beer recipe” makes a memorable headline, but it compresses theological, poetic and technical evidence into a single claim. A modern reconstruction would necessarily make choices not supplied by the surviving text.[^v14-ninkasi]

## A more interesting question

Tate Paulette’s book explores ancient Mesopotamian beer using cuneiform, images and archaeology together. The [historical article](../history/hymn-to-ninkasi-and-beer.md), [archaeology guide](../history/how-archaeologists-identify-beer.md) and [grain conversion](../processes/starch-conversion.md) turn a simplistic origin story into an investigation.[^v14-ninkasi-book]
