# Did one brewer invent porter in a single afternoon?

## The satisfying story

A well-packaged origin anecdote gives porter one inventor, one day, and one neat recipe. It is attractive because it turns complicated economic history into a recognizable character and moment. Yet historians can study how stories were constructed, not only whether an early brewer existed.[^v13-porter-sumner]

## What scholarship says instead

James Sumner argues that porter’s identity and large-brewery industrialization developed together over time; the singular invention story compresses a longer technical and market process. Martyn Cornell provides historical discussion of porter’s changing popularity and contemporary revival. Neither source supports a precise universal first brew.[^v13-porter-sumner][^v13-porter-camra]

Who gained authority by declaring what counted as real porter? Read [Porter industrial history](../history/london-porter-and-industrialization.md) and [Historical London Porter](../styles/historical-london-porter.md) for the research rather than a legend.
