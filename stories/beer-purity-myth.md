# Was the Reinheitsgebot a 500-year-old universal beer recipe?

## Three ingredients, but no yeast?

The familiar three-word ingredients list from Bavaria's 1516 ordinance is often repeated as a timeless definition of beer. Historians show that it was a dated regulatory text concerned with beer quality, pricing and supply, not a complete biochemical explanation of a fermenting drink.[^v13-purity-lexicon]

## A term that arrived later

The Historisches Lexikon Bayerns reports that the word **Reinheitsgebot** itself only appeared in the early twentieth century. Similar brewing rules preceded the 1516 Bavarian legislation in other places. Modern identity and historical ordinance are related, but they cannot be collapsed into an unchanging recipe valid everywhere.[^v13-purity-lexicon]
