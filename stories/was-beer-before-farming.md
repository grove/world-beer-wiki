# Did brewing come before farming? A question from Raqefet Cave

## A provocative discovery

The Raqefet Cave study interprets mortar residues and contextual clues as evidence that a Natufian forager population brewed cereal beer around 13,000 years ago, before local cereal domestication. Brewing need not await the full social and agricultural system of later large settlements.[^v13-raqefet]

## The leap that does not follow

From this one could write the catchy headline that beer caused farming. But evidence for earlier beer cannot show why agriculture developed at multiple places or which human motives caused domestication. The original study explicitly treats that argument as debated rather than settled.[^v13-raqefet]
