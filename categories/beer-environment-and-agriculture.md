# Beer, agriculture and environmental trade-offs

Beer is a system that begins with crops, passes through fuel- and water-using processes, and generates material outputs beyond the served liquid. Comparing its environmental impacts demands more than comparing an old and a new beer style.[^v14-circular]

## Four distinct evidence streams

Models of climate and barley supply are not direct brewing life-cycle audits; hop-field trends are not a measure of carbon emissions. A Greek brewery case provides a water-efficiency example, while spent-grain research describes resource reuse pathways. Conflating these produces false rankings.[^v14-barley-climate][^v14-hops-climate][^v14-water]
