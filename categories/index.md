# Category guides

These **28 educational guides** explain how styles, ingredients, processes, places and periods overlap. They are reading maps, not 17 newly standardized beer styles. Begin with [How beer categories work](beer-classification.md).

| Guide | What it explains |
|---|---|
| [How beer categories work](beer-classification.md) | A practical model for separating beer styles, local traditions, ingredients, processes, and dated judging systems. |
| [Lagers and cold-conditioned beers](lagers-and-cold-conditioning.md) | Compare pale, dark, strong and hop-forward lagers while separating fermentation from cold conditioning. |
| [IPA and hop-forward beers](ipa-and-hop-forward-beers.md) | A navigation and interpretation guide separating IPA families, strength modifiers, hopping techniques and lager neighbors. |
| [Porter and stout](porter-and-stout.md) | Compare roast, strength, fermentation and historical change across porter and stout. |
| [Wheat and rye beers](wheat-and-rye-beers.md) | Distinguish wheat and rye beer families by fermentation, grain form, acidity and balance. |
| [British and Irish ales, cask and dispense](british-and-irish-ales.md) | Navigate bitter, mild, brown, strong and dark ales while keeping cask conditioning separate from style. |
| [Belgian families and farmhouse comparisons](belgian-and-farmhouse-ales.md) | Separate Belgian style families, monastic provenance and wider farmhouse traditions. |
| [Sour, mixed and spontaneous fermentation](sour-and-mixed-fermentation-beers.md) | Separate acidity, microbial cultures, inoculation methods and named sour-beer traditions. |
| [Smoke, wood, fruit and specialty categories](smoke-wood-and-specialty-beers.md) | Distinguish a named style from an added ingredient, wood treatment, smoke source or maturation process. |
| [Traditional and Indigenous brewing](traditional-and-indigenous-beers.md) | A comparative route through living beer traditions, related starch ferments, local knowledge, and the limits of style-based classification. |
| [Starch fermentation and the boundaries of beer](starch-fermentation-and-beer-boundaries.md) | Compare malt beers, mixed-starter grain drinks and related starch ferments without erasing local beverage identities. |
| [Historical evidence, living traditions and revivals](historical-and-revival-beers.md) | Read archaeological findings, brewing records, living practice and modern recreations without conflating them. |
| [Low- and no-alcohol beers](low-and-no-alcohol-beers.md) | A cross-cutting category covering alcohol labels, production routes, and their relationship to sensory style. |

Each guide links to canonical profiles and preserves the scope of its sources. A shared heading, ingredient or comparison link does not establish ancestry.
