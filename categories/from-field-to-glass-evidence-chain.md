# From field to glass: five distinct evidence layers

A beer description often starts with the glass, but there are several causal layers behind what a drinker experiences. The English hop-oast record concerns **agricultural handling**; Pilsen's brewery history concerns **institutions and production**; industrial yeast experiments concern **fermentation conditions**; draught technical guidance concerns **service**; environmental studies concern **resources and their defined accounting boundaries**.[^v114-oasts][^v114-yeastplant]

These layers can interact without becoming the same evidence. An oast's preserved kiln proves an infrastructure practice, not the smell of beer made with its former hops. A microbial viability result in a large controlled tank cannot establish how a farmer-maintained starter will behave. A draught defect at retail cannot be retroactively used to infer the brewer's fermentation organism.[^v114-draught]

Environmental accounting adds another scale. A Chinese industrial closed-loop case uses specific operational and life-cycle system boundaries. It is useful for questions about waste, water and carbon flow but cannot rank a household brewery in another region without comparable functional units and measurements.[^v114-closedloop]

Readers can move through [beer categories](beer-classification.md), [draught service](../processes/draught-quality-from-keg-to-glass.md), and [environmental comparison](../environment/how-to-compare-brewing-footprints.md). The atlas should preserve the *type of evidence attached to each link*, rather than flattening every association into a universal causal assertion. That is what makes knowledge graphs and human stories useful together.[^v114-closedloop]

## Edition 1.17: newly sourced connections

A single sample can cross several evidence layers without keeping the same interpretation. The 2026 Biehl controlled experiment tracks different Fusarium-associated metabolites from deliberately inoculated barley through malt and brewed beer. It demonstrates that chemical forms and measured fractions can change with processing, but cannot determine the status of an unrelated commercial lot or every consumer's exposure.[^v117-fusarium-trial]

The European Union's 2023/915 contaminant rules, in the consolidated version available on 28 October 2026, specify categories and criteria for food products. The legal applicability of a cereal threshold is not automatically a beer compliance test. A regulation's existence also cannot prove that the experiment's batch or any named product has been approved.[^v117-eu-contaminants]

Read the new [field-to-glass grain evidence guide](field-to-glass-grain-evidence.md) alongside [process transfer](../processes/fusarium-malt-to-beer-transformation.md). This reinforces the distinction among field surveys, chemical experiments, quality control, sensory observations and current legal interpretation.

## Edition 1.19: newly sourced connections

The grain-to-beer evidence chain now has two additional industrial destinations: fermentation exhaust and wastewater. A 2026 brewery-waste review describes strongly varying organic demand and cleaning-related characteristics in wastewater streams; a sample's chemical oxygen demand is not the water volume used in crop production or a direct product safety judgment.[^v119-wastewater26]

Grand and colleagues' 2025 research on craft brewing CO₂ recovery examines gas capture technology and interviews five French breweries. Their proposed regional valorization infrastructure is not a documented, operating treatment chain, and a captured fermenter gas stream is not automatically beverage-grade or permanently stored carbon.[^v119-craftco2]

An atlas should keep material and evidence endpoints typed: crop, beer, gas, wastewater, resource recovery, social network and regulatory status. Compare [wastewater COD versus water footprints](wastewater-cod-is-not-water-footprint.md) and [gas recovery and carbon accounting](fermentation-co2-recovery-is-not-net-zero.md). Neither industrial recovery option defines a local beverage's cultural identity.
