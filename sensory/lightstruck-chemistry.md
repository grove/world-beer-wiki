# Why beer can become lightstruck

The characteristic “skunky” note often called lightstruck is associated with a sulfur compound formed through light-triggered chemistry. Laboratory research links excited riboflavin with photodegradation of certain hop-derived bittering principles.[^v14-light]

## A molecular chain, not a single ingredient

The ACS study traces radical precursors on the way to 3-methylbut-2-ene-1-thiol. Exposure, chemical composition and packaging can influence what happens; merely identifying a green bottle is not a laboratory measurement of its contents.[^v14-light]

## Two different forms of aging

[Oxidative staling](oxidative-aging.md) concerns other reactions, while [packaging](../processes/packaging-and-aging.md) considers multiple threats together. Hop bitterness, aroma, light sensitivity and storage time must remain separate variables.[^v14-hop]

## Edition 1.10: newly sourced connections

A 2006 model-beer and mainstream-lager experiment followed riboflavin absorbance loss under light and changes associated with light-struck character. It assessed selected compounds and their interaction with isohumulone. A separate 2001 radical-chemistry study used a different experimental method to investigate iso-alpha-acid photodecomposition.[^v110-photo-2006][^v110-photo-2001]

Together they offer stronger mechanistic triangulation, but neither provides a universal glass-color ranking or safe exposure duration. The expanded [riboflavin ingredient guide](../ingredients/riboflavin-and-beer-light.md) and [light-sensitive packaging](../objects/beer-bottles-and-light-barriers.md) keep the ingredient, matrix and container questions distinct.

## Edition 1.18: newly sourced connections

Huvaere and colleagues' 2005 spin-trapping study identified radical chemistry involving riboflavin and hop-derived bitter compounds, supporting a mechanistic route toward the lightstruck thiol rather than measuring its concentration in all stored beers.[^v118-photochem05] A 2023 pulsed-light study on two beer matrices found differences in 3-MBT formation under selected light-filter conditions, but the apparatus is not an exact simulation of every retail bottle's exposure.[^v118-pulse23]

Source-aware discussion should identify actual light spectrum, beer matrix, chemical assay and sensory test. A green bottle is not a confirmed defect.
