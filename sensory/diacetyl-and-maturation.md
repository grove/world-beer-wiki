# Buttery diacetyl and beer maturation

“Buttery” is a sensory word; diacetyl is a measured molecule that can contribute such an impression. The distinction matters because fermentation chemistry, style expectations and human sensitivity can all differ.[^v14-diacetyl]

## Formation is not a static accident

A review describes diacetyl production from alpha-acetolactate and later reduction by yeast during beer maturation. A single early fermentation measurement does not necessarily describe what a finished, conditioned beer will smell like.[^v14-diacetyl]
