# Sensory reasoning: what does a tasting note actually establish?

A description of a smell, taste or texture is useful evidence of an experience. It is not automatically an ingredient list, a process record or a claim about history.

[Flavor detective: aroma is not an ingredient](aroma-is-not-an-ingredient.md) compares several routes to familiar flavor words.

[Smoke, roast, baked mash and wood](smoke-roast-and-wood.md) follows different transformations that a dark-beer description can accidentally collapse.

These are reading guides, not laboratory assays or a complete flavor-compound encyclopedia. Ingredient-specific chemistry and controlled sensory research remain in the [backlog](../editorial/atlas-specification.md).
