# Edition 1.13: nine evidence-led reading trails

This set of **nine question-led trails** joins source-limited studies across the existing encyclopedia. Similarity in a grain, organism, vessel or process never establishes a shared cultural origin or a safe brewing method.

## The prehistoric beverage that got a second look

[Jiahu evidence](../styles/jiahu-fermented-beverage.md) → [2004 vs 2026](../history/jiahu-2004-to-2026-reinterpretation.md) → [Pottery samples](../objects/jiahu-pottery-residue-sampling.md) → [Starch inference](../processes/jiahu-starch-and-mould-inference.md)

*Evidence boundary:* Chemistry and microfossils are complementary but not identical tests; neither paper contains a surviving recipe.

## A sorghum beer is also a livelihood

[Tchoukoutou](../styles/tchoukoutou.md) → [Brewing enterprises](../culture/tchoukoutou-household-enterprises.md) → [Grain quality](../ingredients/benin-brewing-sorghum-varieties.md)

*Evidence boundary:* 2007 Benin income and consumer reports are dated household observations, not current earnings.

## Which yeast lives in Tchoukoutou?

[Survey disagreement](../microbiology/tchoukoutou-yeast-surveys-in-disagreement.md) → [Kpete-kpete](../microbiology/kpete-kpete-sorghum-starter.md) → [Evidence methods](../categories/yeast-survey-method-disagreements.md)

*Evidence boundary:* Isolate percentages from different locations and identification methods cannot define a universal culture.

## Welcome yeast, unwanted change

[Tchapalo](../styles/tchapalo.md) → [Yeast and spoilage](../microbiology/tchapalo-fermentation-versus-spoilage.md) → [Sourness is not safety](../categories/acidification-is-not-safety-certification.md)

*Evidence boundary:* A selected challenge test of a filtered beer is not a cultural recipe or hazard survey.

## When a fruit works like brewing equipment

[Muratina](../styles/muratina.md) → [Kigelia fibre](../ingredients/kigelia-fruit-fibre.md) → [Object](../objects/muratina-fibrous-starter-carriers.md) → [Attached microbes](../microbiology/muratina-yeast-immobilization.md)

*Evidence boundary:* A carrier can transfer a culture without being the substrate; no collection or culturing instructions are implied.

## No fixed date for a family occasion

[Sagana marriage study](../culture/muratina-and-agikuyu-marriage-negotiation.md) → [Occasion record](../calendar/muratina-and-marriage-occasions.md) → [Calendar limits](../calendar/brewing-time-versus-ceremonial-time.md)

*Evidence boundary:* A local ethnography cannot establish a universally dated Kenyan festival or a required custom.

## Millet, sorghum and the food-beverage boundary

[Bushera](../styles/bushera.md) → [Household practice](../culture/bushera-household-fermentation-in-southwestern-uganda.md) → [Cereal food](../food/bushera-as-fermented-cereal-food.md) → [Chibuku](../styles/chibuku.md)

*Evidence boundary:* Food-drinks and commercial opaque beer differ in classification, methods and reported alcohol.

## Aroma stability is not one chemical number

[Bound aldehydes](../sensory/beer-matrix-bound-aldehydes.md) → [Antioxidant compounds](../ingredients/beer-antioxidants-versus-health-claims.md) → [Packaging](../processes/packaging-and-aging.md)

*Evidence boundary:* Chemical assay endpoints are not universal shelf-life, sensory or health promises.

## The water saved might cost energy

[Italian and Portuguese studies](../environment/brewery-water-reuse-italy-portugal.md) → [Treatment trains](../processes/brewery-water-reclamation-trains.md) → [Czech LCA](../environment/czech-brewery-scale-and-packaging-lca.md)
