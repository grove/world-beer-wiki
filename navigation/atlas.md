# Atlas reading paths

Choose a question, then move between evidence-bearing pages. Links are invitations to compare, not claims of descent.

## Five routes into the atlas

**A culture is not a recipe:** [Vossaøl](../styles/vossaol.md) → [yeast-storage tools](../objects/yeast-storage-tools.md) → [kveik](../ingredients/kveik.md) → [S. cerevisiae](../microbiology/saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md).

**Brewing and cooperation:** [Zoigl](../culture/zoigl-communal-brewing.md) → [Norogachi reciprocal work](../culture/tesguino-and-reciprocity.md) → [ÉPOP exchange](../culture/mising-starter-knowledge.md). Compare institutions without equating their meanings.

**Which part was heated?** [Raw ale](../processes/raw-ale.md) → [baked mash](../processes/baked-mash.md) → [decoction](../processes/decoction.md) → [smoked malt](../ingredients/smoked-malt.md).

**Same adjective, different pathway:** [Straight sour beer](../styles/straight-sour-beer.md) → [kettle souring](../processes/kettle-souring.md) → [Lachancea](../microbiology/lachancea.md) → [microbial succession](../microbiology/microbial-succession.md).

**Time means different things:** [Christmas preparation](../calendar/christmas-brewing-in-hornindal.md) → [cool-season production](../calendar/lambic-brewing-season.md) → [Maibock service](../calendar/maibock-and-spring.md) → [a dated festival edition](../calendar/oktoberfest.md).
