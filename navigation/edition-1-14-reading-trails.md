# Edition 1.14: field to glass

A reading route is an *editorial question*, not a proof of ancestry or causal equivalence. Every stop records source access, provenance, regional scope and limits. Start with one question and follow the sources rather than assuming a modern style name explains everything.

## 1. Who invented Pilsner?

Follow the local 1842 batch from brewing rights and institutional chronology to later styles, without claiming a universal lager invention.

[Pilsen burgher brewing rights](../culture/pilsen-burgher-brewing-rights.md) → [Pilsen 1842 evidence and origin myths](../history/pilsen-1842-evidence-and-origin-myths.md) → [First pilsen batch 1842](../calendar/first-pilsen-batch-1842.md) → [What was new in 1842](../stories/what-was-new-in-1842.md) → [Czech premium pale lager](../styles/czech-premium-pale-lager.md).

## 2. From a hop field to a keg

Read seasonal labor, material equipment, drying and storable ingredient form as separate kinds of evidence.

[English hop harvest and drying season](../calendar/english-hop-harvest-and-drying-season.md) → [Kent hop farms and work](../culture/kent-hop-farms-and-work.md) → [English hop oast](../objects/english-hop-oast.md) → [Hop drying cooling and packing](../processes/hop-drying-cooling-and-packing.md) → [Dried hops and harvest form](../ingredients/dried-hops-and-harvest-form.md).

## 3. Why old hops can still teach us something

Compare isolated protein assays with beer foam and flavor; do not infer that degraded hops improve finished beer.

[Humulinones and aged hops](../ingredients/humulinones-and-aged-hops.md) → [Protein z humulinone foam](../sensory/protein-z-humulinone-foam.md) → [Heat ltp1 and beer foam](../sensory/heat-ltp1-and-beer-foam.md) → [Bitterness is not one number](../sensory/bitterness-is-not-one-number.md).

## 4. Yeast as life and industrial material

Move from live managed cells to studied residual matter and separate laboratory results from deployment.

[Brewing yeast](../ingredients/brewing-yeast.md) → [Industrial lager yeast viability](../microbiology/industrial-lager-yeast-viability.md) → [Industrial lager aeration and filling](../processes/industrial-lager-aeration-and-filling.md) → [Surplus brewers yeast as material](../ingredients/surplus-brewers-yeast-as-material.md) → [Hot trub and spent yeast valorization](../environment/hot-trub-and-spent-yeast-valorization.md).

## 5. What reaches the glass

Link intentional service culture, technical guidance and microbial deposit questions without treating equipment instructions as style definitions.
