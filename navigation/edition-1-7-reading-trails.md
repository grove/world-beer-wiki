# Edition 1.7 — research reading trails

This edition adds a **connected research route** through archaeology, community economies, microorganisms, service objects and brewing science. Following a route does not imply direct ancestry between its examples.

## Research trail 1 — Who brewed for kings?

[The North Abydos royal brewery](../history/abydos-early-royal-brewery.md) → [conical vats and fire legs](../objects/abydos-vats-and-fire-legs.md) → [why excavators reinterpreted the structures](../stories/the-brewery-once-called-a-kiln.md) → [how archaeologists identify beer](../history/how-archaeologists-identify-beer.md). Physical context is stronger than a reconstructed ancient tasting note.

## Research trail 2 — When a brewery is a household

[Women’s work in medieval England](../history/brewsters-household-labor-in-medieval-england.md) → [price and gender regulation](../history/ale-tasters-and-brewster-protests.md) → [Upper West Ghana enterprise survey](../culture/ghana-pito-women-enterprise-survey.md) → [Piura chichera interviews](../culture/piura-chicheras-and-household-sales.md). Compare labor, markets and evidence without asserting one universal gender history.

## Research trail 3 — The signal outside the door

[Piura's household chicha sellers](../culture/piura-chicheras-and-household-sales.md) → [white sales flags](../objects/piura-chicha-white-flags.md) → [local weekly service rhythm](../calendar/piura-chicha-sales-days.md) → [Zoigl communal brewing](../culture/zoigl-communal-brewing.md). Similar signals can arise in unrelated social institutions.

## Research trail 4 — One rice ferment, several microbial roles

[Tapuy](../styles/tapuy-philippine-rice-ferment.md) → [bubod as a starter](../ingredients/bubod-starter.md) → [a Philippine isolate study](../microbiology/bubod-tapuy-isolate-study.md) → [parallel conversion](../processes/parallel-starch-and-alcohol-fermentation.md) → [sake-making heritage](../culture/japanese-koji-sake-heritage-and-apprenticeship.md). Chemistry is comparable; community identity is not interchangeable.

## Research trail 5 — Microbe, package and perception

[Hop-resistance genes](../microbiology/hora-horc-and-hop-resistance-markers.md) → [filling-line microbiology](../microbiology/craft-brewery-filling-line-microbes.md) → [VDKs and maturation](../sensory/vicinal-diketones-as-moving-target.md) → [packaging, reuse and delivery](../environment/beer-packaging-transport-and-return-loops.md). Quality and footprint are different evidence problems.

## Research trail 6 — Sustainability is a conditional claim

[Packaging and return logistics](../environment/beer-packaging-transport-and-return-loops.md) → [spent grain's handling limits](../environment/spent-grain-food-safety-and-circularity.md) → [LCA boundaries](../environment/how-to-compare-brewing-footprints.md) → [food pairing and sensory evidence](../food/cheese-beer-preference-is-not-a-rule.md). Every result needs its own system boundary.
