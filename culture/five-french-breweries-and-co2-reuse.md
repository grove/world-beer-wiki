# Five French craft brewers' CO₂ recovery interviews are not a market census

## A small interview sample exposes adoption barriers

Grand and colleagues' craft CO₂ recovery study included **five semi-structured interviews with French brewery operators** in addition to its review of technologies. The researchers describe interest in reusing fermentation gas, lack of information about smaller-scale capture options and concern over technology cost and reliability. These are responses from particular breweries, not nationally representative attitudes of every brewer or independently observed technology installation.[^v119-craftco2]

The authors propose shared regional infrastructure with different levels of gas recovery and utilization. The proposal is a conceptual response to the interview themes rather than evidence that a consortium was built, gas was certified beverage-grade, or a craft brewery achieved net-zero emissions.

## Technology adoption is also a social decision

An equipment seller's claim about return on investment, a brewer's stated intention and a measured operating case are three distinct forms of evidence. The Brewers Association Energy Sustainability resource lists broader management and reduction topics but its public directory does not show what the French interviewees actually purchased or the source of their financial figures.[^v119-baenergy26]

A historical or cultural article should preserve region, sample size, interview method and the proposal's unimplemented status. For meaningful comparison examine how financing, available gas supply and local market structures shape adoption without speaking on behalf of all brewers.
