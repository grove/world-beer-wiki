# Dan in LoBir social and ritual life

Dan, a beer discussed in Barbara Hagaman’s LoBir ethnography, is presented as food, social exchange and ritual material. The article is especially useful because it makes the social setting part of the subject rather than an anecdote appended to brewing.[^v14-lobi]

## Production and social status

The publisher summary describes women making beer and interactions in which provision and consumption shape the status of participants. It also discusses how particular forms of overconsumption could lower status. Neither point supports a blanket account of all West African grain beers or universal drinking expectations.[^v14-lobi]
