# Sahti in a Finnish UNESCO geopark landscape

UNESCO describes the brewing of sahti as one of the traditions associated with local identity in the Lauhanvuori–Hämeenkangas Global Geopark. That is a useful geographic observation—not a claim that sahti itself is inscribed on UNESCO’s international intangible-heritage list.[^v14-sahti]

## Practice and landscape

The geopark’s heritage narrative places sahti alongside other locally maintained practices. In a wiki, this relationship can help readers understand beer as a community craft rooted in a particular landscape rather than as a globally standardized product.[^v14-sahti]

## Where the evidence stops

UNESCO’s geopark entry does not set universal ingredients, dates or methods for all Finnish sahti. Consult [Sahti](../styles/sahti.md), [raw ale](../processes/raw-ale.md) and [juniper](../ingredients/juniper.md) for technical and regional differences; they are separate lines of evidence.
