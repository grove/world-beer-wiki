# Culture, institutions and shared knowledge

Beer can be an object of hospitality, work, exchange, remembrance or commerce. These are situated accounts, not a claim that alcohol defines membership in any community.

| Case study | Focus |
|---|---|

| [Tesgüino and reciprocal work](tesguino-and-reciprocity.md) | A named Norogachi narrative, invitation and cooperation. |
| [ÉPOP and Mising starter knowledge](mising-starter-knowledge.md) | Women's expertise, plants and exchange in a specific Assam field study. |
| [Trappist work and brewing](trappist-work-and-brewing.md) | Institutional production rules, purpose and the limits of style labels. |
| [Sesotho beer and social occasions](sesotho-social-occasions.md) | Everyday commerce and gatherings, with limits of microbiological evidence. |
