# Five beverages and five communities in southern Assam

A 2024 Frontiers study put five community beverage names side by side: **Zao/Zou (Zeme Naga)**, **Judima (Dimasa Kachari)**, **Zu (Hmar)**, **Hor Alank (Karbi)** and **Haria (Tea tribes cohort)**. The published methods specify **15 samples: three from each group**, selected as community-made products. This is a precise sampling design, not five national archetypes.[^v16-assam]

## Read the names as research-local terms

The same publication varies between “Zao” in the sample list and “Zou” in a comparative table. Preserve that discrepancy rather than quietly treating them as confirmed interchangeable spellings. Similarly, an outsider's generic label “rice beer” must not obscure named preparations or local identities.[^v16-assam]

## What changed after the 2024 survey?

A separate 2026 Assam study sampled **rohi modh, sai mod and jou-bidwi**, linking microbial and metabolite approaches. Its indexed overview is evidence that regional research includes other beverage names; it does **not** imply these three are aliases for the 2024 cohort or that their microbial findings transfer without additional sampling.[^v16-assam-2026]

## Ethics and method

Preserve who was consulted, language and locality when available, distinguish independent community testimony from a researcher's descriptions, and do not derive a community's rituals from an amplicon-sequencing chart. Original sources in local languages are a priority for independent review.
