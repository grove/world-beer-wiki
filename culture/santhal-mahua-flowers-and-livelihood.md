# Mahua flowers in a Santhal livelihood study

The mahua tree appears in food and fermented-beverage economies, but its flower-derived distilled spirit *mahuli* should not be relabeled a cereal beer. A Santhal-focused study from Odisha situates the flower within household use and livelihoods.[^v14-mahua]

## From flower to livelihood

The study indexed by FAO AGRIS reports food, beverage and other uses of mahua, including flower fermentation followed by distillation. It describes a specific part of Similipal and does not document every Santhal practice across India. The taxonomy of the plant should be recorded with the scientific name as given by the source.[^v14-mahua]
