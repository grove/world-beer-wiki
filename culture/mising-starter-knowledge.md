# ÉPOP and Mising starter knowledge

A fermentation starter can be a gift as well as a brewing input. Mili and Sundriyal's Sivasagar study documents **ÉPOP**, used for Mising apong, through village surveys and closer work in two selected villages. Knowledgeable women contributed interviews and observed plant-collection and preparation practices.[^atlas-epop]

## What is being passed on?

The researchers recorded an inventory of 31 plant species across the study, alongside rice-based starter preparation. This is an inventory of reported knowledge, not a single recipe requiring every listed species. Collection, preparation, storage and recognizing suitable material are distinct parts of the expertise.[^atlas-epop]

Sharing starters among families and offering them on festive or family occasions are explicitly documented. The source mentions Ali-Aye-Ligang and weddings among those occasions. It also records small-scale sale of surplus starter. The same material can therefore participate in domestic production, exchange and commerce.[^atlas-epop]

## A useful way to read the evidence

Keep three records separate: **the plant inventory**, **a particular household preparation**, and **the social occasion of exchange**. Combining them into one standardized “ethnic yeast cake” would erase the distinctions the research makes available.

Follow [Apong](../styles/apong.md) for the beverage and its documented variants, and [Mould and mixed starters](../ingredients/mould-and-mixed-starters.md) for biological roles. A starter's name does not by itself identify every microorganism or show that all listed plants contribute enzymes, microbes or flavor in the same way.

## Evidence and stewardship

This page deliberately does not reproduce the source's detailed personal restrictions as universal rules. It also does not adopt its medical or blanket safety language. Future expansion should involve Mising readers and distinguish what makers choose to share publicly from knowledge requiring permission. A local field study is valuable precisely because its setting remains visible.
