# Sorghum beer: makers, markets and grain choices

## It takes more than a brewer

A research review describes dolo production as involving sorghum growers, malt processors, brewers, equipment, fuel and retailers. In Burkina Faso, *dolotières* are identified as important participants in this economy. The named actors and market relationships help explain why the drink has both ceremonial functions and ordinary economic value.[^v13-dolo-review]

## The older economic question

Mahir Saul's study of rural Upper Volta (now Burkina Faso) draws attention to money and grain circulation through women's brewing businesses. It resists treating traditional production as merely noncommercial household folklore. Yet the historical research setting should not be applied as a numerical description of today's entire region.[^v13-women-upper-volta]
