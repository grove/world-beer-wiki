# Tuba, migration and the Torres Strait

Tuba in the Torres Strait is a powerful reminder that a beverage can become locally meaningful through movement and exchange. Brady and McGrath trace how fermentation and distillation practices travelled with people in the nineteenth century.[^v14-tuba]

## Imported method, living identity

The researchers describe exchanges with Pacific Island and Southeast Asian laborers and the adaptation of the drink by Torres Strait Islanders. Calling it simply “ancient Indigenous beer” would erase the very history of mobility that makes it interesting; calling it merely foreign would erase local reinvention.[^v14-tuba]
