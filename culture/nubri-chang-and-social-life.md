# Chang in Nubri: work, ceremony and exchange

## The people behind the beverage

Brown, Sangmo Thakuri and Childs describe learning beverage-making and distilling from a local matriarch in Nubri, Nepal. Their work addresses production, gender, household labor, ceremonial gatherings and systems of exchange. This is not an anonymous example of 'Tibetan culture': it is an ethnographic inquiry with a named region and researchers.[^v13-tibet-nubri]

## Exchange is not simple conviviality

The paper's abstract discusses fermented and distilled drinks in some Tibetan cultural areas as part of labor exchange and social relationships. It also notes that alcohol consumption has been contested. Those two facts belong together: hospitality and communal obligation are not interchangeable with a claim that everyone must drink. Distilled products must not automatically be classified as beer.[^v13-tibet-nubri]
