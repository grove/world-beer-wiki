# Black Is Beautiful: an international brewing collaboration

The Smithsonian interview-record description of Marcus Baskerville’s project and its 2020-era context; no claim to have evaluated all participating breweries.

## A social project, not one recipe

The Smithsonian’s catalog entry for Marcus Baskerville links his career at Weathered Souls Brewing to the Black Is Beautiful collaboration. The archivist describes a project that spread to more than 1,220 participating breweries. The figure is the catalog’s summary at the time of description, not a verified live count of currently active partners.[^v15-smithsonian-baskerville]

## Why a beer collaboration belongs in cultural history

Baskerville’s interview record also covers his perception of racial underrepresentation in beer spaces and efforts to connect with other Black enthusiasts. Brewing participation, fund-raising and visibility can be part of beer culture as much as yeast and hops, but the project’s social purpose should not be reduced to a standardized product definition.[^v15-smithsonian-baskerville]

## Evidence boundary
