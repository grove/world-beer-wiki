# Vienna Lager

**Beer Style** · Europe / Latin America · living

Vienna lager is an amber lager with a dry, toasted-malt character. It offers more bread-crust flavor than a pale lager but should avoid heavy sweetness, strong roast, or a thick finish.[^bjcp-2021-7a]

## Character

A good example combines smooth malt with a supporting herbal or floral bitterness. The color may be coppery, yet the overall impression remains clean and fairly light on its feet.[^bjcp-2021-7a]

## Brewing and context

Vienna-type malt is the central reference ingredient, often used with other pale or lightly toasted malts. Lager fermentation preserves the clear distinction between malt richness and fruity ale fermentation.[^bjcp-2021-7a]

## History and interpretation

The nineteenth-century style is associated with Anton Dreher and the introduction of Vienna lager in 1841. Later brewing in Mexico is part of its international story, but modern Mexican amber lagers are not all identical survivors of Dreher's recipe. Compare specific beers and dated evidence rather than equating a national market category with an unchanged historical style.[^bjcp-2021-7a]
