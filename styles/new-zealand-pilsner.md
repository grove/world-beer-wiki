# New Zealand Pilsner

**Beer Style** · Oceania · contemporary

New Zealand Pilsner is a pale, clean-fermented beer designed to showcase the distinctive aromatic range of New Zealand-type hops. Its smooth, relatively soft finish separates it from a simple imitation of German Pils.[^bjcp-2021-x5]

## Character

Lime, grapefruit, gooseberry, melon, tropical fruit, and grassy notes may appear over a light bread or cracker-like base. Bitterness is clear but should not become abrasive. Malt remains noticeable rather than disappearing completely.[^bjcp-2021-x5]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt and expressive hops are central. The local guideline permits clean lager yeast or a very neutral ale fermentation, so the word “Pilsner” does not by itself settle the yeast used in every example.[^bjcp-2021-x5]

## History and interpretation

The reference developed around modern New Zealand brewing, especially the Emerson's model. Comparisons with Sauvignon Blanc describe possible hop aromas, not grape additions. The style usually has a softer presentation than German Pils and less IPA-like intensity than American IPA, while retaining its own regional hop emphasis.[^bjcp-2021-x5]
