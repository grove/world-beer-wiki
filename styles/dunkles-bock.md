# Dunkles Bock

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Dunkles Bock is a strong, dark German lager centered on deep bread-like malt. Its color and strength should not be mistaken for the coffee-like roast of stout.[^bjcp-2021-6c]

## Character

The flavor can suggest bread crust, toast, and a little caramel, with restrained fruitiness and a smooth warming finish. Bitterness keeps the beer balanced, while obvious hop aroma is usually secondary.[^bjcp-2021-6c]

## Brewing and context

Richly kilned malts supply much of the depth. Lager fermentation helps keep the profile clean even at greater strength. A good example is substantial without being syrupy or tasting sharply alcoholic.[^bjcp-2021-6c]

## History and interpretation

Bock's historical story connects Einbeck with later Munich brewing, but the modern dark-lager category should not be projected unchanged onto every earlier beer bearing a related name. Helles Bock offers a paler interpretation, and Doppelbock pushes the malt intensity and strength further. Those names describe overlapping brewing families, not a mathematical formula for multiplying ingredients or alcohol.[^bjcp-2021-6c]
