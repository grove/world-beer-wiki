# Double IPA

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

Double IPA intensifies hop aroma, flavor, bitterness, and strength while aiming to remain relatively dry and drinkable. “Double” does not mean that every ingredient or the alcohol content has literally been doubled.[^bjcp-2021-22a]

## Character

Citrus, pine, resin, tropical fruit, or other hop characters dominate. Malt provides support, but heavy caramel and a thick, sugary finish would move the impression toward American Barleywine. Alcohol may be noticeable without becoming hot.[^bjcp-2021-22a]

## Brewing and context

A strong wort is fermented sufficiently dry to keep the beer focused. Brewers may use sugars to achieve strength without excessive body. Hops remain the central flavor rather than merely balancing a very rich malt base.[^bjcp-2021-22a]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern American craft style whose interpretation continues to vary. The label “imperial IPA” is commonly used for the same family, not an official guarantee of a fixed strength. Strong Hazy IPA can share the strength but has a different sensory emphasis. Judge freshness and balance, not numerical strength alone.[^bjcp-2021-22a]
