# Koduõlu

**Beer Tradition** · Northern Europe / Estonia · living tradition

Koduõlu means home beer. This entry focuses on the farmhouse tradition documented on Saaremaa, Estonia, rather than claiming that every Estonian homebrewed beer belongs to one uniform style.[^garshol-estonia]

## Character

The recorded example was gently sweet, lightly acidic, fruity, and strongly grain-like, with a soft texture. These are the researcher's tasting observations of one brewer's beer, not a national flavor standard.[^garshol-estonia]

## Brewing and context

Brewer Meelis Sepp used his own barley malt, hops, juniper branches, and baker's yeast. The mash was heated, but the resulting wort was not boiled. Juniper branches and hops were placed in the filter, distinguishing their use from a modern kettle addition.[^garshol-estonia]

## History and interpretation

The account describes a move from maintained household yeast to purchased yeast, showing that traditional practice can change. The brewer's estimate of alcohol strength was not a measurement, so this wiki does not turn it into a numerical style range. Saaremaa's example also should not stand for every Estonian island or household.[^garshol-estonia]
