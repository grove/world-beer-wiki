# Flanders Red Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Flanders Red Ale is a Belgian sour-beer family combining reddish-brown malt character, acidity, fruit-like complexity, and often wood-influenced maturation. It can seem wine-like without being grape wine.[^bjcp-2021-23b]

## Character

Tart cherry, plum, red fruit, caramel, and oak-like notes may appear. Those fruit impressions do not prove that fruit was added. A successful example balances acidity and malt rather than tasting merely sharp or vinegary.[^bjcp-2021-23b]

## Brewing and context

Mixed fermentation and maturation are important to the traditional profile. Blending younger and older beer can shape acidity, sweetness, and complexity. The exact process differs among producers, so one brewery's method should not be treated as universal.[^bjcp-2021-23b]

## History and interpretation

The style is associated especially with western Flanders. It should be distinguished from Oud Bruin, which generally has a different malt-and-acid balance, and from fruit lambic, whose production tradition is different. “Red” describes a family resemblance, not a guarantee of a fixed color, recipe, or amount of time in wood.[^bjcp-2021-23b]
