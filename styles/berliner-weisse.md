# Berliner Weisse

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Berliner Weisse is a pale, low-strength, tart wheat beer associated with Berlin. Its appeal is a lively acidity and light grain character rather than hop bitterness or heavy malt sweetness.[^bjcp-2021-23a]

## Character

The flavor can suggest fresh bread, lemon-like tartness, and gentle fruit. Carbonation is usually brisk. Some interpretations have additional fermentation complexity, but an aggressively funky or vinegary profile is not the default description.[^bjcp-2021-23a]

## Brewing and context

Wheat and barley provide the grain base, while lactic fermentation supplies the characteristic sourness. Production methods differ among traditional, revived, and modern quick-soured versions. Matching acidity alone does not demonstrate that two beers were made by the same method.[^bjcp-2021-23a]

## History and interpretation

Flavored syrups are a familiar serving option, not an essential ingredient of the underlying beer. Modern strongly fruited sour beers may borrow the name or inspiration while differing considerably. Keep the historical Berlin tradition distinct from the broader contemporary practice of making pale, low-strength kettle-soured beers.[^bjcp-2021-23a]
