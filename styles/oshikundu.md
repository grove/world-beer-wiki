# Oshikundu

**Related Fermented-Grain Tradition** · Southern Africa / Namibia · living tradition

Oshikundu is a Namibian fermented cereal beverage made from pearl millet, malted sorghum, millet bran, and water. It is generally described as low-alcohol or non-alcoholic, so this wiki treats it as a neighboring grain-fermentation tradition rather than simply another alcoholic beer style.[^oshikundu-research]

## Character

Its cereal material and acid fermentation are central to its identity. The cited thesis abstract records increasing lactic acid during fermentation and storage, but does not provide a complete regional sensory survey. A detailed universal flavor specification would exceed that evidence.[^oshikundu-research]

## Brewing and context

Traditional fermentation relies on the microorganisms already associated with the ingredients and process. The thesis tested controlled mashing and selected bacterial and brewer's yeast cultures as alternatives, comparing these experimental methods with a traditional preparation.[^oshikundu-research]

## History and interpretation

Those experimental cultures must not be presented as compulsory traditional ingredients. Likewise, “low to non-alcoholic” is a general description, not a verified alcohol value for every batch. This profile uses the public thesis abstract; the full thesis was not available for examination.[^oshikundu-research]
