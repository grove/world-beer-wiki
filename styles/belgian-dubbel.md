# Belgian Dubbel

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Dubbel is a reddish-brown Belgian ale with rich malt, dark-fruit impressions, and a comparatively gentle bitter finish. It is flavorful and warming without the heavy roast of stout.[^bjcp-2021-26b]

## Character

The beer can suggest bread crust, caramel, raisins, plums, or a little spice. Fruit-like flavors often come from fermentation and ingredients rather than added fruit. The finish should be rounded but not sticky.[^bjcp-2021-26b]

## Brewing and context

Malt, brewing sugars, and expressive yeast can create considerable depth. The dark color does not require a large roasted-grain contribution. Carbonation helps keep the richness from feeling overly dense.[^bjcp-2021-26b]

## History and interpretation

Modern Dubbel has strong monastic brewing associations, but neither the name nor an abbey-themed label proves who brewed a beer. “Double” is not a reliable instruction to ferment the beer twice or double every ingredient. Compare Dubbel with stronger Belgian Dark Strong Ale and with sour Oud Bruin: dark color alone does not make those three families equivalent.[^bjcp-2021-26b]
