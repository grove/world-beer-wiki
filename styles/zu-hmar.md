# Zu — Hmar rice-based ferment in southern Assam

A 2024 study identifies **Zu** as a Hmar rice-based fermented beverage in southern Assam, using a locally named starter called *Chol*. It is one of five beverages investigated by Yumnam and colleagues, who deliberately selected community-produced samples rather than commercial market stock.[^v16-assam]

## Time and method

The study's comparative table gives a short fermentation window for Zu and notes that seasons can affect duration. That is a process observation, **not a fixed festival date** or proof that every household follows the same schedule. The investigators documented local descriptions and collected three samples from the Hmar cohort, too few to define a universal regional composition.[^v16-assam]

## Why the local name matters

Translating every rice ferment to “rice beer” can make the shared biochemical logic visible, yet it erases details if used as a replacement for Zu. Names, practices and associated communities should remain findable in their own right. Compare with Karbi Hor Alank, Dimasa Judima, and the broader starch-starter reference.

## Evidence boundary

The study's microbial sequencing assesses a limited selection of samples; it cannot identify every active organism throughout fermentation or assess all Hmar community practices. This is a research reference, not an official style definition.
