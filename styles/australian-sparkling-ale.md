# Australian Sparkling Ale

**Beer Style** · Oceania · living

Australian sparkling ale is a distinctive, highly carbonated ale with a dry finish and a fruity yeast character. It is a specific brewing tradition, not a generic name for every Australian pale ale.[^bjcp-2021-12b]

## Character

The flavor can combine grainy malt with pear- or apple-like fruitiness and firm bitterness. The lively bubbles are central to the experience. Depending on how the beer is poured, yeast sediment may make it cloudy and change its texture.[^bjcp-2021-12b]

## Brewing and context

A pale malt base, Australian brewing traditions, and bottle conditioning are important reference points. The fermentation character is more evident than in a neutral international lager, but strong banana-and-clove wheat-beer flavors are not the defining target.[^bjcp-2021-12b]

## History and interpretation

Historical continuity and modern production should be considered together: a long-established product can still change over time. “Sparkling” describes an important characteristic, not the use of wine or a single carbonation technology. Record whether an example is poured clear or with its sediment when comparing appearance and mouthfeel.[^bjcp-2021-12b]
