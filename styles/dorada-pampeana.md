# Dorada Pampeana

**Beer Style** · South America · contemporary

Dorada Pampeana is an Argentine golden-ale interpretation with a soft malt emphasis and restrained hops. Its name and local history deserve to be preserved rather than replaced with a generic international blonde-ale label.[^bjcp-2021-x1]

## Character

The beer can taste lightly grainy or bready, with gentle fruitiness and modest hop flavor. Caramel is normally limited. A smooth body and an uncomplicated, refreshing balance matter more than intensity.[^bjcp-2021-x1]

## Brewing and context

Pale or Pilsner malt, commonly Cascade hops, and a relatively clean or lightly fruity yeast form the reference. Cold conditioning can help produce a polished finish. This is not a strongly hopped pale ale merely because Cascade is used.[^bjcp-2021-x1]

## History and interpretation

The local guideline connects its development with the restricted ingredient choices available to early Argentine homebrewers. That is a particular modern brewing context, not a claim to represent all beer historically made on the Pampas. Compare it with American Blonde Ale while retaining the Argentine name and locally documented identity.[^bjcp-2021-x1]
