# Wee Heavy

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Wee Heavy is a rich Scottish strong-ale interpretation with deep malt flavor, a full body, and warming alcohol. Its appeal is the combination of bread, toffee, caramel, and sometimes dried-fruit notes.[^bjcp-2021-17c]

## Character

Hop bitterness is usually restrained relative to the malt, but the finish should not be a sticky mass of sugar. A small roasted-grain edge can add balance. Peat smoke is not a defining traditional requirement.[^bjcp-2021-17c]

## Brewing and context

A concentrated malt base and fermentation character create much of the richness. Recipes can be less complicated than the flavor suggests; strength and process matter as well as the number of specialty malts.[^bjcp-2021-17c]

## History and interpretation

The phrase “wee heavy” has a particular commercial and historical background and is now used more broadly. It should not be read as a precise formula for strength. The style is distinct from ordinary Scottish Light, Heavy, and Export ales, and it should not be described as beer made to taste like Scotch whisky merely because of the national association.[^bjcp-2021-17c]
