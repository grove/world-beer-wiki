# American Amber Ale

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

American Amber Ale combines an amber-to-copper color and caramel-like malt with recognizable American hop character. The relationship between those two elements matters more than a particular shade of red.[^bjcp-2021-19a]

## Character

Flavors can include toast, toffee, citrus, and pine. Some examples lean toward rounded malt; others finish distinctly bitter. The body is generally substantial enough to support the hops without becoming syrupy.[^bjcp-2021-19a]

## Brewing and context

A pale-malt foundation with carefully chosen darker malts provides the color and caramel impression. Hops add aroma and balance. Clean fermentation is typical, while pronounced roast would push the beer toward a different family.[^bjcp-2021-19a]

## History and interpretation

“Red ale” is not a universal synonym across countries. Irish Red Ale usually has a different balance, and Red IPA moves further toward IPA-level hop intensity and dryness. The American amber category embraces a range of local craft interpretations rather than preserving one fixed historical recipe. Color alone is therefore a poor identification test.[^bjcp-2021-19a]
