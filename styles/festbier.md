# Festbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

Festbier is the smooth, golden, moderately strong lager associated with the modern Munich festival-beer interpretation. It combines a fuller malt flavor than everyday Helles with a finish that remains easy to drink.[^bjcp-2021-4b]

## Character

The flavor suggests fresh bread and lightly toasted grain rather than dark caramel. Hop bitterness supports the malt without dominating, and the fermentation character is clean. Its body is substantial but should not feel heavy or sticky.[^bjcp-2021-4b]

## Brewing and context

Pale lager malt, sometimes supported by richer toasted malts, creates the balance. It is brewed as a lager, not as an ale whose festival name happens to imply German ancestry.[^bjcp-2021-4b]

## History and interpretation

Modern golden Festbier and amber Märzen are related points of comparison, not synonyms. Outside Germany, “Oktoberfest” can appear on either kind of beer, so the label alone may not predict the color or flavor. This entry describes a sensory style; it does not attempt to determine which breweries or products may use protected festival-related names.[^bjcp-2021-4b]
