# Italian Grape Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

Italian Grape Ale brings grapes or grape must into a beer whose grain-based character remains part of the final drink. It is a varied family rather than a fixed blend ratio of finished beer and wine.[^bjcp-2021-x3]

## Character

The grape variety can contribute recognizable fruit, wine-like aroma, color, and gentle acidity. Malt and hops are usually restrained enough to leave the grapes clear. Some examples are richer or stronger than others.[^bjcp-2021-x3]

## Brewing and context

Grapes or must can enter at different stages, and brewers use different fermentation cultures and maturation approaches. Wild or strongly sour versions are meaningful variants, not the default description of every IGA.[^bjcp-2021-x3]

## History and interpretation

The modern Italian tradition connects craft brewing with regional grape diversity. Its local identity should be distinguished from the wider international Grape Ale category. Neither the presence of grapes nor a wine-like flavor proves a particular legal classification, fermentation method, or geographical origin. Record the grape, producer, and process when comparing individual examples.[^bjcp-2021-x3]
