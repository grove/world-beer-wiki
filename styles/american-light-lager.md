# American Light Lager

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

American light lager is a very pale, lightly flavored beer designed around a clean finish and a light body. Its appeal is restraint: it is not intended to deliver the strong malt or hop flavors of many other beer styles.[^bjcp-2021-1a]

## Character

Expect lively bubbles, a faint grain flavor, little bitterness, and almost no fruity yeast character. Rice or maize may accompany barley malt. The result should taste clean rather than sweet or strongly corny.[^bjcp-2021-1a]

## Brewing and context

This is a modern American commercial style, not simply a smaller serving of ordinary lager. Its identity includes reduced body and intensity as well as relatively modest alcohol. The category is different from German Leichtbier, which can retain a more noticeable hop character.[^bjcp-2021-1a]

## History and interpretation

“Light” is a product description whose exact meaning depends on the beer and market. It does not mean alcohol-free. This article describes the BJCP reference style, not nutritional or labeling rules for individual products.[^bjcp-2021-1a]
