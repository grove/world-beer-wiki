# Witbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Witbier is a pale Belgian wheat beer with a soft grain character, lively carbonation, and a gentle combination of citrus and spice. Its cloudiness is familiar, but the whole flavor balance defines the style.[^bjcp-2021-24a]

## Character

The beer can suggest fresh bread, orange peel, coriander, and light fermentation fruitiness. Bitterness stays restrained. Spice should support the wheat and yeast rather than make the drink taste like a strongly seasoned infusion.[^bjcp-2021-24a]

## Brewing and context

Wheat, often unmalted, joins barley malt; oats may also appear. Orange peel and coriander are common, while yeast supplies additional character. The modern reference does not require a pronounced sour taste.[^bjcp-2021-24a]

## History and interpretation

The tradition has both historical roots and a modern revival, so a present-day commercial example should not be assumed to reproduce every earlier white beer. German Weissbier and American Wheat Beer use wheat too, but emphasize different fermentation and seasoning profiles. “White beer” is therefore a context-dependent name rather than a universal recipe.[^bjcp-2021-24a]
