# White IPA

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

White IPA combines an IPA's bitterness and strength with the wheat, fruit, and spice associations of Witbier. It is usually pale and somewhat hazy, but its identity is not simply “cloudy IPA.”[^bjcp-2021-21b-white]

## Character

Citrus, apricot-like fruit, light bread, and gentle coriander, pepper, or clove impressions may appear. The spice can come from fermentation, actual additions, or both. The finish should remain lively and fairly dry.[^bjcp-2021-21b-white]

## Brewing and context

Pale and wheat malts, expressive yeast, and citrus-oriented hops are common. Orange peel or coriander may be used, but neither must overwhelm the beer. Hopping needs to work with the fruit-and-spice profile rather than erase it.[^bjcp-2021-21b-white]

## History and interpretation

This is a contemporary hybrid rather than a historical synonym for all Belgian white beers. Hazy IPA can look similar but normally has a different fermentation and spice emphasis. Comparing the aroma, bitterness, and yeast character is more informative than comparing photographs of the two beers.[^bjcp-2021-21b-white]
