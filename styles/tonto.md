# Tonto — fermented banana drink in Uganda

Tonto is a traditional Ugandan alcoholic beverage made through fermentation of banana juice. The 2026 study by Atwine and colleagues sampled **42 preparations from Ntungamo and Kalungu districts**. Its regional sampling is more informative than a single standardized recipe or flavor chart.[^v16-tonto]

## Fermenting fruit, not simply a banana-flavored ale

Ripe bananas provide sugars that ferment differently from a starch-based barley mash. A wider review of African fruit fermentations describes banana-beer families that may involve roasted sorghum or millet, but that comparison **does not mean each Tonto preparation uses the same cereal or starter**. Do not project ingredients from a review of several countries onto individual Ugandan batches.[^v16-banana-review]

## What the study actually measured

Across the studied fermentation, mean acidity rose as soluble solids fell, and the team detected **38 volatile compounds**, predominantly esters and alcohols. Fruit and banana impressions were more prominent among sampled Ntungamo preparations. The authors also reported substantial variation in methanol levels, with many samples above the national limit cited by the study. This is a reason to take sample-specific safety evidence seriously, not a basis to stigmatize a whole living tradition or certify other batches safe.[^v16-tonto]

## Evidence boundary

A short experiment in two districts cannot establish a national flavor standard, an ancient invention date, or a single obligatory microbial ecology. Compare the distinct Rwandan drink and read about the **same aroma words arising from yeast even when no banana fruit is used**.
