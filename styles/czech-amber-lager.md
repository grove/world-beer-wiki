# Czech Amber Lager

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Czech amber lager sits between the pale and dark parts of the Czech lager family. Its local description, polotmavé, is commonly understood as “half-dark,” but the flavor can vary considerably.[^bjcp-2021-3c]

## Character

Some examples emphasize toasted bread and a dry, hoppy finish. Others have more caramel and a rounder malt impression. The shared feature is a clean lager background supporting richer malt than in the pale styles.[^bjcp-2021-3c]

## Brewing and context

Pale and more deeply colored malts provide the color and flavor, while Czech-type hops give bitterness and sometimes an herbal or spicy aroma. The beer need not be sweet simply because it is amber.[^bjcp-2021-3c]

## History and interpretation

The category has connections and similarities to Vienna-style amber lager, but modern Czech examples are not all interchangeable with Vienna lager. Local brewery practice matters. When comparing beers, record the producer, strength, and malt-hop balance rather than assuming that the word polotmavé identifies a single fixed recipe across the country.[^bjcp-2021-3c]
