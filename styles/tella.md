# Tella

**Beer Tradition** · East Africa / Ethiopia · living tradition

Tella is an Ethiopian grain beer associated with gesho, the plant Rhamnus prinoides. Gesho may play a role comparable to a brewing herb, but it is not the hop plant used in most modern commercial beer.[^tella-research]

## Character

Grain preparation, herbs, and mixed fermentation shape its character. Tella can be cloudy, and both acidity and alcohol develop during fermentation. The cited study explicitly recognizes differences in ingredients and methods among Ethiopian communities.[^tella-research]

## Brewing and context

The documented preparations used barley, barley malt, and gesho. Some of the grain was roasted and prepared as flatbread or cooked cakes before joining staged fermentations. These preparations show why describing tella as ordinary barley wort with a substitute bittering ingredient is incomplete.[^tella-research]

## History and interpretation

The research also experimented with replacing gesho with moringa. That was an innovation being tested, not the definition of traditional tella. Names such as Farsoo and Siwa occur in related Ethiopian linguistic and regional contexts; this entry does not assume that every local expression is identical. No fixed nationwide recipe or health benefit is claimed.[^tella-research]
