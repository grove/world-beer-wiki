# Foreign Extra Stout

**Beer Style** · Europe / Africa / Asia / Caribbean · living

Foreign Extra Stout is a strong, deeply roasted stout family with a dry to moderately rounded finish. It generally has more power and intensity than ordinary Irish draught stout.[^bjcp-2021-16d]

## Character

Coffee, dark chocolate, burnt grain, and sometimes dark-fruit notes form the main flavor picture. Bitterness is substantial enough to support the roast and strength, but the beer should not become harsh or sharply alcoholic.[^bjcp-2021-16d]

## Brewing and context

A concentrated malt base, roasted grains, and a firm hop contribution provide the modern reference framework. Individual breweries and markets use different recipes and fermentation practices.[^bjcp-2021-16d]

## History and interpretation

The name reflects the history of stronger or more heavily hopped stout intended for distant markets, but historical export beer was not simply today's recipe with extra alcohol. Storage and conditioning also mattered. Distinguish this relatively dry reference style from the sweeter Tropical Stout interpretation, while recognizing that real commercial products do not always respect a neat competition boundary between the two.[^bjcp-2021-16d]
