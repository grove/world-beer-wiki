# Czech Dark Lager

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Czech dark lager is a family of dark, cleanly fermented beers in which bread, caramel, chocolate, and roast can appear in different proportions. It is not simply Czech pale lager with a black appearance.[^bjcp-2021-3d]

## Character

Some versions are soft and malt-led; others finish more bitter or show a clearer roasted edge. Even dark examples can remain surprisingly easy to drink. Strong burnt flavors should not overwhelm the balance.[^bjcp-2021-3d]

## Brewing and context

Brewers combine pale malt with darker malts and use lager fermentation. Hops provide balance, but the precise malt profile varies between breweries and between strength classes.[^bjcp-2021-3d]

## History and interpretation

The Czech word tmavé means dark, while local labels can communicate additional information about the beer's original wort strength. Long-established breweries are important to the tradition, but an old brewery foundation date does not prove that its present dark lager recipe is equally old. The modern style should be compared with dated brewing evidence before making claims about medieval continuity.[^bjcp-2021-3d]
