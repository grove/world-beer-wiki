# Sato

**Related Fermented-Grain Tradition** · Southeast Asia / Thailand · living tradition

Sato is a Thai fermented rice beverage, usually described as rice wine. Its place here is as a related starch-fermentation tradition with its own identity, not as an imported lager style made with rice.[^sato-process]

## Character

The cited technical account emphasizes variation in product quality rather than a fixed tasting description. Rice, starter performance, and fermentation conditions help determine the result. A single nationwide sweetness or alcohol specification would exceed the evidence used in this profile.[^sato-process]

## Brewing and context

Glutinous rice and a traditional starter called lookpang are central ingredients. The starter supports conversion of rice starch and fermentation. The technical discussion distinguishes customary production from improvements intended to make larger-scale production more consistent.[^sato-process]

## History and interpretation

Suggested process improvements are not evidence that every household has always brewed that way. Sato should also not be used as a generic name for all Thai alcoholic drinks. This entry concerns the undistilled rice-fermentation tradition described in the source, with its local identity retained.[^sato-process]
