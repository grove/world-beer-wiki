# Busaa

**Beer Tradition** · East Africa / Kenya · living tradition

Busaa is a Kenyan opaque maize beer. It belongs to a grain-fermentation tradition quite different from a filtered industrial lager, even though maize can also appear in modern lager recipes.[^busaa-nout]

## Character

Its cereal base and suspended material are important to its identity. The evidence assembled here is stronger on production and microbiology than on tasting descriptors, so this entry does not invent a precise hop aroma, color range, or national alcohol standard.[^busaa-nout]

## Brewing and context

Research in western Kenya examined busaa alongside earlier maize-processing materials. It describes mkarango, a roasted fermented maize preparation that can be used in making busaa, and documents lactic acid bacteria and yeasts in the wider process.[^busaa-research]

## History and interpretation

The laboratory study sampled particular products and locations; it is not a census of all Kenyan brewing. A modern controlled starter culture would be an adaptation, not necessarily a complete reproduction of household practice. Busaa also should not be treated as an alias for every East African cereal beer.[^busaa-research]
