# English Porter

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Modern English porter is a dark ale with a soft chocolate, toast, and caramel-like malt character. It usually emphasizes rounded dark-malt flavor rather than the sharper roast associated with some stouts.[^bjcp-2021-13c]

## Character

The beer can show gentle fruitiness, moderate bitterness, and a fairly dry to lightly sweet finish. It should feel satisfying without requiring the high strength or thick body of an imperial beer.[^bjcp-2021-13c]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt is combined with darker malts, which may include brown and chocolate malt. Different recipes can reach similar flavor balances, so the presence or absence of one grain is not a dependable universal border between porter and stout.[^bjcp-2021-13c]

## History and interpretation

This entry describes a modern style reference. Historical London porter changed substantially in ingredients, strength, storage, and blending, and deserves a separate article. A present-day porter should not be presented as a direct sensory replica of an eighteenth-century beer without documentary support. “Porter” is a historical family name with several regional and modern interpretations.[^bjcp-2021-13c]
