# International Dark Lager

**Beer Style** · Worldwide · contemporary

International dark lager combines a clean lager profile with dark color and gentle malt richness. It is usually easier and lighter on the palate than its appearance might suggest.[^bjcp-2021-2c]

## Character

The flavor may include light caramel, toasted grain, or a little chocolate. Strong burnt bitterness is not the goal. Hopping is generally restrained, and the body is lighter than that of many strong dark ales.[^bjcp-2021-2c]

## Brewing and context

Dark malts or coloring ingredients can deepen the appearance without making the entire grain bill heavily roasted. Adjunct grains or sugars are possible. The category therefore cannot be identified simply by assuming that darker beer contains more malt or more alcohol.[^bjcp-2021-2c]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern international grouping with varied national expressions. It differs from Munich Dunkel's fuller toasted-malt emphasis and from Schwarzbier's more distinct dark-roast edge. Those comparisons are descriptive, not a claim that all dark lagers share one historical recipe or a single route of descent.[^bjcp-2021-2c]

## Evidence note

This profile paraphrases a modern judging reference. Its flavor description is better supported than any claim of a single origin or an unchanged historical recipe. It is not a complete brewing recipe.
