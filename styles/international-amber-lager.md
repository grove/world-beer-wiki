# International Amber Lager

**Beer Style** · Worldwide · contemporary

International amber lager is a clean lager with more toast or caramel character than the pale version. It is a broad modern grouping rather than one tightly bounded regional tradition.[^bjcp-2021-2b]

## Character

Expect an amber to copper color, moderate malt flavor, restrained fruitiness, and enough bitterness to prevent a sugary finish. The malt may suggest toasted bread or light caramel, but the beer should not have the burnt intensity of a stout.[^bjcp-2021-2b]

## Brewing and context

Brewers can use combinations of pale and darker malts, sometimes with adjunct grains or sugars. The exact recipe is less important than the balance between smooth lager fermentation, moderate body, and gently richer malt.[^bjcp-2021-2b]

## History and interpretation

Some examples resemble Vienna lager, but resemblance does not establish that they are unchanged descendants of a nineteenth-century Viennese recipe. International amber lager is useful when a beer fits this broad commercial pattern without matching a narrower tradition. Color alone cannot distinguish it from an amber ale: fermentation character and flavor balance also matter.[^bjcp-2021-2b]
