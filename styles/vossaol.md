# Vossaøl

**Beer Tradition** · Northern Europe / Norway · living tradition

Vossaøl is farmhouse beer associated with Voss in western Norway. A firsthand account of brewing with the Gjernes family documents juniper, malt, hops, and a family-kept yeast culture known as kveik.[^garshol-voss]

## Character

The observed beer had striking orange-peel and spice aromas even though orange peel was not an ingredient. The researcher associated those aromas with the yeast. Juniper contributed a separate herbal, woody character. These observations describe one brewing lineage rather than every beer made around Voss.[^garshol-voss]

## Brewing and context

The documented process used hot juniper infusion for mashing, juniper branches in the filter, and a lengthy wort boil. The family maintained its fermentation culture between brewing sessions rather than buying a fresh packet for each batch.[^garshol-voss]

## History and interpretation

Kveik names a yeast tradition, not the beer style itself. A modern IPA fermented with a commercial Voss yeast isolate is not automatically Vossaøl. Conversely, a family culture and its surrounding brewing practice should not be reduced to the flavor of one isolated yeast strain.[^garshol-voss]
