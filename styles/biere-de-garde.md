# Bière de Garde

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Bière de Garde is a northern French family of relatively strong, malt-focused beers matured for a smooth finish. Blonde, amber, and brown versions belong to the tradition.[^bjcp-2021-24c]

## Character

Paler examples can suggest bread, grain, and gentle sweetness; darker ones add toast or caramel. Despite the malt richness, the finish should be fairly dry and clean. Hops are usually supporting rather than dominant.[^bjcp-2021-24c]

## Brewing and context

Malt choice shapes the color and flavor, while fermentation and conditioning contribute to smoothness. The name points toward keeping or maturation, not a requirement that every example use an identical yeast or storage method.[^bjcp-2021-24c]

## History and interpretation

A musty cellar aroma is not an essential mark of authenticity, and neither sourness nor wild fermentation defines the modern reference. Bière de Garde and Saison share a broad farmhouse association but have different sensory emphases: the former is usually malt-led, the latter drier and more yeast-spicy. Treat farmhouse labels as historical context, not as a single international flavor.[^bjcp-2021-24c]
