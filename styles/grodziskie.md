# Grodziskie

**Beer Style** · Europe · revived

Grodziskie is a Polish beer tradition combining low strength, oak-smoked wheat malt, noticeable bitterness, and lively carbonation. Its pale appearance contrasts with a surprisingly distinctive smoke aroma.[^bjcp-2021-27e]

## Character

The beer can suggest fresh grain, gentle wood smoke, and herbal hops. It is usually light-bodied and dry, with a brisk, sparkling impression. Strong sweetness or deliberate sourness is not central to the reference style.[^bjcp-2021-27e]

## Brewing and context

Oak-smoked wheat malt is the defining grain ingredient. Despite the wheat, the familiar reference is comparatively clear rather than thick and cloudy. Fermentation should leave smoke, grain, and hops recognizable without a strong banana-and-clove profile.[^bjcp-2021-27e]

## History and interpretation

The German name Grätzer reflects the same town-linked tradition, not a separate beer that needs another file. Historical discontinuity and modern revival make dates and producer context important. Lichtenhainer offers a useful smoke comparison but adds intentional acidity, while Rauchbier usually means a maltier smoked lager rather than this light wheat beer.[^bjcp-2021-27e]
