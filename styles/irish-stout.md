# Irish Stout

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Irish stout is a dark, roast-forward beer whose familiar flavors suggest coffee, dark chocolate, and toasted grain. Its black appearance does not mean it must be unusually strong or heavy.[^bjcp-2021-15b]

## Character

The balance is typically dry and bitter enough to offset the roast. Some examples feel creamy, especially when served with nitrogen, but nitrogen dispense is a serving choice rather than a defining ingredient in all stout.[^bjcp-2021-15b]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt is combined with roasted grains, often including unmalted roasted barley. The exact recipe varies among breweries. The roast should be clear without becoming harsh or sour.[^bjcp-2021-15b]

## History and interpretation

Modern draught Irish stout is one expression within a larger historical stout family. Bottled beers, stronger Irish extra stouts, and export stouts can have different strength and texture. A creamy tap presentation should therefore not be used as the only standard for identifying stout. Nor is the presence of roasted barley a universal historical rule separating every stout from every porter.[^bjcp-2021-15b]
