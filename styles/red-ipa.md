# Red IPA

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

Red IPA combines the hop-led balance of American IPA with the caramel, toast, and reddish color associated with American Amber Ale. It should not become so sweet or heavy that the IPA character disappears.[^bjcp-2021-21b-red]

## Character

Toffee, bread crust, or light dark-fruit notes support citrus, pine, tropical fruit, or other hop flavors. Bitterness is prominent, and the finish stays relatively dry. The malt adds depth rather than taking over.[^bjcp-2021-21b-red]

## Brewing and context

Caramel and lightly toasty malts modify an IPA-like base. Brewers need to balance those grains against the hop selection and fermentation profile. Color alone is not enough: a red beer with little hop expression is not automatically Red IPA.[^bjcp-2021-21b-red]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern American craft category rather than a traditional Irish red beer. It sits between several neighboring descriptions, especially American Amber Ale and American Strong Ale. Compare the beer's strength, body, sweetness, and hop intensity instead of relying on a brewery's use of the word “red.”[^bjcp-2021-21b-red]
