# Sesotho beer

**Beer Tradition** · Southern Africa / Lesotho · living tradition

Sesotho beer is a traditional cereal drink of Lesotho. A study of five brewing locations describes a cloudy, relatively thin beer with a distinctly sour taste, made for everyday small-scale sale and for important gatherings.[^sesotho-research]

## Character

Its grain base can be maize, sorghum, wheat, or a mixture, depending on availability and brewer preference. A national recipe with one compulsory grain ratio would therefore misrepresent the documented practice.[^sesotho-research]

## Brewing and context

The observed process uses material retained from an earlier successful fermentation, heating and cooking stages, and later additions including sorghum malt. The beer is sieved before drinking. The study found both lactic acid bacteria and yeasts involved as the fermentation developed.[^sesotho-research]

## History and interpretation

The research sampled several districts rather than one household, giving useful evidence of shared patterns and local differences. It does not establish an unchanged ancient recipe. “Sesotho” also names a language and cultural context; this file uses the term specifically for the fermented beverage investigated in the cited study.[^sesotho-research]

The description is limited to the sources below. A local observation or laboratory sample does not establish one recipe, strength, or flavor for every community or period.
