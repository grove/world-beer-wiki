# Czech Pale Lager

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Czech pale lager is the lower-strength member of the Czech pale-lager family. It offers real bread-like malt and noticeable hops without the weight of the stronger beers often exported as Czech pilsner.[^bjcp-2021-3a]

## Character

The best way to understand it is as a complete beer at a smaller scale, not a watered-down premium lager. It is golden, well carbonated, and often has a rounded malt texture followed by a firm, refreshing bitterness.[^bjcp-2021-3a]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt, Czech-type hops, lager yeast, and brewing methods that preserve a full malt impression shape the style. The balance varies among breweries: some examples lean toward malt, others toward hops.[^bjcp-2021-3a]

## History and interpretation

The Czech term výčepní belongs to a local system that classifies beers partly by the strength of the wort before fermentation. It should not be translated as a universal promise about alcohol percentage. This entry describes the pale beer in that tradition, rather than all Czech draught beer.[^bjcp-2021-3a]
