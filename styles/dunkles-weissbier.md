# Dunkles Weissbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Dunkles Weissbier is the darker member of the German wheat-beer family. It keeps the banana-and-clove fermentation character while adding toasted bread and richer malt flavors.[^bjcp-2021-10b]

## Character

The appearance ranges through copper and brown, usually with a soft haze and a generous head. Expect a rounded wheat texture, lively carbonation, and restrained hop bitterness. Strong coffee-like roast would pull it away from the usual style.[^bjcp-2021-10b]

## Brewing and context

Wheat malt is combined with more deeply colored barley malts and fermented with a German wheat-beer yeast. The darker malt should support, not erase, the fruit-and-spice character.[^bjcp-2021-10b]

## History and interpretation

“Dunkel” means dark, but Dunkles Weissbier is not Munich Dunkel with a little wheat added. The fermentation profile is fundamentally different. Likewise, a dark color does not automatically make it stronger than pale Weissbier. Weizenbock is the more appropriate comparison when substantial extra strength and malt intensity are central to the beer.[^bjcp-2021-10b]
