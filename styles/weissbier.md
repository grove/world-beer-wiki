# Weissbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Weissbier is the German wheat-beer tradition known for a lively combination of banana-like fruitiness and clove-like spice. These aromas usually come from fermentation, not from adding bananas or cloves.[^bjcp-2021-10a]

## Character

The beer is pale, highly carbonated, and often cloudy with suspended yeast. Wheat gives a soft, bread-like grain flavor, while hop bitterness stays low. The finish should be refreshing rather than thick or sugary.[^bjcp-2021-10a]

## Brewing and context

A substantial proportion of wheat malt is fermented with a yeast selected for the characteristic fruity and spicy profile. The balance between banana and clove varies, and filtered examples can be much clearer than the familiar yeast-clouded version.[^bjcp-2021-10a]

## History and interpretation

Names such as Weizen, Weissbier, and Hefeweizen overlap but carry local and product-specific nuances. Cloudiness alone is not enough to identify the style. American wheat beer usually has a cleaner yeast profile, while Belgian Witbier often uses a different grain-and-spice approach. Their shared use of wheat does not make them interchangeable.[^bjcp-2021-10a]
