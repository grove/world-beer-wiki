# Gose

**Beer Style** · Europe · revived

Gose is a pale, tart wheat-beer tradition with a restrained contribution from salt and coriander. The intended balance is refreshing and gently seasoned, not intensely salty or dominated by spice.[^bjcp-2021-23g]

## Character

Light grain, lemon-like acidity, subtle citrus or herbal coriander, and a faint mineral impression may combine. Hop bitterness is low. A lively, relatively light body keeps the beer from feeling heavy.[^bjcp-2021-23g]

## Brewing and context

Wheat and barley form the base, while lactic souring supplies acidity. Modern brewing methods vary. Salt and coriander should complement the beer rather than make it taste like a seasoning solution.[^bjcp-2021-23g]

## History and interpretation

The historical geography connects Goslar and, later, Leipzig; contemporary revivals and global craft interpretations broaden the family. Strong fruit additions can create a different drinking experience from the basic reference. Gose is unrelated to Gueuze merely because the names look similar: gueuze belongs to lambic blending, whereas Gose has its own grain, seasoning, and souring tradition.[^bjcp-2021-23g]
