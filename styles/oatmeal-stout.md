# Oatmeal Stout

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Oatmeal stout is a dark ale in which oats contribute to texture and, sometimes, a subtle grain or nut-like flavor. It is usually smoother and rounder than a sharply dry stout, but it need not be very sweet.[^bjcp-2021-16b]

## Character

Expect coffee or chocolate-like roast supported by malt and a soft, sometimes silky body. The amount of oat character varies with the recipe. Hops normally provide balance rather than a dominant fresh aroma.[^bjcp-2021-16b]

## Brewing and context

Oats accompany a barley-malt base and dark grains. They influence the beer's body, but the final texture also depends on fermentation and the rest of the recipe. Oats alone do not guarantee a thick beer.[^bjcp-2021-16b]

## History and interpretation

The name is not a dairy claim and does not make oatmeal stout equivalent to milk stout. Nor does the presence of oats establish a nutritional benefit. Modern examples range from fairly dry to gently sweet, so the useful distinction is the interaction of roast, malt, and texture rather than a rigid assumption about sweetness.[^bjcp-2021-16b]
