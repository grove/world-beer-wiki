# Dark Mild

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Dark mild is a lower-strength British ale that gives malt flavor priority over hop intensity. It can be surprisingly varied, from nutty and caramel-like to lightly chocolatey or gently roasted.[^bjcp-2021-13a]

## Character

The body should be comfortable rather than watery, despite the modest strength. Bitterness is usually restrained, and a small amount of fruity fermentation may add interest. Dark color does not imply high alcohol.[^bjcp-2021-13a]

## Brewing and context

Pale and darker malts, sometimes with brewing sugars, create the range of flavors. A mild can finish fairly dry or somewhat sweet; sweetness alone does not define it.[^bjcp-2021-13a]

## History and interpretation

The modern dark style is only part of the longer history of mild. Historically the term also concerned the beer's condition or freshness, and it should not be projected backward as a permanent synonym for weak, dark ale. This article uses the modern BJCP dark-mild reference. A pale mild or a strong historical mild needs a more specific description rather than being forced into it.[^bjcp-2021-13a]
