# Scottish Export

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Scottish Export is the fuller member of the ordinary Scottish ale family, with bread-like malt, restrained hops, and a smooth, approachable balance. It is still distinct from the much stronger Wee Heavy interpretation.[^bjcp-2021-14c]

## Character

The flavor can include caramel, toast, and a faint roasted-grain dryness. Fruitiness is usually restrained. The finish should be balanced rather than syrupy, and smoke is not a defining feature.[^bjcp-2021-14c]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt, small amounts of character grains, and sometimes brewing sugars provide the recipe framework. The overall effect matters more than a romantic claim about a uniquely ancient Scottish ingredient list.[^bjcp-2021-14c]

## History and interpretation

“Export” and shilling names have historical and commercial meanings that vary by period and brewery. They do not establish that every beer carrying the name was exported or brewed to one exact strength. This article uses the modern BJCP interpretation and does not treat peat-smoked malt as a normal requirement for Scottish ale.[^bjcp-2021-14c]

## Evidence note
