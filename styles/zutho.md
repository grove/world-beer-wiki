# Zutho

**Beer Tradition** · South Asia / India / Nagaland · living tradition

Zutho is a rice beer of Nagaland in northeastern India. This profile is anchored in a study of a sample collected in the Kohima district, rather than in a modern competition definition.[^zutho-research]

## Character

The researchers described a pale, porridge-like liquid with a fruity smell and a sour taste. Those observations offer a useful introduction: zutho is a substantial fermented rice drink, not simply a transparent lager in which rice replaces some barley.[^zutho-research]

## Brewing and context

Analysis identified alcohol and aroma compounds produced during fermentation. A brewing yeast isolated from the drink was identified as Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Finding that organism does not establish that all zutho is fermented with a pure culture or that no other microorganisms participate.[^zutho-research]

## History and interpretation

The sample's aroma was compared with Japanese sake. That is a sensory comparison, not evidence of shared origin or identical production. The public abstract does not justify a complete recipe, an invention date, or an alcohol range for every household's zutho; those details are deliberately left open.[^zutho-research]
