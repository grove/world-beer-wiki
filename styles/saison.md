# Saison

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Saison is a varied Belgian-associated ale family known for a very dry finish, lively carbonation, and fruity, peppery fermentation character. Modern examples differ considerably in strength and color.[^bjcp-2021-25b]

## Character

Light grain, citrus or orchard fruit, pepper, and herbal hops may combine. Bitterness and dryness help produce the characteristic brisk finish. Some versions are tart, but pronounced sourness is not required.[^bjcp-2021-25b]

## Brewing and context

Brewers use pale malt and sometimes other grains or sugar, with yeast that ferments thoroughly and supplies expressive character. Additional herbs or spices can appear, although the beer should not depend on them for every spicy impression.[^bjcp-2021-25b]

## History and interpretation

Historical farmhouse associations do not make every present-day Saison an exact farm recipe or every farmhouse beer a Saison. The contemporary family includes stronger, darker, and more experimental versions than a single stereotype suggests. Record the producer and period when making historical comparisons, and keep the wider European farmhouse traditions separate from this particular reference family.[^bjcp-2021-25b]
