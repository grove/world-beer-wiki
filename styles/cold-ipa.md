# Cold IPA

**Beer Style** · North America / United States / International · modern

Cold IPA is a modern, hop-forward approach built around a very dry, clean, light-bodied beer. In Wayfinder's definition, its purpose is to make aromatic hops and assertive bitterness stand out without the sweetness or weight of a richer IPA.[^cold-ipa-originator]

## Character

Expect a pale, clear appearance, lively carbonation, strong hop aroma, and a crisp finish. The hop flavors can vary widely. Clarity, dryness, and restrained fermentation character are more central to the originator's description than any single citrus or tropical-fruit note.[^cold-ipa-originator]

## Brewing and context

Wayfinder describes pale malt with rice or maize, no caramel malt, and commonly lager yeast fermented relatively warm. The brewery also permits suitable alternative yeast strains. The word “cold” therefore should not be interpreted as a complete or literal fermentation recipe.[^cold-ipa-originator]

## History and interpretation

Wayfinder dates its first example to October 2018; that is the brewery's own origin account. This entry follows that producer's intended profile rather than claiming every commercial Cold IPA obeys an independently enforced definition. Compare with American IPA and Brut IPA without treating them as synonyms.[^cold-ipa-originator]
