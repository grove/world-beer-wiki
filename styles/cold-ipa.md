# Cold IPA

Cold IPA is a modern, hop-forward concept built around a dry, pale base, restrained fermentation character and strong aromatic hopping. Wayfinder describes the name through its own brewing approach and dates its initial Relapse IPA to October 2018.[^cold-ipa-originator]

## What “cold” means in the producer account

Wayfinder describes an adjunct lager grist of pale malt with rice or maize, no caramel malt, and commonly lager yeast fermented relatively warm. It also names suitable alternative strains. Warm dry hopping during spunding or kräusening, filtration and carbonation are part of its stated approach.[^cold-ipa-originator]

Consequently, the name should not be translated into a universal cold-fermentation instruction. The useful record is the actual yeast and process, when the producer discloses them.

## Character and comparison

Wayfinder's target is clear, lean and highly hop-aromatic, with enough bitterness to remain distinct against low malt sweetness. The account frames this as a development of West Coast IPA, while contrasting it with a soft, hazy presentation.[^cold-ipa-originator]

Use [West Coast IPA](west-coast-ipa.md), [Hazy IPA](hazy-ipa.md) and [Brut IPA](brut-ipa.md) as comparison points, but do not classify from one shared adjective such as “dry.” In this wiki's comparison method, sensory resemblance and production method are separate observations. A beer can resemble another in the glass without sharing its complete process.

## Competition placement is a separate claim

BJCP suggests 34B Mixed-Style Beer for Cold IPA, describing an American IPA–American Lager combination.[^modern-bjcp-entry-suggestions]

This is guidance for the 2021 scheme, not a standalone numbered Cold IPA definition. It does not make Wayfinder's choices binding on other breweries.
