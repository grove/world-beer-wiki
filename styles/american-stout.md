# American Stout

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

American Stout emphasizes assertive roasted-grain flavor alongside a firm American hop presence. It is usually fuller and more forceful than the familiar low-strength Irish dry-stout reference.[^bjcp-2021-20b]

## Character

Coffee, dark chocolate, and roast lead, often supported by caramel or bread-like malt. Citrus or resinous hop character can add another layer. Bitterness comes across clearly, but rough astringency and burnt harshness are not necessary features.[^bjcp-2021-20b]

## Brewing and context

Dark malts or roasted grains are balanced against a substantial malt base. A relatively clean fermentation keeps roast and hops in focus. Ingredients such as coffee or chocolate are not required merely because the beer tastes reminiscent of them.[^bjcp-2021-20b]

## History and interpretation

The style is a modern American interpretation of stout rather than one ancient, universally fixed recipe. It differs from Imperial Stout primarily in overall strength and intensity, and from Sweet Stout in its generally less sweet balance. Examples overlap with robust porters, so the practical sensory boundaries deserve more attention than rigid ingredient myths.[^bjcp-2021-20b]
