# Roggenbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

Roggenbier, in this modern German style reference, combines a substantial rye character with the fruity and spicy fermentation profile familiar from German wheat beer. It is not a generic name for every beer containing rye.[^bjcp-2021-27h]

## Character

The flavor can suggest rye bread, grain, banana, and clove. The body is often rounded, with a lively carbonation that helps prevent heaviness. Hop bitterness remains supporting rather than IPA-like.[^bjcp-2021-27h]

## Brewing and context

Rye contributes distinctive bread-like grain character and texture, while the yeast supplies much of the fruit and spice. Barley and sometimes wheat can accompany the rye. The result differs from a clean-fermented Rye IPA even when both contain the same grain.[^bjcp-2021-27h]

## History and interpretation

The modern guideline traces its reference to a late-twentieth-century revival-style development, not an unbroken survival of one medieval recipe. Historical rye brewing is a wider subject. Keep that distinction clear: a historical ingredient tradition and a contemporary named style are related topics, but they are not automatically the same thing.[^bjcp-2021-27h]
