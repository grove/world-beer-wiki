# American Wheat Beer

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

American wheat beer is a wheat-forward beer with a relatively clean fermentation character. It highlights fresh grain and bread-dough flavors without requiring the banana and clove aromas associated with German Weissbier.[^bjcp-2021-1d]

## Character

It may be clear or hazy, and its hop character can range from modest to fairly noticeable. The body is usually light to medium, with an energetic sparkle and a finish that avoids heavy sweetness.[^bjcp-2021-1d]

## Brewing and context

A substantial share of wheat malt is combined with barley malt. Brewers may use either ale or lager yeast, so the style is better recognized by its overall character than by one fermentation label. Wheat supplies grain flavor and texture; cloudiness alone does not define the style.[^bjcp-2021-1d]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern American brewing interpretation rather than a generic name for all wheat beers. A fruit-flavored wheat beer is a variation on a base beer, while a strongly banana-and-clove example belongs nearer the German wheat tradition.[^bjcp-2021-1d]
