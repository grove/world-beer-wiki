# American IPA

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

American IPA is a hop-led, moderately strong ale with an assertive bitter finish. Malt supports the hops rather than turning the beer into a rich, caramel-heavy strong ale.[^bjcp-2021-21a]

## Character

Hop flavors can suggest citrus, pine, resin, flowers, tropical fruit, or other fruit characters. The beer generally finishes dry enough to keep the bitterness distinct. Clean fermentation is typical, though a little fruitiness may fit.[^bjcp-2021-21a]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt supplies the base, and generous late or dry hopping can build aroma. The recipe should provide enough body for balance without losing the brisk, hop-forward impression. Very sweet or heavily roasted versions belong to other interpretations.[^bjcp-2021-21a]

## History and interpretation

This modern American category is related to, but not interchangeable with, English IPA. It also does not encompass every beer now marketed as IPA: Hazy IPA and specialty variants have separate descriptions here. “West Coast” overlaps substantially with the dry, bitter end of this family, but regional marketing terms are not permanent technical boundaries.[^bjcp-2021-21a]
