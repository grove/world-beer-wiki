# Rye IPA

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

Rye IPA is a hop-forward American IPA variant with a noticeable rye-grain contribution. Rye adds bread-like, grainy, and sometimes peppery impressions while the beer retains an IPA's bitter, comparatively dry finish.[^bjcp-2021-21b-rye]

## Character

The texture can feel a little creamy, with citrus, resin, or other hop flavors above the grain. A spicy impression does not mean that caraway or other spices have been added. Harshness is not required.[^bjcp-2021-21b-rye]

## Brewing and context

A meaningful rye contribution joins the other brewing grains, and a relatively clean fermentation leaves hops and grain in focus. The rye should be perceptible rather than merely appearing in a recipe at an undetectable level.[^bjcp-2021-21b-rye]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern craft variation, not another name for German Roggenbier. Roggenbier usually emphasizes much stronger rye-bread character and wheat-beer-type yeast fruit and spice. Rye IPA is also not defined by a particular color or a single percentage of rye; the complete sensory balance matters more than the label.[^bjcp-2021-21b-rye]
