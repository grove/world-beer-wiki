# Old Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Old ale is a stronger English ale in which maturation is part of the character. Rich malt and fruitiness are joined by flavors that can develop as a well-kept beer ages.[^bjcp-2021-17b]

## Character

These may include dried fruit, nuts, caramel, and wine-like notes. Hop intensity often becomes less prominent with time. The beer can finish dry or somewhat sweet, and strength varies among examples.[^bjcp-2021-17b]

## Brewing and context

A malt-led base beer is conditioned or aged long enough for its flavor to change. Wood may be involved, but wood aging is not an automatic requirement. A little acidity can occur without making every old ale a sour beer.[^bjcp-2021-17b]

## History and interpretation

“Old” should not be used to excuse a ruined beer. Pleasant maturation and uncontrolled spoilage are not the same thing. Historical old and stock ales also varied widely, so this modern reference category is not a complete map of earlier British storage practices. Record age and storage conditions when comparing individual examples.[^bjcp-2021-17b]
