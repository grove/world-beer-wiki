# Bhaati jaanr

**Beer Tradition** · South Asia / India / Nepal / Bhutan · living tradition

Bhaati jaanr is an eastern Himalayan fermented rice preparation described in research from the context of Nepal, India, and Bhutan. It uses steamed glutinous rice and belongs to a food-and-drink tradition rather than to a clear, filtered beer category.[^bhaati-research]

## Character

The study followed increasing acidity and alcohol as fermentation developed. This is a useful guide to its changing character: a newly fermenting preparation and a later one need not taste alike. The abstract does not provide a broad sensory survey across communities.[^bhaati-research]

## Brewing and context

Several groups of microorganisms play different roles. Starch-breaking organisms make sugars available, while yeasts and lactic acid bacteria develop during fermentation. The researchers observed changes in their populations rather than one organism remaining equally dominant throughout.[^bhaati-research]

## History and interpretation

Rice distinguishes this entry from finger-millet kodo ko jaanr. The study's average alcohol measurement is not a compulsory style limit, especially where serving and dilution vary. This profile relies on the published abstract and does not pretend to reproduce a complete household brewing method.[^bhaati-research]
