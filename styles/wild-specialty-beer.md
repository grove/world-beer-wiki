# Wild specialty beer

**Specialty Beer Category** · International · modern category

Wild specialty beer is a modern judging category for fermentation-led beers with additional ingredients or distinctive wood treatment. It builds on a Brett, mixed-fermentation sour, or straight-sour base rather than describing one independent historical style.[^bjcp-2021-28c]

## Character

Fruit, herbs, spices, or wood should be recognizable but work with the fermentation character. A beer may be tart, fruity, earthy, or dry in different combinations. Added fruit does not necessarily make it sweet, because its sugars may ferment.[^bjcp-2021-28c]

## Brewing and context

The category also accommodates certain unusual woods or barrels that previously held other alcoholic drinks. Ingredients can add acidity or a drying sensation as well as flavor. Those contributions should support the beer rather than overwhelm it.[^bjcp-2021-28c]

## History and interpretation

“Wild” here is a classification label, not proof that the brewer used an uncontrolled or spontaneous fermentation. A useful description names the base beer and the additions. Traditional fruit lambic remains a separate named comparison rather than an automatic synonym.[^bjcp-2021-28c]
