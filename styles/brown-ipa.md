# Brown IPA

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

Brown IPA combines IPA-level hopping with the chocolate, caramel, and toasted-malt character of a brown ale. It should remain relatively dry and lean despite its richer malt flavor.[^bjcp-2021-21b-brown]

## Character

Cocoa, toffee, nuts, bread crust, or darker fruit can accompany citrus, pine, or other expressive hops. The two sides need to work together. Strong burnt roast and heavy residual sweetness would change the intended balance.[^bjcp-2021-21b-brown]

## Brewing and context

A pale-malt base is supplemented with caramel and lightly roasted or intermediate-color malts. Hopping and fermentation follow the broad IPA idea, while the darker malts add complexity rather than a dense stout-like body.[^bjcp-2021-21b-brown]

## History and interpretation

The category distinguishes especially hoppy, stronger brown ales from the more balanced American Brown Ale reference. It is a modern classification boundary, not evidence of an entirely separate ancient lineage. Compared with Black IPA, brown-malt flavors such as chocolate and caramel are more central than the contrast between very dark color and restrained roast.[^bjcp-2021-21b-brown]
