# Irish Extra Stout

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Irish extra stout is a stronger, fuller Irish stout interpretation with a pronounced dark-roast character. It occupies a different space from the lighter, creamy draught stout many drinkers first encounter.[^bjcp-2021-15c]

## Character

Coffee, dark chocolate, and roasted grain are central flavors. The malt has more depth, and a little fruitiness may appear, but the finish usually remains fairly dry rather than dessert-like.[^bjcp-2021-15c]

## Brewing and context

The recipe combines a pale-malt base with roasted grains and enough hops to support the darker flavors. Bottled examples need not have the nitrogen-driven texture of a draught stout.[^bjcp-2021-15c]

## History and interpretation

“Extra” is a relative brewery designation, not a globally standardized measurement. This category should be distinguished from Foreign Extra Stout, which often brings still greater strength and a different export-oriented interpretation. Historical products carrying similar names must be assessed using dated evidence. A modern label cannot by itself establish the strength or flavor of a beer sold under that name a century earlier.[^bjcp-2021-15c]
