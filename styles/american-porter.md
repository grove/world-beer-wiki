# American Porter

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

American Porter is a dark, moderately strong beer with substantial chocolate or roasted-malt flavor. It often gives hops and roast more prominence than the modern English Porter reference.[^bjcp-2021-20a]

## Character

The flavor may suggest cocoa, coffee, toasted bread, or caramel. Hop bitterness can be firm, with hop aroma varying considerably. The body supports the dark flavors, but the finish need not be sweet or heavy.[^bjcp-2021-20a]

## Brewing and context

A pale-malt foundation is combined with darker malts and a generally clean fermentation. Brewers balance roast intensity against malt sweetness and hops. No single roasted ingredient or recipe defines every example.[^bjcp-2021-20a]

## History and interpretation

“Robust porter” is a familiar earlier classification term. American Porter belongs to the wider porter tradition, but its modern guideline is not a direct description of eighteenth-century London beer. The border with American Stout is porous: names, recipes, and sensory impressions can overlap. Compare the actual roast, balance, and strength rather than assuming the label settles every distinction.[^bjcp-2021-20a]
