# IPA Argenta

**Beer Style** · South America · contemporary

IPA Argenta is an Argentine IPA interpretation that pairs local hop character with a modest wheat contribution. It aims for aromatic intensity and bitterness without a rough or heavy finish.[^bjcp-2021-x2]

## Character

Grapefruit, mandarin peel, flowers, and occasional herbal or resinous notes can lead. Malt remains supportive, while wheat contributes grain character and texture. The overall impression should be refreshing and comparatively dry.[^bjcp-2021-x2]

## Brewing and context

The published local specification uses pale malt with a limited amount of wheat and favors Argentine-grown hop varieties or local expressions of established varieties. Fermentation is generally clean or only lightly fruity. Wheat does not require a German wheat-beer yeast profile.[^bjcp-2021-x2]

## History and interpretation

The guideline records a formal definition developed through Somos Cerveceros meetings in 2013. This establishes a modern community classification, not the first-ever use of hops or wheat in Argentina. IPA Argenta is therefore linked to American and English IPA for comparison while retaining its own documented regional formulation.[^bjcp-2021-x2]
