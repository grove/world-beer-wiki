# Belgian Pale Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Belgian Pale Ale is a moderate-strength, copper-colored ale with a restrained balance of toasted malt, light fruitiness, and gentle hops. It is usually less intense in yeast spice than many stronger Belgian ales.[^bjcp-2021-24b]

## Character

Bread crust, biscuit, light caramel, and subtle pear or orange-like fruit may appear. Hop bitterness balances the malt without dominating. The finish is relatively approachable rather than strongly sour, heavily sweet, or sharply alcoholic.[^bjcp-2021-24b]

## Brewing and context

Pale and characterful malts create a rounded grain profile. Fermentation adds some fruit, while hopping remains proportionate to the malt. A Belgian origin does not require every beer to taste strongly of clove or pepper.[^bjcp-2021-24b]

## History and interpretation

The Spéciale Belge tradition belongs to the development of modern Belgian pale ales, including responses to imported beer styles. It should not be confused with the stronger golden Belgian Blond Ale merely because both names contain a pale-color word. This entry describes a particular reference family, not all pale beers brewed in Belgium.[^bjcp-2021-24b]
