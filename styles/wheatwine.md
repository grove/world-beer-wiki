# Wheatwine

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

Wheatwine is a strong American ale in which wheat contributes a distinctive bread-like, grainy, sometimes honey-like richness. Despite the name, grapes are not a defining ingredient.[^bjcp-2021-22d]

## Character

The beer can feel full and smooth, with warming strength and a rounded wheat flavor. Hops may balance the richness without overwhelming it. The familiar banana-and-clove character of German wheat-beer yeast is not central to this style.[^bjcp-2021-22d]

## Brewing and context

A substantial wheat contribution is combined with other fermentables in a concentrated wort. A comparatively clean fermentation lets the grain character remain recognizable. The result should have depth rather than simply tasting sweet or alcoholic.[^bjcp-2021-22d]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern craft-beer development, not a generic term for every strong wheat beer. Weizenbock uses a different fermentation profile, while American Barleywine generally emphasizes barley-malt richness and stronger hopping. Individual wheatwines vary in color and balance, so the grain-led impression and fermentation character are more useful identifiers than the name alone.[^bjcp-2021-22d]
