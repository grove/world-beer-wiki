# Eisbock

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Eisbock is a powerful bock-family beer made more concentrated by partially freezing the beer and removing some ice. That production step is central to its identity.[^bjcp-2021-9b]

## Character

The result can have intense bread, caramel, and dark-fruit flavors, with a full body and obvious warming strength. The beer should remain smooth; rough alcohol and harsh roast are not necessary consequences of concentration.[^bjcp-2021-9b]

## Brewing and context

Freezing removes part of the water-rich ice fraction, concentrating what remains. This differs from simply brewing a stronger wort, and it also differs from heat-based distillation. A very strong lager is not automatically Eisbock.[^bjcp-2021-9b]

## History and interpretation

Kulmbach is an important historical reference point. Stories about a barrel accidentally left outside are appealing, but the BJCP account explicitly treats such origin stories cautiously. The securely useful description is the concentration method and its effect on a bock-like base beer. Exact historical invention claims require better evidence than the repetition of a colorful brewery tale.[^bjcp-2021-9b]
