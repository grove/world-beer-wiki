# Hor Alank — Karbi rice-based ferment in southern Assam

Hor Alank is the Karbi-associated rice-based fermented beverage named in a 2024 comparative investigation in **southern Assam, India**. The researchers collected three community-produced samples per group across five named groups, excluding commercial market products. Naming a drink in that research context does not make it a nationally standardized beer style.[^v16-assam]

## Documented preparation, not a universal recipe

The paper describes glutinous rice and locally prepared starters for the sampled beverages. Its comparative table associates Hor Alank with **thap** starter and a short preparation window subject to local conditions. Specific plant ingredients and fermentation outcomes should be checked against Karbi practitioners before extrapolating the one study into fixed instructions.[^v16-assam]

## Read beside its neighbors

The same project studied Hmar Zu, Zeme Naga Zao, Dimasa Judima and Haria in the Tea tribes sampling cohort. Rice is shared; community language, starter practices and identity are not therefore interchangeable. The laboratory's bacterial sequence data should be read as a snapshot, not as a cultural taxonomy.

## Evidence boundary

The study is valuable because it states its sampling frame and makes names visible. It is not a complete ethnography, microbial safety audit, or survey of every Karbi village. This draft invites corrections from primary community sources.
