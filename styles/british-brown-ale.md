# British Brown Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

British brown ale is a malt-led brown beer whose flavors often suggest nuts, toast, caramel, or light chocolate. It is usually gentler in roast and hop intensity than porter or American brown ale.[^bjcp-2021-13b]

## Character

The balance can range from lightly sweet to fairly dry. Moderate fruitiness may come from the yeast, while hops give enough bitterness to keep the malt from becoming heavy.[^bjcp-2021-13b]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt is combined with more deeply colored malts and sometimes sugars. The aim is a rounded brown-malt character rather than the burnt intensity associated with stout. Brown color alone does not establish which ingredients were used.[^bjcp-2021-13b]

## History and interpretation

“Brown ale” has described different beers at different times. The modern British reference is not identical to the sweeter, lower-strength bottled London brown-ale tradition, nor to every historical brown beer mentioned in old records. Comparisons are more useful when they specify sweetness, roast, strength, and period instead of treating the name as an unchanging recipe.[^bjcp-2021-13b]
