# Märzen

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Märzen is an amber German lager whose central flavor is toasted bread and rounded malt. It should feel rich without becoming sugary, heavily caramelized, or roasted like a dark stout.[^bjcp-2021-6a]

## Character

The color runs from amber to copper, and the finish is usually smooth and moderately dry. Hop bitterness supplies balance rather than a dominant aroma. Clean lager fermentation lets the malt remain the main feature.[^bjcp-2021-6a]

## Brewing and context

Pale and more deeply kilned barley malts create the bread-crust and toast character. The name connects the style with older seasonal brewing and storage practices, but a modern Märzen is not automatically a reconstruction of one historical March brew.[^bjcp-2021-6a]

## History and interpretation

In international usage, amber Märzen is often associated with Oktoberfest. Modern Munich-style Festbier, however, is usually golden and should have its own entry. This distinction is especially useful when a label says only “Oktoberfest”: the festival reference does not, by itself, tell the reader which sensory interpretation is in the bottle.[^bjcp-2021-6a]
