# British Golden Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

British golden ale is a pale, hop-accented ale with a clean, refreshing finish. It applies the drinkability of bitter to a lighter-colored beer, often with a more obvious fresh hop aroma.[^bjcp-2021-12a]

## Character

The malt is usually restrained and bread-like rather than caramel-heavy. Hop flavors may be floral, citrus-like, or otherwise fruity, depending on the varieties used. The beer should remain balanced and not feel like an overstrong IPA.[^bjcp-2021-12a]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt supplies the base, sometimes with wheat or small additions of other grains. British or newer international hop varieties can both appear. Yeast fruitiness is usually modest enough to let the hops remain clear.[^bjcp-2021-12a]

## History and interpretation

The style is a relatively modern British development and is often associated with summer drinking, though it need not be seasonal. “Golden ale” is used differently in other countries, so a British golden ale should not automatically be equated with an American blonde or a Belgian golden strong ale. The adjective describes color, not a universal recipe.[^bjcp-2021-12a]
