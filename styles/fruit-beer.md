# Fruit beer

**Specialty Beer Category** · International · modern category

Fruit beer is an ingredient-defined category: recognizable beer combined with recognizable fruit character. It can begin with many base styles, so the name alone does not tell you its color, strength, bitterness, or fermentation method.[^bjcp-2021-29a]

## Character

Fruit aroma can be subtle or strong, depending on the fruit and the beer. Color may change as well. The intended result is an integrated drink rather than fruit flavor simply covering up the base beer.[^bjcp-2021-29a]

## Brewing and context

Fruit contributes more than sweetness. Its sugars may ferment away, leaving aroma and a drier finish; it can also add acidity or a drying tannic sensation. A raspberry beer and a blueberry beer need not show the same intensity to use their fruit effectively.[^bjcp-2021-29a]

## History and interpretation

The BJCP category provides a way to evaluate this combination, not a claim that fruit brewing began in one place. Named traditions such as fruit lambic and regional modern styles such as Catharina Sour deserve their own entries and fermentation context.[^bjcp-2021-29a]
