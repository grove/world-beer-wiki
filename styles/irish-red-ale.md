# Irish Red Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

Irish red ale is a reddish, malt-led beer with a restrained hop character and a finish that can be gently dry. Its color is more conspicuous than its flavor intensity.[^bjcp-2021-15a]

## Character

Expect grain, light caramel or toffee, and sometimes a small roasted-grain note in the finish. The beer should not have the strong roast of stout or the assertive hopping of an American red or amber ale.[^bjcp-2021-15a]

## Brewing and context

Small amounts of darker grain can provide both color and a drying edge. Recipes and fermentation approaches vary, so not every commercial example has exactly the same sweetness or yeast profile.[^bjcp-2021-15a]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern style grouping with a range of interpretations. The Irish association should not be turned into an unsupported claim that every red ale follows a medieval national recipe. When comparing products, note whether the malt is toasty, caramel-like, or lightly roasted and whether the finish is dry or sweet. Those details are more informative than redness alone.[^bjcp-2021-15a]
