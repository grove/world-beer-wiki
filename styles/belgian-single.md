# Belgian Single

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

Belgian Single describes a relatively light, dry, hop-balanced monastic-style ale. It offers expressive Belgian fermentation character without the strength of Tripel or Belgian Dark Strong Ale.[^bjcp-2021-26a]

## Character

The flavor can combine bread, fruit, peppery spice, and floral or herbal hops. Bitterness is noticeable, while high carbonation and a dry finish keep the beer brisk rather than heavy.[^bjcp-2021-26a]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt and a characterful yeast form the foundation. The beer's lower strength does not mean it should taste watery; the balance of malt, hops, and fermentation supplies interest. Exact recipes differ among breweries.[^bjcp-2021-26a]

## History and interpretation

This modern reference is not a universal description of everything monks drink or brew. Terms such as “single,” “patersbier,” and “table beer” overlap only partly and can carry producer-specific meanings. Monastery association is also not the same as proof that a commercial product meets a particular certification. The sensory family should be kept separate from institutional status and branding.[^bjcp-2021-26a]
