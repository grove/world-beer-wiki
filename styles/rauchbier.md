# Rauchbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

In this entry, Rauchbier means the Bamberg-style smoked lager built on a Märzen-like malt foundation. The German word more broadly means smoked beer, so not every beer made with smoked malt belongs to this narrower style.[^bjcp-2021-6b]

## Character

Expect toasted bread, a smooth lager background, and wood smoke that may range from restrained to prominent. The smoke can suggest a campfire or savory food, but those impressions do not mean bacon or meat was added.[^bjcp-2021-6b]

## Brewing and context

Malt dried over beechwood smoke is the defining reference ingredient. The balance depends on both the proportion of smoked malt and the underlying beer. Hops support the malt and smoke without becoming the main attraction.[^bjcp-2021-6b]

## History and interpretation

Bamberg preserves a strong smoked-lager tradition, but smoking is also found in unrelated brewing cultures. Similar smoky aromas are not proof of a shared lineage. Compare this style with oak-smoked Polish wheat beer and Norwegian farmhouse beer to see how grain, wood, fermentation, and serving context create different results.[^bjcp-2021-6b]
