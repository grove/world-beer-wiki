# Kölsch

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Kölsch is Cologne's pale, delicate beer tradition, combining ale fermentation with cold conditioning. It demonstrates why fermentation method and the experience of drinking a beer are not always captured by a simple ale-or-lager contrast.[^bjcp-2021-5b]

## Character

The flavor is lightly grainy and fresh, with restrained bitterness and sometimes a faint fruity or wine-like note. It should finish cleanly and avoid the strong banana, clove, caramel, or heavy hop flavors found in other styles.[^bjcp-2021-5b]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt forms the base, and some recipes include wheat. Yeast and fermentation management are especially important because the beer has little heavy malt or hop flavor behind which faults can hide.[^bjcp-2021-5b]

## History and interpretation

Kölsch has a specific regional identity, while beers inspired by it are brewed elsewhere. This entry describes that brewing and sensory tradition, not the legal entitlement of a particular product to use the name. A generic pale ale does not become Kölsch merely by being cold, clear, or served in a narrow glass.[^bjcp-2021-5b]
