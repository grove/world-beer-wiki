# Schwarzbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Schwarzbier is a dark German lager that combines a clean, relatively light body with restrained roasted-malt flavor. It is not a stout simply fermented with lager yeast.[^bjcp-2021-8b]

## Character

The color can approach black, but the taste is usually smooth rather than aggressively burnt. Light chocolate or coffee-like notes sit alongside bread-like malt and a balancing hop bitterness. The finish should remain fairly dry.[^bjcp-2021-8b]

## Brewing and context

A pale or toasted-malt base is supplemented with dark malts. Careful handling of those roasted ingredients allows color and flavor without excessive roughness. Lager fermentation keeps the background clean.[^bjcp-2021-8b]

## History and interpretation

The style is associated particularly with central and eastern German brewing traditions. Modern black lagers elsewhere may take inspiration from it without matching it exactly. Compare the amount of roast, body, and sweetness: Munich Dunkel is more bread-and-toast focused, while many stouts are more strongly roasted. Appearance alone is a poor guide to strength, weight, or bitterness.[^bjcp-2021-8b]
