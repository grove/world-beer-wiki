# American Strong Ale

**Beer Style Family** · North America · contemporary

American Strong Ale is a broad modern category for substantial, bitter, malt-rich ales that do not sit neatly in narrower styles. It is a useful description of a balance, not a single historically unified recipe.[^bjcp-2021-22b]

## Character

The beer can combine deep caramel, bread crust, toast, dried fruit, and strong citrus or resinous hops. Both malt and bitterness are prominent. Alcohol warmth is expected to fit into the overall flavor rather than dominate it.[^bjcp-2021-22b]

## Brewing and context

A concentrated malt base and assertive hopping provide the structure. Color, residual sweetness, and hop aroma vary. The category permits more malt depth than a typical Double IPA without necessarily reaching the weight of American Barleywine.[^bjcp-2021-22b]

## History and interpretation

Because this is a grouping, its borders are intentionally broad. A commercial beer may also be described as a strong amber ale or another brewery-specific name. Do not assume that everything called “strong ale” belongs here: the British category has a different scope, and strength alone does not determine the style.[^bjcp-2021-22b]
