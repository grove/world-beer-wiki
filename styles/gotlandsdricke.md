# Gotlandsdricke

**Beer Tradition** · Northern Europe / Sweden · living tradition

Gotlandsdricke is the farmhouse beer tradition of Gotland, Sweden. It is best understood as a family of locally transmitted brewing practices, not a single ancient recipe preserved without change.[^garshol-gotland]

## Character

Juniper and malt are central reference points. Smoke is common but not universal in the documented recipes. Grain, degree of smoking, and whether the wort is boiled differ, so one rigid tasting specification would hide meaningful variation.[^garshol-gotland]

## Brewing and context

Garshol's comparison of collected recipes found barley dominant on Gotland, with rye appearing in the Fårö material. Hops were widespread. Juniper commonly appeared as branches in the filter and often as an infusion; this is not equivalent to adding a measured dose of juniper berries.[^garshol-gotland]

## History and interpretation

The account also documents changes in malt purchasing and yeast use. Those changes are part of a living tradition, not evidence that every contemporary beer is inauthentic. Descriptions such as “Viking beer” need separate historical support and should not replace evidence about how particular Gotland brewers actually work.[^garshol-gotland]
