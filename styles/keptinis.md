# Keptinis

**Beer Tradition** · Northern Europe / Lithuania · living tradition

Keptinis is Lithuanian beer distinguished by baking the mash before extracting the wort. The important ingredient is not necessarily bread: the brewer heats the mashed grain itself in an oven.[^garshol-keptinis]

## Character

A firsthand account of the Jančys family's beer describes a very dark reddish beer with deep toast, caramel, and earthy flavors. It was rounded and sweet rather than sharply roasted. That tasting is a useful example, not a mandatory profile for every keptinis.[^garshol-keptinis]

## Brewing and context

In the observed process, pale malt was mashed, placed in metal boxes, and baked in a wood-fired oven. The baked mash was broken up and rinsed over a filter containing straw. The heating of the mash supplied substantial color and flavor before fermentation.[^garshol-keptinis]

## History and interpretation

The report records family teaching alongside practical changes such as metal containers replacing older equipment. It therefore documents continuity and adaptation together. A dark beer made from roasted malt alone does not demonstrate the baked-mash process that gives keptinis its identity.[^garshol-keptinis]
