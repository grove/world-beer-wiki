# Gueuze

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Gueuze is a sparkling blend of lambics of different ages. Its lively carbonation, dryness, and layered fermentation character distinguish it from the often still, unblended lambic served on its own.[^bjcp-2021-23e]

## Character

The flavor can combine citrus-like acidity, gentle fruit, wheat, earth, and complex fermentation aromas. A well-balanced example feels dry and lively rather than simply sour. Harsh vinegar or an overwhelming single aroma should not substitute for complexity.[^bjcp-2021-23e]

## Brewing and context

Blenders choose younger and older lambics for complementary qualities. Fermentable material remaining in the blend supports further fermentation in the bottle, producing carbonation. Exact age combinations and blending decisions differ among producers.[^bjcp-2021-23e]

## History and interpretation

The familiar spelling variants “gueuze” and “geuze” refer to the same broad tradition here. Gose, despite the similar-looking name, is a different beer. Modern sweetened products and traditional dry blends can present very different experiences, so a label needs to be read in its production context rather than treated as a complete flavor guarantee.[^bjcp-2021-23e]
