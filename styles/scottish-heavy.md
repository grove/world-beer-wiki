# Scottish Heavy

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Scottish Heavy is a malt-focused Scottish ale of moderate strength. The name can sound more formidable than the beer: “heavy” is a traditional relative designation, not a promise of extreme alcohol or body.[^bjcp-2021-14b]

## Character

Expect bread-like malt, light caramel or toffee, modest bitterness, and little hop aroma. A small dark-grain edge can help dry the finish. The beer should not be thick or overwhelmingly sweet.[^bjcp-2021-14b]

## Brewing and context

Recipes can use pale malt, darker grains, and brewing sugars. Fermentation is generally restrained, allowing the malt balance to lead. Peat smoke is not required and should not be assumed from the Scottish association.[^bjcp-2021-14b]

## History and interpretation
