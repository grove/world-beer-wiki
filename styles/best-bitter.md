# Best Bitter

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Best bitter is the middle-strength expression of the English bitter family. It has enough malt presence to feel satisfying while retaining the dry, refreshing balance that makes the family distinctive.[^bjcp-2021-11b]

## Character

The flavor often combines biscuit or bread, a little caramel, modest fruity yeast character, and firm hop bitterness. Neither sweetness nor hop aroma should completely dominate the beer.[^bjcp-2021-11b]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt, small amounts of character malts, and sometimes brewing sugars form the base. English-type hops and yeast are common reference points. Cask conditioning can give a gentle sparkle and a rounded texture, but the beer is not defined by being flat.[^bjcp-2021-11b]

## History and interpretation

The terms “best,” “special,” and “premium” vary among breweries. They should not be treated as globally fixed strength guarantees. Compared with ordinary bitter, this interpretation usually offers more malt and strength; compared with strong bitter, it is generally lighter. Those are useful tendencies, not hard boundaries that override a brewery's historical naming practice.[^bjcp-2021-11b]
