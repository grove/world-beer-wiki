# English IPA

**Beer Style** · Europe / South Asia · living

English IPA is a hop-forward pale ale in which bitterness, a dry finish, and a supporting bread or biscuit-like malt character remain in balance. It usually has more noticeable yeast fruitiness than a very clean American IPA.[^bjcp-2021-12c]

## Character

Hop aromas can suggest flowers, earth, herbs, or citrus peel. The malt should support the beer rather than turn it into a sweet strong ale. Modern examples vary considerably in strength and intensity.[^bjcp-2021-12c]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt, English-type ale yeast, and a substantial hop contribution provide the reference framework. Brewing sugars may also appear. The flavor does not depend on a beer physically traveling to India.[^bjcp-2021-12c]

## History and interpretation

The popular story that IPA was uniquely invented because ordinary beer could not survive the voyage is misleading. Other beers were shipped to India, and historical export pale ales should not be reduced to one heroic invention story. Modern English IPA is a present-day style interpretation; reconstructing a nineteenth-century example requires a dated recipe or brewing record.[^bjcp-2021-12c]
