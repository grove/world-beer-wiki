# Catharina Sour

**Beer Style** · South America · contemporary

Catharina Sour is a Brazilian fruit-led sour wheat beer associated with Santa Catarina. Its defining impression is fresh, recognizable fruit over clean acidity and a light, lively base.[^bjcp-2021-x4]

## Character

The chosen fruit should be easy to identify, while malt and hop bitterness stay in the background. Tropical fruit is common but not mandatory. The finish is normally refreshing rather than thick, jam-like, or dominated by wild-fermentation aromas.[^bjcp-2021-x4]

## Brewing and context

Wheat and pale barley malt support lactic souring, commonly through a kettle-sour approach followed by relatively neutral fermentation. Fruit is often introduced later in production. The fruit's flavor and acidity need to remain balanced.[^bjcp-2021-x4]

## History and interpretation

The local description was formalized by brewers in 2015, while similar beers had existed under other names. That distinction matters: a naming event is not necessarily an invention event. Catharina Sour is related in modern brewing practice to fruited pale sours but should not be collapsed into lambic or treated as every fruit beer made in Brazil.[^bjcp-2021-x4]
