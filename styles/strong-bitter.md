# Strong Bitter

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Strong bitter is the fuller, stronger end of the English bitter family. It combines malt depth, fruity fermentation, and hop bitterness rather than making any one of them the whole point.[^bjcp-2021-11c]

## Character

The malt may suggest bread, biscuit, toast, or caramel. Hops can be clearly noticeable, and the finish ranges from fairly dry to gently rounded. It should not become a syrupy barleywine or an aggressively hop-dominated American IPA.[^bjcp-2021-11c]

## Brewing and context

Recipes vary considerably, using pale malt with optional colored malts and brewing sugars. Yeast contributes much of the character, especially the familiar fruitiness of many British ales.[^bjcp-2021-11c]

## History and interpretation

“ESB” needs care. It is closely associated with a particular English brewery's beer, while outside Britain it is also used as a broad style label. Not every strong bitter is an imitation of that product. The category is most useful as a range of balance and strength, with the producer and serving format recorded alongside the name.[^bjcp-2021-11c]
