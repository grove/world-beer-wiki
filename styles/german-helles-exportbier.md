# German Helles Exportbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

German Helles Exportbier is a pale lager with enough malt body and hop bitterness to stand between softer Helles and more sharply bitter Pils. Dortmund is the principal historical reference point.[^bjcp-2021-5c]

## Character

Expect firm bread-like malt, a clean fermentation character, and an even balance rather than a dramatic hop aroma. It is generally fuller than a very lean pilsner while still finishing crisply.[^bjcp-2021-5c]

## Brewing and context

Pale lager malt, traditional German-type hops, and lager fermentation shape the beer. Water and local brewing practice can contribute to its firmer impression, but no single mineral taste should substitute for a balanced beer.[^bjcp-2021-5c]

## History and interpretation

The Dortmund interpretation developed in the industrial brewing context of the nineteenth century. “Export” can also function as a strength or market designation, so it does not prove that a particular batch was shipped abroad. Other beers labeled Export may not match this pale German reference style. Judge the actual malt-hop balance, body, and fermentation profile.[^bjcp-2021-5c]
