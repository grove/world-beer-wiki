# Weizenbock

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Weizenbock is a strong German wheat beer that brings together wheat-beer yeast character and bock-like malt depth. It can be pale or dark.[^bjcp-2021-10c]

## Character

Banana, clove, bread, and warming alcohol form the main flavor picture. Dark examples may add toast and dried-fruit impressions; pale ones are more focused on fresh bread and grain. High carbonation helps keep the substantial beer from feeling flat or heavy.[^bjcp-2021-10c]

## Brewing and context

A concentrated wheat-and-barley wort is fermented with a German wheat-beer yeast. The “bock” part of the name signals strength and malt intensity here, not a requirement for lager yeast.[^bjcp-2021-10c]

## History and interpretation

Weizenbock is therefore a useful counterexample to the assumption that all bocks are lagers. It is also different from Wheatwine, which emphasizes wheat-derived bread flavors without requiring the same banana-and-clove profile. For historical comparisons, distinguish a named modern product or interpretation from the much broader fact that strong wheat beers have existed in different forms.[^bjcp-2021-10c]
