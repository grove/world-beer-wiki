# Altbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Altbier is Düsseldorf's copper-colored ale tradition, combining a firm hop bitterness with bread-like malt and a clean, well-conditioned finish. It is an ale that is typically matured cold.[^bjcp-2021-7b]

## Character

The flavor may suggest toast, nuts, or light caramel, but should not become heavily sweet or roasted. Bitterness is a structural part of the beer rather than merely a little seasoning.[^bjcp-2021-7b]

## Brewing and context

Top-fermenting yeast works with a barley-malt base, while cool fermentation management and cold conditioning keep fruitiness restrained. This combination gives the beer a profile that can seem lager-like without turning it into a lager by definition.[^bjcp-2021-7b]

## History and interpretation

The “old” in Alt refers to a brewing tradition, not to a requirement that the beer taste stale or be aged for years. The Düsseldorf reference is also narrower than every beer elsewhere sold under an Alt label. For comparison, look at the balance of bitter hops, toasted malt, and clean fermentation rather than color alone.[^bjcp-2021-7b]
