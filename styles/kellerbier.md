# Kellerbier

**Beer Tradition** · Europe · living

Kellerbier is best understood as a traditional presentation and conditioning approach to lager rather than one fixed color or recipe. It commonly describes unfiltered, unpasteurized lager served with a fresh, less processed character.[^bjcp-2021-27a]

## Character

The flavor depends on the underlying beer: a pale version may emphasize bread and hops, while an amber or dark version can bring toast and richer malt. A little yeast character may remain, but obvious brewing faults are not a sign of authenticity.[^bjcp-2021-27a]

## Brewing and context

Filtration, carbonation, storage, and service all affect the final impression. “Unfiltered” does not mean the beer must be unfinished or extremely cloudy. Nor does the word Kellerbier guarantee a particular fermentation vessel.[^bjcp-2021-27a]

## History and interpretation

Regional names and practices overlap, including some uses of Zwickelbier. The modern guideline deliberately recognizes this variability. Compare a Kellerbier with its likely base style rather than forcing every example into a single amber-beer template. In this wiki it is marked as a beer tradition rather than a narrowly defined style.[^bjcp-2021-27a]
