# American Pale Ale

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

American Pale Ale puts expressive hops over a noticeable but supportive malt base. It is generally less strong and less intensely hop-dominated than American IPA, although individual examples overlap.[^bjcp-2021-18b]

## Character

Citrus, pine, flowers, or newer tropical and stone-fruit hop aromas may lead. Underneath, bread, biscuit, or a little caramel provides substance. A balanced example finishes refreshing, with enough bitterness to prevent a sugary impression.[^bjcp-2021-18b]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt and characterful hops are central. Clean ale fermentation allows the ingredients to show clearly, though a small amount of yeast fruitiness can fit. Clarity and the amount of caramel character vary among interpretations.[^bjcp-2021-18b]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern American development within the wider pale-ale family, not an assertion that earlier pale ales lacked hops. Nor is every pale-colored ale an APA. Compared with British Bitter, hop character is usually more prominent; compared with IPA, the beer normally gives malt and moderate strength more room.[^bjcp-2021-18b]
