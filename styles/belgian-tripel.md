# Belgian Tripel

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Tripel is a strong, pale Belgian ale that combines fruit, spice, noticeable bitterness, and a dry finish. It should feel more lively and drinkable than its strength might suggest.[^bjcp-2021-26c]

## Character

Bread-like malt, citrus or orchard fruit, pepper, and gentle clove can appear. Carbonation is high, and alcohol warmth should be smooth. Heavy caramel sweetness or an oily body would obscure the intended balance.[^bjcp-2021-26c]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt, often supplemented with sugar, supports a highly fermentative yeast profile. The use of sugar can lighten the final body because much of it is consumed during fermentation. Spicy aromas do not necessarily come from added spices.[^bjcp-2021-26c]

## History and interpretation

The modern reference is closely associated with Belgian monastic brewing, especially the Westmalle model. That association does not turn every commercial Tripel into a monastery-made beer. Nor does the name mean three separate fermentations are required. Belgian Golden Strong Ale is a useful comparison, but the styles differ in the balance of malt, hops, and yeast.[^bjcp-2021-26c]
