# International Pale Lager

**Beer Style** · Worldwide · contemporary

International pale lager is a broad reference category for clean, golden lagers made in many countries. It describes a shared flavor pattern, not a single birthplace, recipe, or continuous brewing lineage.[^bjcp-2021-2a]

## Character

The beer is usually crisp and well carbonated, with mild grain flavors and modest hop bitterness. Some examples are mostly malt-focused; others have a little more herbal or floral hop character. Strong fruitiness, heavy caramel, and substantial roast are not central features.[^bjcp-2021-2a]

## Brewing and context

Recipes may use only barley malt or include other grains and sugars. Lager fermentation and maturation are more important to the category than a claim that every example uses identical ingredients.[^bjcp-2021-2a]

## History and interpretation

This grouping is useful for comparison but can hide local differences. A brewery's national identity does not by itself establish a separate historical style. Light-struck, skunky aromas caused by poor protection from light are not a required characteristic, even when consumers associate them with particular packaged beers.[^bjcp-2021-2a]

## Evidence note

This profile paraphrases a modern judging reference. Its flavor description is better supported than any claim of a single origin or an unchanged historical recipe. It is not a complete brewing recipe.
