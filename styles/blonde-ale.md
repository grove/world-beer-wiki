# Blonde Ale

**Beer Style** · North America · contemporary

Blonde ale is an approachable pale ale in which light malt, gentle hops, and a clean fermentation work together. It is a distinct American craft-beer reference, not simply another spelling of Belgian Blond Ale.[^bjcp-2021-18a]

## Character

The flavor can suggest fresh bread or light grain, with a little fruitiness and modest floral, herbal, or citrus-like hops. Bitterness provides balance without becoming the main attraction. The beer is usually light enough in body to finish cleanly.[^bjcp-2021-18a]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt supplies the foundation. Brewers may use small amounts of other grains, but strong caramel, heavy roast, or an intense spicy yeast character would change the overall impression. Both fermentation and conditioning choices can vary.[^bjcp-2021-18a]

## History and interpretation

The name is not globally standardized: “golden ale” can describe other traditions in other countries. Identify the intended national or brewery context before equating labels. Blonde ale is especially useful as a comparison point between a very light lager and a more assertively hopped American Pale Ale.[^bjcp-2021-18a]
