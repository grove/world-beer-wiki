# Doppelbock

**Beer Style** · Europe · living

Doppelbock is a strong German lager with an unusually deep malt character. Both pale and dark versions exist, so the name does not mean “very dark beer.”[^bjcp-2021-9a]

## Character

Dark examples can suggest toasted bread, caramel, and dried fruit, while pale ones are usually lighter in malt flavor and somewhat drier. Alcohol provides warmth, but a well-made beer should not taste hot or solvent-like. Hop aroma is generally secondary.[^bjcp-2021-9a]

## Brewing and context

A rich malt bill and a concentrated wort supply the strength. Lager fermentation keeps the profile comparatively clean. Much of the impression of richness comes from malt flavor, not necessarily from a large amount of unfermented sugar.[^bjcp-2021-9a]

## History and interpretation

Doppelbock belongs to the bock family, but “double” is a conventional name rather than a rule that it contains twice the alcohol of another beer. Compare pale and dark examples separately. Eisbock introduces an additional concentration process and should not be treated as merely a different label for any powerful Doppelbock.[^bjcp-2021-9a]
