# Belgian Golden Strong Ale

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

Belgian Golden Strong Ale combines considerable strength with a pale appearance, a light-feeling body, and a very lively, dry finish. The contrast between strength and apparent delicacy is central to its appeal.[^bjcp-2021-25c]

## Character

Pear, apple, citrus, light spice, and floral or herbal hops can stand above a subtle grain background. Carbonation adds lift. Alcohol may warm, but a hot or heavy impression would undermine the balance.[^bjcp-2021-25c]

## Brewing and context

Pale malt and fermentable sugar help create strength without excessive residual body. Expressive Belgian-type fermentation supplies fruit and spice while leaving the beer dry. Sugar is a production ingredient, not proof that the finished beer must taste sweet.[^bjcp-2021-25c]

## History and interpretation

This is a modern Belgian reference family with a strong association with the Duvel model. It should not be treated as identical to Tripel, even though the two share pale color, strength, and yeast character. Golden Strong Ale generally places more emphasis on a lean, sparkling, hop-balanced impression.[^bjcp-2021-25c]
