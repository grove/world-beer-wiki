# German Leichtbier

**Beer Style** · Europe · contemporary

German Leichtbier is a light-bodied, lower-alcohol lager that still aims to taste recognizably like a German pale lager. The reduced strength does not require an absence of malt flavor or hop bitterness.[^bjcp-2021-5a]

## Character

Expect a crisp finish, restrained grain flavor, and a noticeable but balanced herbal, floral, or spicy hop impression. Its body is lighter than standard Pils, while the clean fermentation character remains important.[^bjcp-2021-5a]

## Brewing and context

Brewers work with a less concentrated beer or other production choices that reduce strength while preserving balance. The style description does not prescribe one universal industrial method.[^bjcp-2021-5a]

## History and interpretation

This modern reference category should not be confused with alcohol-free beer. Nor is it identical to American light lager, whose typical flavor is more restrained. The useful comparison is how much flavor and finish remain at a lower strength. Numerical labeling and dietary claims belong to individual products and jurisdictions, not to this encyclopedia's definition of the style.[^bjcp-2021-5a]
