# Malt starch, gelatinization and enzyme action

Barley malt provides starch, but the starch must become accessible to enzymes before yeast can ferment its sugars. A 2024 review emphasizes relationships among starch granule characteristics, gelatinization and enzymatic hydrolysis.[^v14-starch]

## Many linked variables

The review notes that barley genotype, malting conditions and climate alter starch behavior. The study of two malt houses further shows that enzyme performance and cell-wall modification are different parts of malting quality; one “malt strength” number does not capture them all.[^v14-starch][^v14-malt]
