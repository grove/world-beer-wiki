# Barley beta-glucans and wort texture

Beta-glucans are barley cell-wall polysaccharides, not hop bitterness molecules or yeast strains. A study of two commercial malt houses relates them to modification of barley endosperm and to wort viscosity and filtration behavior.[^v14-malt]

## Why brewers track them

Undermodified malt and high-molecular-weight beta-glucan can complicate wort separation, pumping or downstream filtration. The paper also discusses arabinoxylans and physical size of polymers; the measured effect depends on the malt and process, not merely an ingredient-list word.[^v14-malt]

## Do not mistake texture for a defect

A compound associated with filtration challenges does not make every full-bodied or hazy beer wrong. [Colloidal haze](../sensory/beer-haze-and-clarity.md), [barley malt](barley-and-malt.md) and [starch conversion](../processes/starch-conversion.md) explore these different mechanisms.
