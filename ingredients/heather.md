# Heather in beer and the modern Fraoch revival

## Which story does the maker tell?

Williams Bros. Brewing recounts a 1988 encounter with a family recipe for heather ale and the development of its **Fraoch** beer. The company reports experimentation with malt and botanical mixtures before producing commercial versions. This is valuable first-person evidence of a *modern revival project*; its claim that the recipe's roots stretch into remote antiquity is not independent archaeological proof.[^v13-heather]

## Plant flavor versus historical lineage

Reference descriptions include heather among botanicals sometimes associated with herbal beers. But a botanically flavored modern ale and a medieval beer need not share a formula, brewing procedure or uninterrupted chain of transmission. Flowering and harvesting seasons can influence modern production, yet should not be presented as ancient calendrical laws.[^v13-gruit][^v13-heather]
