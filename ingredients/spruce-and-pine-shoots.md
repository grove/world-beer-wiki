# Spruce, fir and pine shoots in beer

## Shoots are not interchangeable with bark or resin

The Oxford Companion to Beer describes fresh spruce, pine and fir tips as brewing materials. Young shoots can be less resinous and sometimes more citrus-like than mature needles, but those adjectives are not chemical equivalence across tree species. Specific plant identity and material form matter.[^v13-spruce]

## Traditions and modern reinterpretations

The reference connects evergreen-flavored beer with several historical and colonial brewing settings. Its summary of maritime and North American associations is a useful lead, but not a single genealogy uniting every spruce beverage. A contemporary 'spruce IPA' may employ hops and spruce independently, and its tropical aroma need not come from either alone.[^v13-spruce]
