# Brewers’ spent grain as a resource

Brewers’ spent grain is the wet grain fraction remaining after wort separation. Because soluble components were extracted unevenly, its composition differs from the original malt and depends on the brewing run.[^v14-malt]

## Value is not intrinsic to a disposal label

A sustainability review surveys feed, compost, anaerobic digestion, food uses and more intensive forms of material processing. It cautions that the viability and impact of these pathways depend on local collection, drying, transport and processing conditions.[^v14-circular]

## Do not mistake an idea for a safety standard

Calling a by-product potentially food-grade does not establish storage stability, handling hygiene or suitability for individual consumption. Read [circular brewery systems](../environment/spent-grain-and-circular-use.md) and [barley beta-glucans](barley-beta-glucans.md) for separate material and environmental questions.
