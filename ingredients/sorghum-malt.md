# Sorghum malt in West African grain-beer systems

## Why germinate sorghum?

Malting is a managed sprouting-and-drying process that contributes enzymes and other transformations required for converting cereal starch. A detailed review of African sorghum beer discusses region-specific red and white sorghum choices, processing and heat steps, rather than a standardized 'African beer grain.' Sorghum malt also has its own makers and market role.[^v13-dolo-review]

## What the grain does not determine

The review distinguishes examples of dolo from Burkina Faso and pito from Ghana, with different material choices and operations. Grain color, malt production, fermentation conditions and customary serving contexts can all vary. The same cereal does not erase the histories of the people who make the beverages.[^v13-dolo-review]
