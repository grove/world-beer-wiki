# Rye bread and malt in kvass

A rye loaf and a malt extract can both be starting points for a drink called *kvass*, but they are not identical ingredients. Bread has already undergone baking and carries a different set of aromas and soluble substrates from barley malt wort. A 2022 paper explicitly contrasts traditional dark-rye-bread kvass with an experimental barley-malt preparation designed using cultured microorganisms.[^v112-kvass-2022]

## A fermentation ingredient is a processed object

Grain identity alone does not determine its role: cooked maize porridge, malted millet, baked rye bread and koji-prepared rice all expose starch to different processing histories. Their soluble carbohydrates and available nutrients can influence which microbes grow and which metabolites accumulate. That makes comparisons by the word 'rye' or 'malt' too coarse to predict a beverage's finished character.[^v112-kvass-2022]

## Evidence note

The cited paper focused on optimizing and storing its own barley-malt kvass, not cataloguing historic bread recipes. The label 'probiotic' in a study title is a research frame, not proof that ordinary kvass is probiotic or a safe substitute for water. Further food-history, producer and archival sources should be added before giving regional proportions or invented first-use dates.[^v112-kvass-2022]
