# Jora: germinated maize as a brewing material

## A grain transformed before fermentation

*Jora* refers here to maize that has been germinated for chicha production. A 2020 review describes local Peruvian practices of germination, drying and cooking before a variable fermentation. That sequence differs from simply adding milled, unmalted maize to a modern lager mash: malting and other processing determine how starch becomes available.[^v13-chicha]

## Why a regional recipe cannot be universal

A 2026 review compares studies with different maize varieties, drying methods, vessels, backslopping, adjuncts and laboratory controls. It shows scientific interest in standardizing some operations, but that ambition should be separated from the historical and present-day diversity of family or community methods. The review does not justify claiming one definitive pre-Columbian chicha recipe.[^v13-2026-chicha-review]

Scientific reviews include modern controlled-process studies and health-related discussion; neither is adopted as evidence for clinical benefit or a universal heritage recipe. All dated observations are tied to the source and location described above. This is a research draft.
