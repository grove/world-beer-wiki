# Mahua flowers as a fermented-beverage material

Mahua flowers contain fermentable sugars and are used in several foods and drinks. A study indexed by FAO AGRIS describes how Santhal communities in a specified part of Odisha use the flowers, including to prepare the distilled beverage mahuli.[^v14-mahua]

## The botanical boundary matters

The study records the name *Madhuca latifolia* in its source context. Plant naming and local taxonomies deserve verification before substituting a modern synonym. This is flower-based fermentation with distillation in the documented drink, not an undocumented grain-beer variant.[^v14-mahua]
