# Bog myrtle (sweet gale) in herbal beer

## Identify the plant first

Bog myrtle, also called sweet gale and historically referred to as *Myrica gale*, appears in discussions of European herbal beer. The Oxford Companion to Beer describes it as an aromatic wetland shrub used in various regional gruit mixtures. A plant species and a historical herbal blend should not be treated as the same thing.[^v13-bog]

## What the addition does—and does not prove

A reported resinous, herbal or astringent association describes potential sensory contribution, not a fixed concentration or finished-beer flavor. Gruit itself could denote varying mixtures and institutional practices across times and places. A maker using bog myrtle today need not be reproducing a documented medieval formulation.[^v13-bog][^v13-gruit]
