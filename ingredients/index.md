## A useful reading order

Start with an ingredient's definition and processing form, then follow one modern style and one regional tradition where available. Compare what the cited evidence actually says about the ingredient's role. Keep unknown recipe details unknown; a tasting descriptor alone is not a recipe.

These are reference pages, not complete recipes, exhaustive ingredient inventories, legal ingredient declarations or dietary assessments. See [research method](../editorial/methodology.md) for source limits and [coverage and gaps](../editorial/coverage-and-gaps.md) for the wider scope.
