# 20 August 1991: ATF circular on non-alcoholic malt beverage marketing

## What is dated

On **20 August 1991**, the US Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco and Firearms issued Industry Circular 91-10 concerning marketing of non-alcoholic malt beverages. The archived document names its addressees and explains the agency's position under the Federal Alcohol Administration Act. In particular, it responds to a question about cash inducements to retailers that might exclude competing products; it is not the announcement of a new beer style. It is a primary administrative record with a known date, not an annual observance or an invitation to celebrate a beer event.[^v115-circular]

## Why it matters to the atlas calendar

The circular is valuable evidence of how a US regulatory agency spoke about a category at a particular moment. It does not show when the first non-alcoholic drink was made, how consumers received it, or how every product was labeled. The source is agency communication and its audience includes regulated businesses; independent trade and consumer accounts would be necessary for a social history.

Contemporary TTB guidance uses defined terms and particular label disclosures. An editor can compare the present public guidance against the circular without pretending every piece of 1991 language automatically remains enforceable or that later changes retroactively describe 1991 products.[^v115-alcohol]
