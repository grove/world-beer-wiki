# Beer in Ndau rain-petition ceremonies

In an ethnographic analysis of Ndau climate responses in Zimbabwe, interviewees described ceremonies in which brewed beer is offered to ancestral spirits in connection with petitions for rain. The account records local beliefs and reported practice—not a meteorological demonstration that offerings cause rainfall.[^v14-ndau]

## A ceremonial calendar is not a universal date

The study describes preparation, convening and leadership roles through named local accounts. Rainfall patterns and customary authority shape the circumstances; there is no reliable fixed Gregorian date for all Ndau communities in the cited evidence.[^v14-ndau]
