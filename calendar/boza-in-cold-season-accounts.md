# Boza in Istanbul cold-season vending accounts

The Istanbul artisans' association describes boza street sellers becoming particularly recognizable as **autumn cools toward winter**. Sellers' calls are part of its cultural account of the city. This is a reported seasonal practice, not evidence that everyone brewed on a specific date, that all boza shops operated only in winter, or that today's vendors can be found at a particular hour.[^v112-boza-artisans]

## Three different kinds of time

There is the **harvest/ingredient season**, the **producer's production schedule**, and the **consumer's remembered street season**. A narrative of a cold-weather seller belongs to the third. It should not be converted into a religious festival date or an annually repeated appointment. Historical service may also have changed with transport, refrigeration and retail distribution.[^v112-boza-artisans]

## A calendar that knows its limits

This entry intentionally has no numbered months, recurrence edition dates or booking claim. Later research could add dated archival advertisements, market records or interviews, but the present documentation only supports an approximate local seasonal association. Compare the similarly conditional harvest and farmhouse-brewing articles to see why a cultural calendar must be source specific.[^v112-boza-artisans]
