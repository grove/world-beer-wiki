# Bryggjardøgra: the pre-Christmas brewing days

## Why the days had names

Norsk Folkemuseum records *bryggjardøgra*, a period commonly described from **9 to 21 December** for making Christmas beer, and an associated auspicious date of 9 December (*Annadagen*). The museum links timing to the practical number of days involved in producing beer before Christmas. The words preserve a customary seasonal frame, not a regulation requiring every farmhouse to brew on those dates.[^v13-norwegian-christmas]

## A production window is not a tasting standard

The shared calendar helps explain why brewing was folded into other winter preparations, including special foods and household work. But date alone does not determine malt color, fermentation culture, strength or how a beer was served. Later industrial Christmas lagers and contemporary spiced beers belong to a changing commercial category.[^v13-norwegian-christmas]
