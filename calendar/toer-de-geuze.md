# Toer de Geuze and collaborative lambic culture

A festival can make cooperation tangible. HORAL, an association of Oude Geuze producers in the Pajottenland and Zenne Valley, describes **Toer de Geuze** as a biennial event held since 1997. Since 2009, the association says the event has also been accompanied by a collaborative **Megablend** using lambic from participating producers.[^atlas-horal]

## An event, an association and a blend

These are three distinct subjects. The association coordinates activity; the event brings public attention to a brewing tradition; the blend is a particular product of collaboration. The existence of one does not define the complete recipe or practice of every member.[^atlas-horal]

Follow [Lambic](../styles/lambic.md), [Gueuze](../styles/gueuze.md) and [Blending and bottle conditioning](../processes/blending-and-bottle-conditioning.md) for the production distinctions. A shared event is not evidence that all breweries ferment or blend identically.

## Calendar limits

“Biennial” is a recurrence description, not sufficient data for calculating a future opening date. This entry deliberately supplies no next-edition day, transport timetable, participation list or ticket information. Those require a dated organizer announcement.

For research, useful questions include how collaborations select component beers, how participants describe the result, and how public events transmit practical knowledge. Answers should be tied to a named edition, not generalized from an association's short overview.
