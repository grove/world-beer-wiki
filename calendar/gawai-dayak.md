# Gawai Dayak: an annually dated public festival

## A recurring public date

Tourism Malaysia describes Gawai Dayak as a public festival observed on **1 June** in Sarawak, following the rice harvest. Its own history says the first official celebration was in 1965. This date is useful for the modern public festival, but is not evidence that every traditional local observance started on exactly 1 June throughout history.[^v13-gawai-tourism]

## Preparation precedes the event

Rice tuak appears in the tourism authority's account of preparations, alongside food and visiting. When studying brewing seasons, distinguish the occasion when a beverage is served from the earlier work of preparing and fermenting it. Regional methods, participants and rules vary.[^v13-gawai-tourism]

## A calendar with context

For the social context see [Gawai and rice tuak](../culture/gawai-dayak-rice-tuak.md); for the beverage see [Tuak](../styles/tuak-rice.md); for an unrelated rice-based tradition compare [Makgeolli](../styles/makgeolli.md). Recurrence does not establish identical celebration programs from one year to the next.
