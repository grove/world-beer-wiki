# Paiwan Five-Year Ritual and millet-wine offerings

The Council of Indigenous Peoples describes a **Five-Year Ritual** of certain Vuculj Paiwan communities that includes public descriptions of millet-wine offerings and ancestral relations. A recurrence expressed as 'five-year' should not become an automatically computed meeting every five Gregorian calendar years, because participating communities and actual arrangements determine its timing.[^v112-paiwan-agency]

## Not the harvest thanksgiving festival

The same agency separately discusses the *Masalut* millet-harvest thanksgiving. Grain planting, household seed storage, annual agricultural events and the multi-year ceremonial cycle are distinct kinds of time. Bundling them into a single 'millet wine festival' would lose the community's own categories and might mistake symbolic offerings for routine consumption.[^v112-paiwan-agency]
