# Aludi and Georgian mountain summer gatherings

Georgia's tourism administration describes aludi being prepared in advance of highland gatherings and offered during summer festivities. Its account uses region-specific names for brewing preparations and describes a social context of blessing and communal participation. Such information shows **when beer becomes socially meaningful**, but gives no reliable date for all villages.[^v112-aludi-georgia]

## A local occasion is not an annual appointment

The published description does not establish a fixed modern public festival edition or a uniform seasonal brewing limit for every Georgian highland valley. Pastoral, ritual and settlement calendars can vary. Even where an event happens recurrently, the precise date, community practices and access conditions should be established from a source for that year before calendar presentation.[^v112-aludi-georgia]
