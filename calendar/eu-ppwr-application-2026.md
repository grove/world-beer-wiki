# 12 August 2026: EU packaging regulation begins applying

## An administrative date, not a drinking festival

On **12 August 2026**, Regulation (EU) 2025/40 on packaging and packaging waste began applying in the European Union. The European Commission published a dated announcement explaining how the new framework addresses material use, reuse, recycling and waste. This exact calendar date records the application of legislation, not an annual public beer event or a date when every manufacturer instantly achieved later targets.[^v118-ppwr-news26]

The original regulation includes numerous provisions, scheduled deadlines, coverage conditions and exceptions. Reusable beverage packaging, grouped packaging, transport containers and consumer sales packaging need not carry identical obligations. The effective application date therefore cannot be substituted for a detailed compliance test for a brewery, bar, bottle or keg.[^v118-ppwr25]

## Why a beer wiki stores a legal event

Packaging rules are part of the social and material environment in which beer is distributed. They concern decisions about containers, collection networks and supply-chain responsibility rather than styles, fermentation origins or the identity of a local beverage. A legal text's date is also different from the year when a producer adopts a specific technology.
