# Sahti occasions: weddings and funerals, not a fixed festival date

The producer history of Lammin Sahti describes a drink associated with **weddings and funerals**. These are social occasions whose dates belong to individual families and communities. There is no credible basis for publishing a nationwide “sahti day” or inventing recurring dates from this evidence.[^v16-sahti-maker]

## Occasion is not season

A public festival edition may have a fixed date, while a household celebration does not. The site's sahti association emphasizes present-day clubs and cultural transmission, also without creating a universal annual schedule. In the atlas calendar model, this case uses a recurring-occasion description **without fabricated months or editions**.[^v16-sahti-community]

## Reading across calendars

Compare [Christmas brewing in Hornindal](christmas-brewing-in-hornindal.md), which is a documented field observation at a particular season, with the [dated Oktoberfest edition](oktoberfest.md), which has organizer-supplied dates. The sahti account answers *why people prepare and share a drink*, not *which fixed week every Finnish brewer works*.

## Evidence boundary

A maker's account documents the connection to occasions; independent Finnish-language oral history would be needed to describe regional variation. This article does not supply a future event listing.
