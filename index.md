# World Beer Wiki

## Beer styles, living traditions, ingredients and brewing categories

**Research edition 1.2 · 27 October 2026 · 240 subject articles across ten reference layers**

An English-language, source-referenced Markdown knowledge base for readers and LLM retrieval. Start with a named beer, explore its ingredients, then compare the traditions and processes around it. Articles distinguish current judging descriptions, local practice, related ferments and historical evidence.

The [Atlas specification](editorial/atlas-specification.md) defines the scope and acceptance criteria. The [progress ledger](editorial/atlas-progress.md) separates implemented work from review and research still needed. Read [what changed in 1.2](editorial/research-update-1-2.md).
