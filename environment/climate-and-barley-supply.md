# Extreme weather and the barley-to-beer supply chain

Barley is an agricultural crop before it is a brewing ingredient. A Nature Plants modeling paper coupled crop yields with trade and economic models to explore what simultaneous heat and drought extremes could mean for barley availability and beer supply.[^v14-barley-climate]

## A scenario is not a forecast for your pint

The authors estimated global average barley-yield losses between 3% and 17% across the modeled severity scenarios. Their beer supply and price outcomes also depend on economic assumptions, competing grain uses and consumer markets; these figures are not observed losses in the year 2026.[^v14-barley-climate]

## The grain itself changes

A separate 2024 review highlights that climate, barley genotype and growing conditions affect starch properties and how malting and mashing behave. Ingredient availability and brewing performance are connected but not identical questions.[^v14-starch]
