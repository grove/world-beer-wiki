# Brewery wastewater-to-renewable-gas scenarios are models, not certified fuel deliveries

## An energy-rich waste stream can have multiple destinations

Liu and colleagues' 2025 analysis investigated brewery wastewater as feedstock for renewable natural gas via anaerobic digestion and additional methanation. Its four scenarios vary the source of hydrogen and resulting capital costs, minimum gas selling prices and lifecycle greenhouse-gas estimates. Those numbers are the results of assumed investments and process models, not bills from four commissioned facilities.[^v119-rng25]

A separate 2026 laboratory study followed an upflow bioelectrochemically assisted reactor treating **real brewery wastewater** for one year. It reported COD removal and methane-production changes within the reactor's conditions. A successful experimental endpoint does not establish the scale-up costs, effluent permits or climate benefits of the renewable-gas scenarios above.[^v119-bead26]

## Wastewater is not a homogeneous product

A 2026 review of brewery bioenergy routes describes substrate variability, microbial consortia, anaerobic digestion and other conversion pathways. Wastewater from different stages may have substantially different organic loading and cleaning-chemical characteristics. Biogas production, gas purification and final fuel use are therefore separate processes and evidence classes.[^v119-bioenergy26]

A defensible comparison names the wastewater volume and composition, methane yield, required imported energy, hydrogen source, captured versus released gases, functional unit and final emissions accounting. One scenario's potentially low emissions are not a general license to call every brewery gas carbon negative. See [anaerobic digestion](../processes/brewery-wastewater-anaerobic-digestion.md), [water and wastewater](brewery-water-and-wastewater.md) and [why treatment is not water certification](../stories/the-wastewater-does-not-become-drinking-water.md).
