# Climate change, hop harvests and alpha-acid content

Hop cultivation involves both quantity and chemistry. A 2023 Nature Communications study linked historical European hop records with climate projections and found observed shifts in ripening and alpha-acid concentration in several growing regions.[^v14-hops-climate]

## What changed

The study reports regional yield declines and changes in alpha content comparing periods before and after 1994. Its models suggest further losses under projected conditions, but cultivar, geography, farm adaptation and realized emissions trajectories affect the outcome.[^v14-hops-climate]

## Aroma is not a climate index

Alpha-acid content is relevant to potential brewing bitterness but does not measure all hop aroma compounds. [Hop alpha acids](../ingredients/hop-alpha-acids.md), [hop harvest timing](../calendar/hop-harvest-and-changing-ripening.md) and [Hops](../ingredients/hops.md) distinguish crop measurements, recipe chemistry and sensory perception.
