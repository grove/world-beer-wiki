# EU packaging rules from August 2026 are not immediate 2030 reuse results

## A law's application date is not the date of every later target

Regulation (EU) 2025/40 entered into force in February 2025 and began applying on **12 August 2026**, according to the European Commission's dated announcement. Its packaging framework covers waste prevention, recyclability, reuse and collection, with different obligations and target years. The fact that it started applying in August does not mean all 2030 packaging requirements were already achieved that day.[^v118-ppwr-news26]

The regulation's original legal text sets substantive obligations with scope limits, exclusions and schedules. The rules must be interpreted for the relevant operator, packaging function and product category, not by treating every bottle, can, crate and keg as the same kind of consumer packaging.[^v118-ppwr25]

## Kegs show why a category definition matters

The Commission's June 2026 guidance distinguishes large reusable kegs delivered business-to-business to hospitality venues from the sales packaging actually offered to consumers. For certain HORECA reuse targets, simply having beer arrive in a reusable keg does not automatically mean that a venue has offered consumers a reusable packaged product.[^v118-ppwr-guidance26]

A legal reuse target and a life-cycle environmental ranking answer separate questions. To assess a specific brewery, obtain current law, national implementation, exemptions, actual packaging sales and verified operations.
