# Brewery water use, cleaning and wastewater

Water enters the brewery in the beer itself and also in cleaning, cooling and other processes. A 2025 Greek case study measured water use and explored wastewater reuse options rather than merely invoking a generic “liters per liter” slogan.[^v14-water]

## What one site found

The study describes an industrial brewery with total water consumption of 5.6 hectoliters per hectoliter of beer and a treatment plant with greater energy use than anticipated. Those are observations for its named case, not targets applicable to farmhouse batches, small breweries and every factory worldwide.[^v14-water]

## Reuse requires boundaries

The researchers distinguish proposed reuse pathways, including restricted irrigation, from utility water needs. An attractive numerical reduction in freshwater demand does not by itself demonstrate that wastewater is fit for direct ingredient use. ## Edition 1.15: newly sourced connections

The Greek case study's on-site water-per-beer figure should not be conflated with a product water footprint. Water Footprint Network's public calculator draws a distinction between direct operations and indirect supply chains and distinguishes types of water use and pollution. A figure derived from barley agriculture can dominate one life-cycle assessment without contradicting a separate brewery-floor water ratio.[^v115-wfn]

The Brewers Association's public manual description treats brewery conservation and wastewater management as management domains, but does not disclose the member-only detailed practices or authorize a numeric universal efficiency benchmark. A scientifically comparable report must specify withdrawals, consumption, discharge, upstream coverage, region and reporting period.[^v115-baWater]

Read [water-use ratio versus footprint](brewery-water-ratio-versus-water-footprint.md) and [direct/indirect supply-chain water](direct-and-indirect-water-in-brewing.md). Water treatment, reuse eligibility and basin impacts require independent technical assessment.

## Edition 1.19: newly sourced connections

Water withdrawn by a brewery and organic material in its wastewater are different measurements. A 2026 brewery-waste review records diverse COD, BOD, nutrient, suspended-solid and pH values across reported streams, reflecting the presence of wort, yeast and cleaning residues.[^v119-wastewater26]

A separate 2026 365-day bioelectrochemical digestion experiment on real brewery wastewater measured reactor COD removal, methane generation and volatile fatty acid behavior. The outcome is specific to that process and sampled influent; it is not an assessment of agricultural water footprint or a certification that downstream water meets all discharge requirements.[^v119-bead26]

Record wastewater flow alongside concentration, treatment stage and analytical method, separately from water consumption, energy and lifecycle footprint.
