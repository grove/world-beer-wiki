# Earthen pots and covers in southern Assam rice-fermentation accounts

In its introductory accounts of rice-fermented drinks, a 2024 southern Assam study describes cooked rice mixed with starter and held in **earthen pots**, sometimes covered with leaves or sacks, before the liquid is separated. It describes practices across surveyed groups; specific materials and timing differ by beverage and reported setting.[^v16-assam]

## A vessel is part of the process

Porous and reusable surfaces, covering materials and household routines may affect fermentation conditions, but the study's bacterial sequence analysis does not independently prove what each pot or leaf contributed. A note about a vessel must not be converted into a mandatory method for a named community drink or a guarantee of microbial character.[^v16-assam]
