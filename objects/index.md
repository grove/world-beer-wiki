# Objects and ways of serving

Objects make brewing knowledge tangible. Their purpose, use and documented context matter more than a picturesque shape or an assumed age.

| [Czech foam pours](czech-foam-pours.md) | Three named presentations of one producer's beer, rather than three recipes. |
