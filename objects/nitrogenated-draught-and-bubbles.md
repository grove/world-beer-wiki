# Nitrogenated draught beer and bubble cascades

The downward-looking bubble movement seen in a settling glass of Guinness is not evidence that the bubbles violate buoyancy. Experiments published in Scientific Reports examined how fluid circulation and gravity-current instability create the visible texture.[^v14-bubbles]

## Flow and perception are separate

The researchers measured bubbles and observed inclined vessels to explain the motion. Their physical model concerns a particular nitrogenated product and geometry; it is not a tasting experiment or a universal rule about all stout.[^v14-bubbles]
