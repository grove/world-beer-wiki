# Beer-clean glassware: residue, foam and service

## A glass has a surface history

A vessel can be transparent and still carry a film that changes what a poured beer looks like. The Colorado Brewers Guild's public guide describes practical observations such as water sheeting, salt coverage and the behavior of bubbles and lacing. These are indicators of how a glass surface behaves during service, not laboratory assays for sterility.[^v115-glass]

The Brewers Association's draught quality resource treats glassware and pouring as part of the same route from keg to drinker as line cleanliness and gas balance. A glass is therefore a quality-relevant **object**, not simply an invisible container for judging a recipe.[^v115-ba]

## Distinguish defects from designed effects

Visible bubbles adhering to an interior surface may prompt a cleaning check under the guild's service guidance. But some glasses are intentionally made or marked to promote nucleation. The presence of a stream of bubbles cannot, on its own, tell the observer which mechanism is operating. Compare the separate [physical chemistry research](glass-surface-nucleation-sites.md).

Cleanliness, gas release, foam stability, glass geometry and the product's ingredients contribute differently to presentation. A credible wiki must not assign a contaminant or specific sanitizer cause to an observed pattern without a controlled inspection.

Read [foam and lacing](../sensory/foam-lacing-and-residues.md) together with [draught lines](draught-lines-and-gas-balance.md). This page deliberately avoids dosing instructions and does not certify a glass or venue as safe.

## Edition 1.17: newly sourced connections

Practical glass-cleaning checks can be useful, but foam alone cannot always identify the cause of an abnormal beer presentation. A 2024 study on barley Fusarium strain effects discusses fungal hydrophobins as one mechanism in primary gushing of packaged beer. It does not imply that a glass's adhering bubbles came from a disease-infected malt lot or that the glass is unclean.[^v117-fungal-strains]

The 2022 review on Fusarium-derived mycotoxins is about **chemical risk evidence**, not a visual service indicator. A clean-looking vessel and an apparently calm pour do not establish the absence of compounds in ingredients.[^v117-fusarium-review]
