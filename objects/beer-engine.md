# Beer engine: a hand pump and its social setting

## The apparatus

A beer engine is a mechanical pump that draws beer from a cellar cask to a dispense point. CAMRA describes the late-eighteenth-century development and Joseph Bramah's 1797 patent. The same account expressly cautions that a patent establishes a documented claim, not necessarily sole invention.[^v13-camra-dispense]

## Why it changed the pub

Instead of moving staff to a cask or positioning heavy casks at the bar, a pump and line allow beer to be stored below. Temperature, accessibility and the visible physical action of drawing beer became part of public-house culture. The technology does not identify which brewery supplied the beer.[^v13-camra-dispense]
