# Clarification, haze and colloidal stability

Beer can be clear, naturally cloudy or deliberately hazy. A haze review identifies biological particles and non-biological complexes such as proteins, polyphenols and polysaccharides as different causes of turbidity.[^v14-haze]

## Not one problem with one fix

Filtration and other stabilization methods can change appearance and shelf behavior, but not every haze signals contamination. The review separates microbiological instability from interactions among existing beer components that can occur as temperature and storage conditions change.[^v14-haze]

## Style and process are distinct

A [hazy IPA](../styles/hazy-ipa.md) may intentionally retain visual opacity, while a [German Pils](../styles/german-pils.md) description emphasizes clarity. Neither style name directly reveals measured microbial safety or every component responsible for cloudiness. See [haze science](../sensory/beer-haze-and-clarity.md).
