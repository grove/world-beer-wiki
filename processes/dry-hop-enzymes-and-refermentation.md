# Dry hopping, enzyme activity and hop creep

## Hops can alter fermentable material

The Brewers Association technical brief explains **hop creep**: enzymes carried with some dry-hop materials can modify residual carbohydrates, making additional sugars available to yeast. Under relevant conditions, this can restart fermentation even after the brewer thought primary fermentation was finished.[^v13-hopcreep]

## Why the outcome is conditional

Residual fermentable substrate, hop materials, contact, yeast viability, storage and temperature all influence whether the process becomes noticeable. Possible outcomes include further attenuation, changes in diacetyl, and excess package pressure. Dry hop aroma and hop creep are not identical processes; a strongly hoppy beer need not undergo measurable creep.[^v13-hopcreep]
