# Packaging, oxygen, light and beer aging

Packaged beer is not chemically frozen. A review of oxidative stability describes reactions involving oxygen and transition metals, but also changes that occur without additional oxygen.[^v14-aging]

## Three distinct pathways

Oxygen and some metals can favor reactions associated with stale flavors; light can activate hop-derivative photochemistry; live yeast in bottle-conditioned beer can continue to change the chemical environment. These are not synonyms for one generic “bad storage” effect.[^v14-aging][^v14-light][^v14-bottle]
