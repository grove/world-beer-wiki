# Wort boiling, precursors and dimethyl sulfide

Wort boiling affects both the chemical creation and removal of volatile compounds. Research on dimethyl sulfide (DMS) describes how malt-derived S-methylmethionine can break down during heating while DMS evaporates from the hot wort.[^v14-dms]

## Two competing directions

A longer or stronger heating stage can change precursor breakdown and volatile stripping simultaneously. The pilot-scale study evaluates this chemistry rather than claiming that one boil length works for every malt, altitude or brewhouse.[^v14-dms]

## Environmental and sensory tradeoffs

Boiling also consumes thermal energy. The [brewery energy](../environment/brewery-heat-and-refrigeration.md) and [cooked-cereal sensory](../sensory/dms-and-cooked-cereal.md) references explain why production efficiency and aroma judgments must be evaluated separately.[^v14-energy]
