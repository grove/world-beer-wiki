# Release history

## 2026-10-27 — Research edition 1.2

Added 44 subject articles across culture, calendars, microbiology, focused ingredients, processes, objects, sensory reasoning and stories. The corpus now has 240 subject articles, while retaining all 166 beer/tradition profiles. Twelve original articles gained labelled reading links.

Added a versioned [specification](editorial/atlas-specification.md), [progress ledger](editorial/atlas-progress.md), source supplement, evidence-qualified graph exports, calendar checks, regression tests and read-only validation artifacts. Preserved the searchable-site work and extended compatibility. Read [the research update](editorial/research-update-1-2.md) and [validation report](editorial/validation-report-1-2.md). Independent human/community review and licensed media remain unfinished.

## 2026-10-27 — Research edition 1.1

Expanded to 166 beer and tradition profiles, 17 ingredient references and 13 category guides, with 260 external subject-source records. Added thirteen profiles and improved twenty-two existing ones while preserving all original canonical paths.

Added ingredient navigation, rebuilt directories and source backlinks, expanded evidence and LLM reading guidance, and supplied an optional offline validator. Source limitations and draft status remain explicit. See the [research update](editorial/research-update-1-1.md), [coverage report](editorial/coverage-and-gaps.md) and [validation report](editorial/validation-report.md).

## 2026-10-27 — Research edition 1.0

Published research edition 1.0 with 153 canonical [profiles](styles/index.md), 161 cited profile-source records, regional and thematic navigation, aliases, an evidence policy, and reading instructions for LLMs.

Separated named beer styles from specialty categories, related grain and cassava ferments, historical terminology, and archaeological evidence. Recorded source access limits and retained draft review status.

The [validation report](editorial/validation-report.md) records the release checks. The [source register](references/source-register.md) provides the research references.

## Research edition 1.3 — 27 October 2026

Added 55 researched reference articles across cultural practice, regional calendars, microbial ecology, historical evidence, botanical ingredients, process explanations and sensory interpretation. Created the `history/` section; extended fifteen established articles; updated atlas specification and tracked work items ATLAS-015–023. Added source-access notes, critical history and qualified comparisons, a deterministic offline timeline export, regression tests and reproducibility checks. Articles remain drafts without community or specialist review; licensed media and a live calendar remain deferred. See [release research note](editorial/research-update-1-3.md).
