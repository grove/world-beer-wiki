# Release history

## 2026-10-27 — Research edition 1.1

Expanded to 166 beer and tradition profiles, 17 ingredient references and 13 category guides, with 260 external subject-source records. Added thirteen profiles and improved twenty-two existing ones while preserving all original canonical paths.

Added ingredient navigation, rebuilt directories and source backlinks, expanded evidence and LLM reading guidance, and supplied an optional offline validator. Source limitations and draft status remain explicit. See the [research update](editorial/research-update-1-1.md), [coverage report](editorial/coverage-and-gaps.md) and [validation report](editorial/validation-report.md).

## 2026-10-27 — Research edition 1.0

Published research edition 1.0 with 153 canonical [profiles](styles/index.md), 161 cited profile-source records, regional and thematic navigation, aliases, an evidence policy, and reading instructions for LLMs.

Separated named beer styles from specialty categories, related grain and cassava ferments, historical terminology, and archaeological evidence. Recorded source access limits and retained draft review status.

The [validation report](editorial/validation-report.md) records the release checks. The [source register](references/source-register.md) provides the research references.
