## Limits and corrections

Publisher abstracts, original field samples, broader scientific reviews and laboratory trials answer different questions. Some linked full texts were not independently inspected because the publisher endpoint blocked access; the article claim is therefore narrowed to what the indexed abstract or excerpts actually establish. No article has been independently reviewed by a living tradition bearer, brewing microbiologist or historian.

Avoid treating a lower coliform count in a small sample as proof that a drink or water is safe. Avoid ingesting or foraging named bark based on a fermentation experiment. Avoid giving exact practical fermentation schedules based on published assays. The [edition 1.8 review queue](../editorial/source-review-queue-1-8.md) identifies the next evidence needs.
