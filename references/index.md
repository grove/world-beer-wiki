# References

The [edition 1.1 source register](source-register.md) records the original **260 external subject-source records** and their access limits.

The [edition 1.2 atlas supplement](atlas-sources.md) traces **24 external records and 29 internal corpus dependencies** used by the **44 new subject pages**. Some external works were already present in 1.1; these counts must not be added together as independent studies. The supplement states what was actually accessed and which new articles rely on it.

Source records, independent publications and independent confirmations are different counts. Articles retain their own footnotes and metadata for standalone use.
