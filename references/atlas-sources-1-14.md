## Research and community access limits

UNESCO nominations are institutional cultural evidence rather than recipe or ownership certificates. Historic England summarizes material heritage rather than each farm worker's testimony. Producer chronicles and the indexed opening of a blocked brewery-history PDF require independent archive corroboration. Industrial and protein experiments retain their specific vessels, matrices and methods; environmental cases retain functional units. The BA manual is technical guidance, not sampled evidence of venue conditions. **No independent community or specialist review has been performed.**
