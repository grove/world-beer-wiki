# Source supplement 1.6 — access and traceability

This release emphasizes methods, studied sample frames and limits. The included works mix original laboratory reports, field/sampling studies, regional reviews, practitioner accounts and institutional project descriptions. ## Research quality, limitations and rights

- **Names and communities:** Southern Assam's five-community survey includes three samples per beverage; “Zao/Zou” spelling differs between its study text and a table. Do not silently harmonize source variation or turn sample findings into ethnic labels.
- **Traditional fermented fruit:** Tonto is sampled in two Ugandan districts; a secondary Rwandan description of Urugwa is not independent field verification. The Tonto study reports variable methanol results. This is neither a safety endorsement nor a finding about all customary banana ferments.
- **Microbiology:** Bacterial marker detection, isolated yeast sequencing and predicted functions are not equivalent to demonstrated enzyme activity or community endorsement. The 2026 kveik and European farmhouse studies have separate sampling frames.
- **Environment:** Mexican microbrewery and Japanese small-brewery studies define incompatible cradle boundaries; modeled results cannot support a universal product ranking.
- **Heritage:** Sahti historical milestones belong to the cited maker; UNESCO recognition of Belgian beer culture is not legal recognition of all other named beer styles.

The prose is new synthesis; external publications, locally held knowledge, images, archival testimony and audio retain their own copyrights and permission requirements. The [independent review queue](../editorial/source-review-queue-1-6.md) records high-priority uncertainties.
