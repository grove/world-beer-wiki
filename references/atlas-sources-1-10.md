## Evidence boundaries

Original ethnographic or archaeology study abstracts cannot stand in for full cultural consent, transcription, laboratory methods or local-language practitioner validation. Experimentally selected microbes cannot be presented as a safe traditional starter. A 40% alternative-grain / 60% barley-malt experiment must **not** be described as proof of gluten-free beer. A controlled pathogen challenge demonstrates a risk under study conditions, **not** contamination rates for commercial products. No independent expert or community review is claimed.
