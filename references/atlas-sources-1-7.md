## Editorial boundaries

- The Abydos estimate for one production cycle comes from excavation-team interpretation, not an ancient production ledger or a chemically reconstructed brew. Different structure counts belong to different field seasons.
- Piura’s rotating service days describe interviewed households during fieldwork, not today’s opening hours. An archaeological comparison cannot establish direct Chimu ancestry.
- The 2024 Japanese sake inscription is a declaration about knowledge and social practice; it does not make sake a standardized beer style.
- Bubod isolate counts are laboratory recoveries, not literal ingredients or universal starter composition. The 2025 tapuy lees paper is not a clinical health-benefit trial.
- HorA/HorC and strain adaptation are experimental contexts, not a validated diagnostic test for every beer.
- Two packaging life-cycle assessments can favor different choices because routes, return rates and boundaries differ; no universal environmental ranking follows.
- The 2026 Arequipa account analyzes archival court files through an author’s historical interpretation. Original archive files were not independently read for this edition.
